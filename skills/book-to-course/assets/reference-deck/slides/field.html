Internomics · Module 0 — Orientation

Here's your assignment for this week. It's deliberately simple. Observe. Don't try to change anything yet. Record three interactions in your journal. One at work. One at home. One with a stranger or a customer. For each one, note one thing that shaped the outcome that wasn't the words themselves. Maybe it was a person who wasn't in the room. Maybe it was an incentive. A deadline. A history between you. Next week we'll debrief with three questions. Which of the three interactions had the most invisible structure? Where did you catch yourself assuming you knew what someone wanted? And which of the eleven questions do you suspect you'll need most? In Module One we'll start building the first tool for seeing these things on purpose. It's called the Game Map.
