Internomics · Module 0 — Orientation

Your tool for this module is the Interaction Journal. It's in your workbook. You'll use it every week of this course. It's one page per interaction. Here's how it works. Start with the date and the setting. Then write down who was involved. That includes people who weren't in the room. Then what you wanted. Then what you think they wanted. Those first four prompts are your model of the game. The next two are the move and the response. What did you say or do? How did they respond? Then the two questions that make this a learning tool. Did their response surprise you? And if so, what might that surprise tell you? Finally, if you could replay it, what question would you ask yourself first? Notice that the journal follows the loop from the last lesson. Model. Act. Observe. Update. You're practicing it on paper before you practice it live. Why bother writing it down? Because memory is kind to us. After a conversation goes badly, we remember our words and forget our assumptions. The journal catches those assumptions while they're still fresh. Each module will add new questions to this page. By the end of the course it becomes a full strategic map of an interaction. Here's the common mistake. People try to fill it out for every small conversation and give up after three days. Don't do that. Pick the interactions that matter. A few good entries a week beat a dozen rushed ones.
