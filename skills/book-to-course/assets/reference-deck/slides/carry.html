The question to carry

What did I assume before I spoke?

Internomics · Next: Module 1 — See the Game

So here's the question to carry with you this week. After any conversation that matters, ask yourself one thing. What did I assume before I spoke? What did I assume about what they wanted? What did I assume about what they knew? What did I assume about how they'd respond? You don't have to fix anything yet. Just notice. That noticing is where strategic judgment begins. Once you can see the game, you have far more choices about how you play. I'll see you in Module One. That's where we learn to see the game.
