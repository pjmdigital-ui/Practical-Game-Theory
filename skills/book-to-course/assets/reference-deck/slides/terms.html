Internomics · Module 0 — Orientation

Here are the key terms from this module. You'll find all of them in your workbook. Internomics is the study of what happens between people. It's game theory applied to communication and human interaction. A game is any situation where outcomes depend on the choices of more than one person. A player is anyone whose choices affect the outcome. That includes people who aren't in the room. Strategic interaction is an interaction where each person's best choice depends on what others do. A mental model is a lens that tells you what to look at. It's not a law that predicts exactly what people will do. And strategic judgment is knowing which model matters and when it matters. It's knowing how much weight to give it. And it's knowing when to change your mind. If you can explain these six terms to a friend in plain language, you're ready for Module One.
