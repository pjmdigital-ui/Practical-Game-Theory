type (noun)

Something important about me that you don't know.

Skilled or inexperienced. Patient or desperate. Trustworthy or not. Willing to walk away or not. You can't observe it directly. You have to infer it. And I know you're trying to.

Internomics · Module 6 — Map Information & Beliefs

Here's one piece of vocabulary. Players can have private information about their own type. A type is a set of characteristics relevant to the game that other players don't observe. Something important about me that you don't know. Maybe I'm highly skilled or inexperienced. Maybe I'm desperate or willing to walk away. You have to infer it from what I say and do. Here's the misunderstanding to avoid. Type isn't a label you stick on someone. It's your best current guess about something hidden. Let's watch types at work in the job interview.
