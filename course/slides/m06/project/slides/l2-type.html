type (noun)

Something important about me that you don't know.

Skilled or inexperienced. Patient or desperate. Trustworthy or not. Willing to walk away or not. You can't observe it directly, so you have to infer it—and I know you're trying to.

Internomics · Module 6 — Map Information & Beliefs

Here's one piece of formal vocabulary worth keeping. In game theory, players can have private information about their "type": characteristics relevant to the game that other players don't observe. Incomplete information is uncertainty about other players' payoffs or types. You don't need any of the mathematics. Just think of type as something important about me that you don't know. Maybe I'm highly skilled, or inexperienced. Wealthy, or desperate. Patient, or impatient. Risk-averse. Willing to walk away. You can't see these things directly. You have to infer them. And I know you're trying to infer them. Now we have a game.
