Example · Two departments

A misunderstanding can become self-fulfilling

The situation

Department A believes, "Department B will never cooperate with us." So A stops sharing information.

What happened

B sees this and thinks, "They don't trust us." So B stops sharing too. A says, "See? We were right."

The lesson

Neither side started with malice. The belief changed behavior. The behavior reinforced the belief.

Internomics · Module 6 — Map Information & Beliefs

Here's how this plays out inside a company. Department A believes Department B will never cooperate. So A stops sharing information. B sees this and concludes A doesn't trust them. So B stops sharing too. Then A looks at B and says, "See? We were right." The belief has become self-reinforcing. It's a closed loop. Neither side necessarily started with malicious intent. The belief altered behavior and the behavior reinforced the belief. When someone's behavior confirms your belief about them, ask whether your own behavior helped produce it.
