Shared information isn't common knowledge

Everyone hears it

Only half hear it

Common knowledge: we both know it. And we both know that we both know it.

Internomics · Module 6 — Map Information & Beliefs

Suppose we both know the meeting starts at nine. But do I know that you know it? Game theory calls this recursively shared awareness common knowledge. We both know something and we both know that we both know it. Look at the left card. A room of ten hears that everyone leaves at noon. Everyone sees that everyone heard it. Coordination is easy. Now look at the right card. Only half the people hear it. They don't know whether the others heard. Now coordination is hard. The problem isn't lack of information. It's lack of shared knowledge that the information is shared. Shared expectations can help.
