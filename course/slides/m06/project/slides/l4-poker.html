Internomics · Module 6 — Map Information & Beliefs

Poker makes all of this intuitive. You can't know your opponent's cards. So you ask different questions. What do they think I have? What do they think I think they have? And what do they think I think they're going to do? We're predicting what they'll do based on what they believe. This is why bluffing is possible. Convince someone you have a strong hand and you've changed their beliefs. Their behavior changes. A bluff doesn't work because it's true. It works because the other player believes it. But beliefs can be wrong.
