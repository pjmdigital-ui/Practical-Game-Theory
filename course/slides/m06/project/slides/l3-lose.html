Internomics · Module 6 — Map Information & Beliefs

Here's the practical tool. It's one question. What does this person have to lose by making this claim? If the answer is nothing, be more cautious about treating the statement as strong evidence. A coworker says, "Trust me. This vendor is the best option." If only your project suffers when the vendor disappoints, the claim is fairly cheap. If their name goes on the recommendation, the same sentence gets heavier. This isn't cynical. You're just asking how much weight their words can bear. Now let's look at words that cost nothing to say.
