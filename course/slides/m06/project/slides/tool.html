Internomics · Module 6 — Map Information & Beliefs

Your tool for this module is the Information Map. Use it before any important interaction, or after one that confused you. It starts with seven questions. What do I know? Separate facts from assumptions. What don't I know? What do they know that I don't? What do I know that they don't? What do I think they believe? What do they think I believe? If the stakes are high, that matters too. And what evidence could change my mind? That last one protects you from getting trapped in your own assumptions. Then the workbook takes you further. Sort your answers into facts, unknowns, beliefs and assumptions about them, and label your confidence in each belief: I know, I have good evidence, I suspect, or I'm guessing. Check whether key information is truly common knowledge. Ask of every message you're relying on: why should I believe it? And finally, name the single piece of information that would change your decision, or theirs, and how you'll get it.
