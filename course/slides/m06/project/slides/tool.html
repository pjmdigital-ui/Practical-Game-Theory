Internomics · Module 6 — Map Information & Beliefs

Your tool for this module is the Information Map. Use it before or after a hard interaction. It starts with seven questions. What do I know? Separate facts from assumptions. What don't I know? What do they know that I don't? What do I know that they don't? What do I think they believe? What do they think I believe? And what evidence could change my mind? If you can't name any, you're holding a conviction and not a hypothesis. Just write a line for each. The workbook takes the map further. Let's look at the rest.
