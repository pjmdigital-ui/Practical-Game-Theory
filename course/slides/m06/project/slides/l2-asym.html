Internomics · Module 6 — Map Information & Beliefs

Here's the formal name. Information asymmetry means one player knows something relevant that another doesn't. Once you look for it you see it everywhere. A doctor knows more about medicine than the patient. A mechanic knows more about cars than the customer. A child knows a lot more about what they did five minutes ago than their parent does. None of these is a battle. But in every one somebody decides with less information than the other person has. So how do you decide? The obvious answer is to ask. Let's see why that's harder than it sounds.
