Internomics · Module 6 — Map Information & Beliefs

Remember Schelling's meeting example from Module Five? We agree to meet tomorrow but don't say where. You choose Grand Central. I choose Times Square. Now look at the right card. Suppose one location stands out. It may become a focal point. We each think the other will probably choose Grand Central. Because we both think that, we both choose it. A shared expectation solved the problem. That's why rituals and conventions and public announcements matter strategically. They create shared awareness about what people expect others to do.
