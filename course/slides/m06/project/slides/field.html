Internomics · Module 6 — Map Information & Beliefs

Here's your assignment for this week. Catch yourself in one moment of confusion about someone's behavior. An unanswered message. A cool response in a meeting. An unexpected "no." Before you react, take two minutes and run the Information Map, at least Part One and the confidence labels. Then take one honest step to get decision-relevant information. Ask a direct question. Share something you know that they might not. Or verify a claim you were relying on. In your journal, record the situation, your first interpretation, your confidence label before, what you learned, and whether your belief survived, changed, or collapsed. Next week, I'll ask you how far apart your first read and the truth turned out to be.
