Example · The bank run

A belief can help create the outcome it fears

The situation

You have money in a bank. You believe it may fail. You don't know whether that's true.

What happened

You know enough withdrawals could sink it. So you withdraw. Others see you and get nervous. They withdraw too.

The lesson

The outcome isn't set only by the bank's condition. It's shaped by what depositors believe other depositors will do.

Internomics · Module 6 — Map Information & Beliefs

Here's the classic example. You have money in a bank. You believe it may fail but you don't know whether that's true. You also know that if enough depositors withdraw, the bank could actually fail. So you withdraw. Others see you and get nervous. They withdraw too. Suddenly a belief helps create the very outcome people feared. Here's the lesson on the right.
