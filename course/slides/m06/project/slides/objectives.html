Internomics · Module 6 — Map Information & Beliefs

Here's what you'll be able to do by the end of this module. You'll be able to separate four things that usually get blurred together: what's actually true, what each player knows, what each player believes, and what you're simply assuming. You'll be able to spot information asymmetries, where one side knows something the other doesn't. You'll be able to evaluate a statement or a signal by asking what the speaker gains, what it costs them, and why you should believe it. You'll recognize beliefs about beliefs, and the difference between information that's shared and information that's common knowledge. And you'll learn to treat your interpretations of other people as hypotheses, and to name the evidence that would change your mind.
