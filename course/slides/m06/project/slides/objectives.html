Internomics · Module 6 — Map Information & Beliefs

Here's what you'll be able to do by the end of this module. First you'll separate four things that usually get blurred together. There's what's actually true. There's what each player knows. There's what each player believes. And there's what you're simply assuming. Second you'll spot information asymmetries. Those are the places where one side knows something the other doesn't. Third you'll test a statement or a signal. You'll ask what the speaker gains and what it costs them. And you'll ask why you should believe it. Fourth you'll recognize beliefs about beliefs. You'll also see the difference between information that's shared and information that's common knowledge. And fifth you'll learn to treat your reads of other people as hypotheses. You'll name the evidence that would change your mind. Let's begin with the most basic distinction of all.
