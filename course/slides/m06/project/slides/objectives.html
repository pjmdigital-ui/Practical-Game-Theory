Internomics · Module 6 — Map Information & Beliefs

Here's what you'll be able to do by the end of this module. First you'll separate what's true from what each player knows and believes and what you're assuming. Second you'll spot information asymmetries. Third you'll test a statement or a signal. Fourth you'll recognize beliefs about beliefs and common knowledge. And fifth you'll treat your reads of people as hypotheses. Let's begin.
