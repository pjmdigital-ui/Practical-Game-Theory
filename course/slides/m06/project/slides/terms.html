Internomics · Module 6 — Map Information & Beliefs

Here are the key terms from this module, and you'll find all of them in your workbook. The information structure is simply who knows what in an interaction. Change it and you can change the game. Information asymmetry: one player knows something relevant that another doesn't. Incomplete information: uncertainty about the other players' payoffs or types. A type is something important about a player that others don't directly observe and have to infer: skill, patience, desperation, trustworthiness, willingness to walk away. And a signal is an action or piece of information that helps another player infer something they can't directly observe. Remember: evidence, not proof.
