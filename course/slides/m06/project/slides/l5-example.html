Internomics · Module 6 — Map Information & Beliefs

Here's one almost everyone has lived. You text someone, "Can we talk?" They don't respond. What do you know? Very little. But your mind immediately starts generating explanations. Maybe they're angry. Maybe they're busy. Maybe they didn't see it. Maybe they're avoiding you. Maybe something happened. You have incomplete information, and because you don't know, you start forming beliefs. Then those beliefs drive your next action. Maybe you send another text. Maybe you withdraw. Maybe you get angry. And now your belief about their behavior is influencing their future behavior. That's why uncertainty can create conflict even when neither person intends it. Nobody did anything wrong yet. The gap in information did the damage.
