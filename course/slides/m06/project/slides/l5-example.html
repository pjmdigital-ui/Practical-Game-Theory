Example · The unanswered text

"Can we talk?" No reply.

The situation

You text someone, "Can we talk?" They don't respond. You actually know very little.

What happened

Your mind fills the gap. Angry? Busy? Avoiding you? The belief drives your next move. Another text. Withdrawal. Anger.

The lesson

Your belief about their behavior now shapes their future behavior. Uncertainty can create conflict nobody intended.

Internomics · Module 6 — Map Information & Beliefs

Here's one almost everyone has lived. You text someone, "Can we talk?" They don't respond. That's the situation. Now stop and ask the honest question. What do you actually know? Very little. You know you sent a message. You know there's no reply yet. That's it. But look at the middle card. Your mind immediately starts generating explanations. Maybe they're angry. Maybe they're busy. Maybe they didn't see it. Maybe they're avoiding you. Maybe something happened. You have incomplete information. And because you don't know, you begin forming beliefs. Then those beliefs influence your next action. Maybe you send another text. Maybe you withdraw. Maybe you become angry. And here's the lesson on the right. Now your belief about their behavior is influencing their future behavior. Say they were simply in a meeting. They pick up their phone and find three increasingly tense messages. Now they're the ones forming beliefs about you. And the conversation you wanted to have has gotten harder before it even started. That's why uncertainty can create conflict even when neither person intends it. Nobody did anything wrong at the start. The gap in information did the damage. So what would the habit from the last slide look like here? You'd notice your first read. Maybe it's that they're angry. You'd label it a hypothesis. You'd ask what else could explain the silence. And you'd wait for real evidence before you make your next move. Here's a question for you. What's your default story when someone doesn't reply? Knowing your default is half the battle. Let's close out this lesson.
