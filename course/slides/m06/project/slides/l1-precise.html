Say it precisely

Not

"Perception is reality."

But

"Perception is part of the causal structure of strategic interaction."

Internomics · Module 6 — Map Information & Beliefs

This deserves careful wording. A rumor can change behavior. A misunderstanding can change behavior. A false belief, a prediction, a credible warning: all of them can change behavior. The strategic effect comes from what people believe and how those beliefs affect their choices. But that does not mean truth doesn't matter. Truth determines whether a belief is accurate. And inaccurate beliefs can eventually collapse when reality produces contradictory evidence. So we're not saying "perception is reality." That's too sloppy, and it lets you off the hook for finding out what's actually true. We're saying something more precise: perception is part of the causal structure of strategic interaction. Beliefs are one of the things that make outcomes happen.
