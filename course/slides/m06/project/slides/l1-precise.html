Internomics · Module 6 — Map Information & Beliefs

You've probably heard the phrase "perception is reality." I don't use it. Beliefs do drive behavior. But truth still matters. Truth determines whether a belief is accurate. And inaccurate beliefs can collapse when reality produces contradictory evidence. If the quarters keep coming in strong and nobody gets laid off, the layoff fear starts to crack. So here's the precise claim. Perception is part of the causal structure of strategic interaction. Beliefs sit alongside the facts as causes.
