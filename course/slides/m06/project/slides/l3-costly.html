Why it works: different types of players face different costs or benefits from sending a costly signal.

Internomics · Module 6 — Map Information & Beliefs

Suppose I tell you I'm extremely confident. That costs me almost nothing to say. Now suppose I put ten thousand dollars of my own money behind my prediction. My action has become costly. Different types face different costs from sending a signal. Someone confident and someone bluffing can both say the words for free. They can't both comfortably risk the same money. A friend who says they'll help you move is offering words. A friend who shows up with a truck has paid a cost. That leads to a question you can use every day.
