Four more mistakes

Face value or dismissal

Words aren't automatically facts. Cheap talk isn't automatically worthless. Ask why you should believe it.

Uncertainty means deception

They may not know themselves. The information may not exist yet. Or you may not have asked.

"Perception is reality"

Beliefs drive behavior. But truth still decides whether beliefs are accurate.

Chasing more information

You need the right information. Ask what would actually change your decision. Go after that.

Internomics · Module 6 — Map Information & Beliefs

The fifth mistake is taking statements at face value or dismissing them entirely. Evaluate the speaker and the incentives. Then ask why you should believe it. The sixth is assuming uncertainty means deception. They may not know themselves. Or you may not have asked. The seventh is saying "perception is reality." Beliefs drive behavior. But truth still decides whether beliefs are accurate. The eighth is gathering more information instead of the right information. Most of it won't change anything. So ask what would actually change your decision. Now here's your assignment.
