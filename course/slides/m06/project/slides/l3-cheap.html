Cheap talk

Cheap talk isn't useless. It can carry real information.

What it is

Communication that doesn't directly change the material payoffs of the game.

When it informs

Especially when the speaker's interests are aligned with the listener's.

The key question

Why should I believe you?

Internomics · Module 6 — Map Information & Beliefs

Game theory calls this cheap talk. It's communication that doesn't directly change the material payoffs of the game. Cheap doesn't mean worthless. Messages can carry meaningful information even when sending them is costless. That's especially true when the sender's interests are aligned with the receiver's. A coworker on your own team says the client meeting moved to three o'clock. You'd be foolish to ignore it. If our interests pull apart, my free words carry less weight. So ask the question in the dark card. Why should I believe you? Let's make this concrete with a relationship.
