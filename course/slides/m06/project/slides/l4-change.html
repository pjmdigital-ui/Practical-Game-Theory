Internomics · Module 6 — Map Information & Beliefs

This is where communication enters the game. Suppose you're in Department B. The other side wrongly believes you won't cooperate. The obvious move is to tell them. But the statement is cheap. A team that really wasn't willing could say the same words. So you may need to change their information. Make a commitment or provide evidence. Involve a trusted third party or change your behavior. Offer a verifiable concession. Or take an action that would be costly if you weren't sincere. None asks them to take your word for it. Let's land this lesson.
