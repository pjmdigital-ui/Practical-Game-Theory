Internomics · Module 6 — Map Information & Beliefs

Let's bring this home. Suppose someone tells you, "I really value our relationship." What does it cost them to say it? Almost nothing. Now imagine they repeatedly demonstrate that value: making sacrifices, keeping commitments, showing up when it's difficult, behaving consistently over time. Now you have something much more informative. Or imagine two salespeople who both tell you, "This is our best price." Salesperson A has a reputation for being completely transparent. Salesperson B has a reputation for exaggerated claims. Same words. Different information. Why? Because you aren't evaluating the words in isolation. You're evaluating the speaker, the incentives, the history, the context, the alternatives, and the consequences. That's why communication can never be separated from the game surrounding it.
