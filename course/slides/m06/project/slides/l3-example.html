Example · "I really value our relationship"

Words are cheap. Patterns tell you more.

The situation

Someone tells you, "I really value our relationship." It costs them almost nothing to say.

What happened

Then they make sacrifices. They keep commitments. They show up when it's hard. They stay consistent over time.

The lesson

The repeated behavior is far more informative. The words still matter. Their weight depends on the speaker's incentives.

Internomics · Module 6 — Map Information & Beliefs

Suppose someone tells you, "I really value our relationship." What does it cost them to say it? Almost nothing. Now look at the middle card. Imagine they make sacrifices and keep commitments. They show up when it's difficult. Each of those actions costs something. Someone who didn't really value the relationship would find those costs much harder to keep paying. The pattern of costly behavior tells you far more than the sentence. Words still matter. They just can't carry the whole load. Now let's see what happens when two people say exactly the same words.
