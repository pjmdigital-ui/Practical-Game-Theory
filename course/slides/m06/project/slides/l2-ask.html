The problem with "just ask"

The other person gets to decide what to tell you.

And they may have an incentive to give you information that benefits them.

You ask: "Has this car ever had any major problems?"

The seller says: "No."

You've received information. Should you automatically believe it?

Internomics · Module 6 — Map Information & Beliefs

The obvious advice is to just ask. But the other person gets to decide what to tell you. And they may have an incentive to give you information that benefits them. Back to the used car. You ask whether it has ever had any major problems. The seller says no. If the car is fine he says no. If it has a hidden problem he might also say no.
