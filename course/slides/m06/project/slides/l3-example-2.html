Internomics · Module 6 — Map Information & Beliefs

Imagine two salespeople. Both tell you, "This is our best price." Salesperson A has a reputation for being completely transparent. Salesperson B has a reputation for making exaggerated claims. Same words. Different information. When A says it, it's probably true. When B says it, it's probably the opening line. You never weigh words alone. You weigh the speaker and the incentives. You weigh the history and the context. And you have a reputation too. If your history says you mean it, your cheap talk becomes valuable. Let's land this lesson.
