Your tool · Parts 2–5 · In the workbook

Sort it. Label it. Verify it.

Part 2 · Sort it

Facts. Unknowns. Beliefs. Assumptions about them. Label each belief: I know / good evidence / I suspect / I'm guessing.

Part 3 · Shared or common?

Is the key information really in their information set? Do we both know that we both know it?

Part 4 · Messages I rely on

What do they gain if I believe it? How could I verify it? Why should I believe it?

Part 5 · Decision-relevant

What single piece of information would change my decision or theirs? How can I get it?

Internomics · Module 6 — Map Information & Beliefs

Here's the rest of the Information Map. Part two is sort it. Put everything into facts and unknowns and beliefs and assumptions. Then label your confidence from "I know" down to "I'm guessing." You'll often find an "I know" that really sits at "I suspect." Part three checks shared versus common knowledge. Part four checks messages you rely on. What do they gain if you believe it? How could you verify it? Part five asks what single piece of information would change a decision. The common mistake is gathering more information instead of the right information. Now let's practice.
