Internomics · Module 6 — Map Information & Beliefs

Let's slow the bank run down. It starts with a belief. My bank may fail. That belief leads to an action. You withdraw. Others see it and get nervous. And they follow. Belief drives action. Action becomes information. Information shapes the next belief. Here's the principle. What people believe others will do can affect what they themselves do. Inside a company it looks like this. Employees who expect layoffs start job hunting. They stop taking risks. Nobody had to lie. The spiral ran on beliefs. Here's the line to remember from this lesson.
