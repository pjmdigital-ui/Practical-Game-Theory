Internomics · Module 6 — Map Information & Beliefs

Here's the habit. Treat your first interpretation as a hypothesis and not a fact. Say you think he's angry with you. Ask three questions. What evidence would support it? Maybe he's been short in two meetings. What evidence would contradict it? Maybe he thanked you for your input yesterday. What else could explain it? Maybe he's under pressure from his own boss. Then you update as new information appears. Humans are remarkably good at forming an interpretation and then looking for confirming evidence. It's our loop from Module Zero. Model. Act. Observe. Update. Adapt. Let's try it on a familiar moment.
