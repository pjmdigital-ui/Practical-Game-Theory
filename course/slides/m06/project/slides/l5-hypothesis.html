Internomics · Module 6 — Map Information & Beliefs

So here's the habit. Don't treat your initial interpretation as a fact. Treat it as a hypothesis. You think, "He's angry with me." Fine. That's a hypothesis. Now ask three questions. What evidence would support that? What evidence would contradict it? And what else could explain the behavior? Then, as new information appears, update. This sounds obvious. But human beings are remarkably good at forming an interpretation and then looking for evidence that confirms it. Strategic thinking asks for something different. It's the same loop we've used since Module Zero: model, act, observe, update, adapt. And it matters especially here, because incomplete information is unavoidable. You will never know everything.
