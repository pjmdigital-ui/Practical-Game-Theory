Internomics · Module 6 — Map Information & Beliefs

When you don't know what someone is thinking, it's tempting to assume they're hiding something. But look at the five cards. Sometimes they are deliberately withholding. Sometimes they don't know themselves. Sometimes the information isn't available. Sometimes you just haven't asked. And sometimes you've interpreted ambiguous evidence incorrectly. Only the first involves anyone hiding anything. So strategic thinking asks a healthier question. What information do I actually have? And how confident should I be in my conclusions? If you jump to deception, you get guarded. And people feel it. So treat your read as a hypothesis.
