Internomics · Module 6

Map Information & Beliefs

What do they know—or believe—that I don't?

Paul Mascetta

Welcome to Module Six. So far we've learned to see the game and to see the payoffs. We've learned to think in responses. We've noticed the patterns people learn about us. And we've seen the equilibrium those patterns can create. Now we go one level deeper. We go to the beliefs underneath the behavior. What does everyone actually know? And just as important, what does everyone believe? Two people can stand in exactly the same situation and still play very different games. The question that runs through this whole module is simple. What do they know or believe that I don't?
