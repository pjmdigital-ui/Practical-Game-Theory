Internomics · Module 6 — Map Information & Beliefs

A few more. A costly signal is one that would cost the sender something. It's often more informative because different types face different costs for sending it. Cheap talk is communication that doesn't directly change the material payoffs. It can still be informative. That's especially true when interests are aligned. Higher-order beliefs are beliefs about what others believe. That includes what they believe about your beliefs. They're also called belief hierarchies. Common knowledge means we both know something. And we both know that we both know it. That's especially important for coordination. And a self-fulfilling belief is one that changes behavior in a way that produces the evidence that seems to confirm it. Think of our two departments.
