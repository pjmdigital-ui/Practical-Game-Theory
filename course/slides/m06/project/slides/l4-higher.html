Higher-order beliefs

Sometimes you respond to what you think they know about what you know.

One level: I believe you think I'm bluffing.

Two levels: You believe I think you believe I'm bluffing.

Three levels: I believe you believe I think you're bluffing.

Internomics · Module 6 — Map Information & Beliefs

Now we get into something surprisingly deep. You may have beliefs about what the other person believes about you. I believe you think I'm bluffing. That's already a belief about a belief. And it can continue indefinitely. Game theorists call these higher-order beliefs or belief hierarchies. Sometimes you're not responding to what someone knows. You're responding to what you think they know about what you know. People think this is overthinking. Sometimes it is. But in high-stakes moments it's often exactly what's driving the behavior. The clearest place to see it is a poker table.
