The late report

What happened: Jordan's quarterly report went out two days late. Dana's director raised it in front of other managers.

Earlier: Dana said "the director is watching this one" in a hallway chat Jordan missed. She skimmed his "Still waiting on finance's numbers" as routine.

Friday: Dana writes to Jordan. "We need to talk Monday about ownership."

Jordan's view: He rebuilt the model late Thursday after finance delivered. He thinks she just wants someone to blame.

Dana's view: Jordan didn't flag the risk because he doesn't take the director's priorities seriously.
