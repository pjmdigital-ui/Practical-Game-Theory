Internomics · Module 6 — Map Information & Beliefs

Let's practice. Dana manages a five-person analytics team. On Friday, the quarterly report Jordan owned went out two days late, and Dana's director asked her about it in front of other managers. On Monday, Dana had told the team "the director is watching this one closely," but she said it in a quick hallway exchange Jordan wasn't part of. Mid-week, Jordan sent a message: "Still waiting on finance's numbers." Dana skimmed it on her phone and read it as a routine status update. Friday afternoon she writes to Jordan: "We need to talk Monday about ownership." Jordan stayed late Thursday rebuilding the model after finance finally delivered. He reads her message as a sign she thinks he's careless, and tells a friend, "She already knows finance was late. She just wants someone to blame." Dana, meanwhile, believes Jordan didn't flag the risk because he doesn't take the director's priorities seriously. Pause the video and work through these five questions.
