Internomics · Module 6 — Map Information & Beliefs

Let's start with a simple idea. Information has value. Think back to the negotiation from Module Two. If they believe this is Paul's only option, they might push harder. If they believe Paul has three other offers, they may behave very differently. Same Paul. Same deal. Only their belief about my alternatives changed. That's why people ask questions and gather intelligence. It's why they sometimes stay silent or reveal information selectively. They're all trying to improve their model of the game.
