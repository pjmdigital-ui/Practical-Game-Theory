The question to carry

What do they know—or believe—that I don't?

Internomics · Next: Module 7 — Separate Words From Credibility

So here's the question to carry. The next time someone's behavior confuses you, don't immediately ask, "Why are they doing this?" Ask, "What do they know, or believe, that I don't?" Then ask: what do I actually know? What am I assuming? What might they be assuming? What information would change either of our decisions? And when someone tells you something important, don't stop at "What did they say?" Ask, "Why should I believe it?" That question is where our next model begins.
