The question to carry

What do they know—or believe—that I don't?

Internomics · Next: Module 7 — Separate Words From Credibility

So here's the question to carry. The next time someone's behavior confuses you, don't immediately ask why they're doing this. Ask what they know or believe that you don't. Then ask what you actually know. Ask what you're assuming. Ask what they might be assuming. And ask what information would change either of your decisions. When someone tells you something important, don't stop at what they said. Ask why you should believe it. Information changes the game. Beliefs change behavior. And that question about belief is where our next model begins. I'll see you in Module Seven. It's called Separate Words From Credibility.
