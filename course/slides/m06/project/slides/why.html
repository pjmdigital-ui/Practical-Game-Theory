Internomics · Module 6 — Map Information & Beliefs

Imagine you're negotiating to buy a used car. The seller knows it has a serious mechanical problem. You don't. You think you're negotiating over what the car is worth. The seller knows you're negotiating over how much you'll pay before you discover the problem. Same car. Different information. A very different game. Sometimes what matters most isn't what's true. It's what each person believes is true. So you need an honest map. Who knows what? And what are you merely assuming? Let's look at what you'll be able to do by the end of this module.
