Internomics · Module 6 — Map Information & Beliefs

This distinction is extremely important. There is what is actually true. And there is what each player believes is true. Those aren't necessarily the same thing. Imagine a company that's actually doing very well. Management knows it. But the employees believe layoffs are coming. What do they do? Their behavior responds to their belief about the future, not to the objective reality management can see. And it goes further than that. What you believe other people are going to do can change what you do. That's where beliefs stop being private thoughts and start shaping outcomes, sometimes helping to create the very thing people were afraid of. We'll see exactly how in a moment.
