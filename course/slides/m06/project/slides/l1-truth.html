Two different things

What is true and what people believe aren't the same thing.

The reality

The company is actually doing very well. Management knows it.

The belief

Employees believe layoffs are coming.

The behavior

It follows the belief about the future. Not the reality management can see.

Why it matters: to predict what someone will do, start with what they believe.

Internomics · Module 6 — Map Information & Beliefs

There is what is actually true. And there is what each player believes is true. People react to their picture of it. Imagine a company that's actually doing very well. But the employees believe layoffs are coming. So they start looking for other jobs and stop taking risks. That behavior follows their belief and not the reality management can see. So when someone's behavior doesn't make sense, start by asking what they believe. And here's the twist. A belief doesn't even have to be true to change what people do.
