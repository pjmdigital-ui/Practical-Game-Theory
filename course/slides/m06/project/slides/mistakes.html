Common mistakes

Promoting a hypothesis to a fact

Label it. Name what would contradict it. Then update as information arrives.

Assuming "they know"

No one can respond to information they never had. What evidence says it's in their information set?

Treating a signal as proof

A degree or a confident tone is evidence. Weigh how informative it is and what it cost to send.

Shared isn't common

Telling half the room isn't everyone knowing. Make key information public and mutual.

Internomics · Module 6 — Map Information & Beliefs

Here are four mistakes to watch for. The first is promoting a hypothesis to a fact. Label it a hypothesis and update as information arrives. The second is assuming "they know." A person cannot respond to information they never had. The third is treating a signal as proof. A confident tone is evidence. But it costs nothing. Don't overweight it.
