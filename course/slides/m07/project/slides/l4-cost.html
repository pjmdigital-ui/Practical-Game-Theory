Advertising

Image

Offices

Gestures

Better question: why would this signal be easier or more worthwhile for someone whose claim is true?

Internomics · Module 7 — Separate Words From Credibility

People hear about costly signaling and conclude "If someone pays a large cost they must be telling the truth." No. A terrible company can spend twenty million dollars on advertising. Cost matters only if it falls differently on the true type and the false type. A written warranty is a good signal. A contractor who does sloppy work will pay out on it again and again. A flashy truck is different. A bad contractor can buy one just as easily. So don't ask how expensive it was. Ask who could afford to fake this. Next come actions.
