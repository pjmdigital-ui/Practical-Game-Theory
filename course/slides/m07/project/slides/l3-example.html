Example · Two credibility problems

One question blurs them together

The situation

A friend says an investment is "guaranteed to go up." A salesperson pitches a financial product.

What's going on

The friend is sincere but knows almost nothing about investing. The salesperson knows a lot and earns a commission.

The lesson

The friend has a knowledge problem. The salesperson has an incentive problem. "Do I trust them?" can't tell them apart.

Internomics · Module 7 — Separate Words From Credibility

A friend sincerely tells you "That investment is guaranteed to go up." But they know almost nothing about investing. They may simply be wrong. Now the salesperson. High knowledge. Uncertain alignment. The friend's problem is knowledge. The salesperson's problem is incentives. If all you ask is "Do I trust them?" you take the friend's tip and tune out the salesperson. You've got it backwards on both counts. So check the friend's claim with someone who actually knows. Use the salesperson's knowledge to understand the product. Then get it verified independently.
