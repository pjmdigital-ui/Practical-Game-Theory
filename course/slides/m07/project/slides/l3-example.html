Internomics · Module 7 — Separate Words From Credibility

Put two people side by side. A friend sincerely tells you, "That investment is guaranteed to go up." Maybe they believe it completely. But perhaps they know almost nothing about investing. Honesty isn't the same thing as accuracy. A false statement isn't automatically a lie, because a lie requires intentional deception. They may simply be wrong. That's the difference between truthfulness, do they report what they believe, and accuracy, is what they believe correct. Now the salesperson: high knowledge, uncertain alignment. Those are two completely different credibility problems. The friend's problem is knowledge. The salesperson's problem is incentives. If all you ask is "Do I trust them?", you blur them together and you can't tell which one you're facing.
