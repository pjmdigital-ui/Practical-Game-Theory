Separate Words From Credibility

What makes this message credible?

Paul Mascetta

Welcome to Module Seven: Separate Words From Credibility. In the last module we dealt with information. People decide based on what they know, what they believe, and what they think other people believe. That immediately creates another problem. Suppose I know something you don't, and I tell you. Should you believe me? That depends. Because I'm not simply a source of information. I'm also a player in the game. I have interests and incentives, and what I want you to believe may affect what you decide. This module is about one question: what makes this message credible?
