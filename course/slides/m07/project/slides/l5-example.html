Example · When you're the one telling the truth

"I know I'm honest" isn't evidence for them

The situation

You're selling your car. You know the transmission is perfectly fine. The buyer asks "Any transmission problems?" You say "None."

What happened

The buyer wants a mechanic to inspect it. You feel insulted. Why don't they trust me?

The lesson

The inspection isn't an accusation. It's a response to an information asymmetry.

Internomics · Module 7 — Separate Words From Credibility

You're selling your car. The transmission is fine. The buyer asks "Any transmission problems?" You say "None." And then you get offended when they want a mechanic to inspect it. Look at it from their side. The seller knows more about this car and benefits from the sale. The inspection isn't an accusation. It's a response to an information asymmetry. Almost every seller would say "No transmission problems" anyway. So your true sentence sounds like a dishonest seller's false one. Offer the inspection before they ask.
