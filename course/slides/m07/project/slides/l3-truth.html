Internomics · Module 7 — Separate Words From Credibility

Suppose someone tells you something false. Does that mean they lied? No. A lie requires intentional deception. They may simply be wrong. Truthfulness asks whether they report what they actually believe. Accuracy asks whether what they believe is correct. Someone can be truthful but inaccurate. When a claim turns out false we tend to jump to "They lied to me." Often they believed it. Their knowledge was the weak link and not their character. So you don't stop trusting their honesty. You stop relying on their knowledge in that area. Now let's pull knowledge and incentives apart.
