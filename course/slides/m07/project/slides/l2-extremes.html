Internomics · Module 7 — Separate Words From Credibility

Joseph Farrell and Matthew Rabin reviewed the game-theory literature on cheap talk. They argued that both common extremes are wrong. The first says costless words are meaningless. Under the right incentive structure ordinary talk can transmit substantial information. The second says people will just tell each other the truth because talking is free. Costless communication does not automatically produce efficient truth-telling. Both make the same mistake. They judge the words without looking at the game. Which way do you lean? Now let's see what happens in the lab when you change the incentives.
