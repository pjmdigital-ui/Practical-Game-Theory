Internomics · Module 7 — Separate Words From Credibility

Imagine two players. One knows something the other doesn't. The sender sends a message. The receiver makes a decision. And the sender cares what decision the receiver makes. Now communication becomes strategic. Vincent Crawford and Joel Sobel built a classic model of exactly this. Its central insight is that how much information can be credibly transmitted depends partly on how closely the players' interests are aligned. So never evaluate a message without the sender's incentives. In your next important conversation, who is the sender? Now a warning before you take this too far.
