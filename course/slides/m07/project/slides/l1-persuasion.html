Two different things

Persuasive and credible are two separate questions

How convincing was the message?

Charisma. Confidence. Articulate delivery. Emotional pull.

How credible is the underlying claim?

Knowledge. Incentives. Evidence. History. Verification.

A message can be persuasive without being credible. It can be credible without being persuasive.

Internomics · Module 7 — Separate Words From Credibility

Here's the first big idea. Persuasion and credibility are not the same thing. Persuasiveness is about delivery like charisma and confidence. None of that tells you whether they're accurate. Credibility is about the claim underneath. Does the person know? What do they gain if you believe them? Is there evidence? So keep two questions apart. How convincing was the message? And how credible is the underlying claim? Think about the last time you bought something because the pitch was great. So where does credibility come from? Let's look at the whole game.
