Internomics · Module 7 — Separate Words From Credibility

Suppose you're the one saying something true. When we aren't believed our instinct is to reach for better words. And sometimes the other person still doesn't move. Here's the test. Did they understand you? If they misunderstood you then work on the message. But if they understood you perfectly and still don't believe you, more rhetoric may accomplish nothing. You've got a credibility problem and not a message problem. The question isn't "How do I say this better?" It's "How do I make this claim more credible?" Let's look at why a reasonable person might not believe you.
