A persuasion failure may be a credibility failure

From

"How do I say this better?"

To

"How do I make this claim more credible?"

Internomics · Module 7 — Separate Words From Credibility

So far we've mostly looked at credibility from the listener's side. Now suppose you're the one communicating something true. You know you're telling the truth. That doesn't mean the other person should automatically believe you, and that's one of the hardest things for people to accept. You say, "But I'm telling the truth!" Fine. How would they know? From their position, you may have an incentive to say it. They may not have access to your information. They may have been misled before. They may have competing evidence. So if the other person understood you perfectly and still doesn't believe you, more rhetoric may accomplish nothing. The problem isn't "How do I say this better?" It's "How do I make this claim more credible?" Those are very different problems.
