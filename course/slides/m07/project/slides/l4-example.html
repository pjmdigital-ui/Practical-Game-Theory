Example · "We stand behind our work"

Two contractors, one claim

The situation

Two contractors bid on your job. Both say "We stand behind our work." The verbal claim is identical.

What happened

A wants full payment upfront. No references. No warranty. B has hundreds of verifiable reviews and a written warranty. Twenty years in the community.

The lesson

B has created evidence that would be hard to maintain if the claim were repeatedly false.

Internomics · Module 7 — Separate Words From Credibility

Two contractors both say "We stand behind our work." Contractor A requires full payment upfront. No warranty. Contractor B has hundreds of verifiable reviews and a written warranty. The verbal claim is identical. You'd hire B. B has created evidence that would be harder to maintain if their claim were repeatedly false. Would A say "We stand behind our work" anyway? Of course. A warranty is far cheaper for someone whose work really is good. A might still be a fine contractor. You just have no way to tell. Now the flip side.
