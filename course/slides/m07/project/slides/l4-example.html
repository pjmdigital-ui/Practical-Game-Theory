Internomics · Module 7 — Separate Words From Credibility

Imagine two contractors. Both say, "We stand behind our work." Contractor A wants full payment upfront, gives no references, offers no warranty, and disappears after the job. Contractor B has hundreds of verifiable reviews, a written warranty, twenty years in the same community, and fixes problems at their own expense. The verbal claim is identical. The surrounding signals are very different. You don't need game theory to feel the difference. What game theory does is explain why: Contractor B has created evidence that would be hard to maintain if the claim were repeatedly false. Now the flip side. A manager tells the team, "I want everyone to challenge me." An employee disagrees in a meeting. The manager gets angry, and later that employee is left off an important project. Next month the manager asks again for honest feedback. What do people believe? Not the words. The observed consequence of disagreement has entered the game.
