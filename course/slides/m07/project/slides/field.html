Internomics · Module 7 — Separate Words From Credibility

Here's your assignment for this week. Find three high-stakes claims made to you: at work, in a purchase, or at home. Run the eight questions on each one, and note whether the claim felt more or less credible than the evidence supported. Catch one "Would they say this anyway?" statement, and write down what evidence would actually distinguish the cases. Then turn it around. Before you make one truthful claim of your own, a status update, a commitment, a recommendation, add one piece of evidence the listener can check without relying on your word. Record how they responded. Next week we'll debrief: where persuasiveness and credibility pointed in different directions, and where you slid toward cynicism and found your way back.
