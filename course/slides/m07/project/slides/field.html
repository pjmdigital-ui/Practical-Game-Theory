Internomics · Module 7 — Separate Words From Credibility

Here's your assignment for this week. Find three high-stakes claims made to you. They might come at work or in a purchase or at home. Run the eight questions on each one. Then note whether the claim felt more or less credible than the evidence supported. Next catch one "Would they say this anyway?" statement. Write down what evidence would actually tell the cases apart. Then turn it around. Pick one truthful claim of your own. It could be a status update or a commitment or a recommendation. Before you make it, add one piece of evidence the listener can check without relying on your word. Then record how they responded. Next week we'll debrief. Where did persuasiveness and credibility point in different directions? Which one did you act on? And where did you slide toward cynicism? How did you find your way back?
