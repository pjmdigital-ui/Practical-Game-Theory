Internomics · Module 7 — Separate Words From Credibility

Imagine someone says "Trust me." Now picture two situations. Your business partner has kept every major commitment to you for fifteen years. They say "Trust me. I'll take care of this." Then a stranger you're about to wire fifty thousand dollars to says exactly the same thing. Same words. Completely different credibility. Credibility isn't contained inside the sentence. It comes from the game surrounding the sentence. This module replaces "Do I believe them?" with a better question. "What makes this believable?" Let me show you what you'll be able to do by the end.
