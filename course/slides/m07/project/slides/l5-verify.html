Internomics · Module 7 — Separate Words From Credibility

When you're telling the truth, make it easier for the other person to evaluate. That may mean evidence they can check. A demonstration. References who aren't you. A guarantee. Documentation. Independent verification. Every one of these moves the claim away from your word alone. Your word is the one thing they can't check. You don't need a certificate to tell your friend where you parked. But when you benefit from being believed, their skepticism is reasonable. And offering verification before anyone asks is itself a signal. Before the examples we need one distinction.
