Internomics · Module 7 — Separate Words From Credibility

We've all heard "actions speak louder than words." Sometimes. But actions can be strategic too. A company makes a large public investment in a new product. That could signal real confidence. Or management could be trying to convince investors, or they're too committed to admit they were wrong. So treat behavior as evidence, not proof. Verification changes things too. If I'm selling you my house and tell you the roof is fine, you can hire an independent inspector. That doesn't remove all uncertainty, but it brings in a player whose incentives are better aligned with an accurate assessment. Reputation is a summary of information from previous rounds of the game. And it's fragile. If someone has told you the truth a hundred times and you catch them fabricating something important, you don't just update on that one statement. You may update your model of the person.
