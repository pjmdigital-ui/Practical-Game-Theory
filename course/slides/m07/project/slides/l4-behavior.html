Internomics · Module 7 — Separate Words From Credibility

"Actions speak louder than words." Sometimes. But actions can be strategic too. A company makes a large public investment in a new product. It could signal confidence. Or they're too committed to admit they were wrong. So treat behavior as evidence and not as proof. Behavior gets really useful when a stated value is tested. Someone says "I value honesty." Then you watch how they react to an uncomfortable truth. Do they retaliate? Staying calm when honesty stings costs more for someone who doesn't mean it. Now let's bring in someone else to check the claim.
