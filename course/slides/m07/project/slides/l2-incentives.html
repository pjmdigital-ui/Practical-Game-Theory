Credibility is partly a property of incentives

Internomics · Module 7 — Separate Words From Credibility

Caleb Cox and Brock Stoddard ran an experiment on exactly this. Players got private information about whether contributing to a public good would be valuable. They could send costless messages first. Individuals sometimes had an incentive to exaggerate. Communication was largely truthful and it improved efficiency. Then the researchers increased the incentive to exaggerate. Truthfulness declined. The payoff for exaggerating changed. So when you hear a claim, ask how much the speaker gains from moving your decision. Big gain means the words carry less. Not zero. Less.
