Internomics · Module 7 — Separate Words From Credibility

Here's where people go wrong once they learn this. They jump straight to "They benefit from saying it. So it must be false." That's not strategic thinking. That's paranoia. The precise version goes like this. They benefit from me believing this. So the statement by itself gives me less reason to update than it would if their incentives were neutral. It doesn't make the claim false. It makes the words weaker evidence. And plenty of honest people benefit from the truth. A good contractor benefits when you believe their work is good.
