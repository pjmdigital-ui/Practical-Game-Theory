Credibility is not confidence

Don't confuse the delivery of a message with the evidence behind it

The negotiator

Sounds certain because doubt would weaken their position

The salesperson

Speaks emphatically because hesitation could cost the sale

The leader

Projects certainty because the group may need confidence

None of this makes the claim true or false. And a nervous person can be completely right.

Internomics · Module 7 — Separate Words From Credibility

Someone speaks decisively and never hesitates. We read that as "They know what they're talking about." But confidence is not evidence of accuracy. And confidence itself can be strategic. A negotiator may sound certain because uncertainty would weaken their position. A salesperson may speak emphatically because hesitation would reduce the chance of a purchase. In each case the confidence is a response to incentives. It isn't a readout of what they know. So separate the delivery of a message from the evidence behind it. And confidence is only one way a message can move you without informing you.
