Example · "Challenge me"

The manager who wants honest feedback

The situation

A manager tells the team: "I want everyone to challenge me when you think I'm wrong."

What happened

An employee disagrees in a meeting. The manager gets angry. Later that employee is left off an important project.

The lesson

Next month the manager asks again. People believe the observed consequence. Not the words.

Internomics · Module 7 — Separate Words From Credibility

A manager tells the team "I want everyone to challenge me when you think I'm wrong." An employee disagrees in a meeting. The manager becomes angry. Later that employee gets excluded from a project. The next month the manager says "I really want more honest feedback." What do employees believe? The observed consequence of disagreement has entered the game. The manager's behavior has created a different incentive structure. People decide based on the payoff they saw. If you lead anyone, what have your reactions taught people about telling you the truth?
