Internomics · Module 7 — Separate Words From Credibility

This module could become "How to make yourself look credible." Our standard is the same as always. As the speaker you make truthful claims easier to evaluate. As the listener you evaluate important claims intelligently. Fake reviews and fabricated evidence and staged signals are deception. Once you learn to analyze incentives it's easy to distrust everything. Someone helps you and you think "What's their angle?" Most human communication is not an elaborate deception game.
