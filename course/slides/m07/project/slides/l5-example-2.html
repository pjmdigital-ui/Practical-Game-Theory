Example · The on-time promise

A better story won't fix a history

The situation

You tell a client "We're going to deliver this project on time." You were late on the last three projects.

What happened

You explain it beautifully. You tell a story. You handle every objection. They still don't believe you.

The lesson

It's a credibility problem. Milestones they can check might help where more rhetoric won't.

Internomics · Module 7 — Separate Words From Credibility

You tell a client "We're going to deliver this project on time." You explain it beautifully. The client still doesn't believe you. So you conclude you need a more persuasive argument. Maybe not. Perhaps you've been late on the last three projects. They have three rounds of evidence. Your speech is one round of cheap talk. So what might actually help? Milestones they can check. Early warning when something slips. Maybe a structure where the risk of lateness lands on you. Where are you trying to out-talk a track record right now? Let's close the lesson.
