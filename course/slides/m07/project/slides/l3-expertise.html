Internomics · Module 7 — Separate Words From Credibility

First is expertise without alignment. You're buying a complicated financial product. The salesperson knows far more about it than you do. But they also receive a large commission if you buy. You separate two questions. How much do they know? And how much does their payoff depend on what I decide? Now reverse it. Your brother genuinely wants what's best for you. But he knows nothing about the medical decision you're considering. So don't just ask "Do I trust my brother?" Ask "Trust him to do what?" Diagnose a complex condition? Maybe not.
