Example · Same words. Different game.

"Turn left."

The situation

We're driving to an unfamiliar place. You can see the GPS. I can't. You say "Turn left."

What happened

If we share a destination the words are highly informative. If we're racing to get there first I have to ask what you gain if I believe you.

The lesson

The words cost nothing either way. Their meaning depends on whether our interests line up.

Communication didn't change. The game did.

Internomics · Module 7 — Separate Words From Credibility

Imagine we're driving to an unfamiliar place. You can see the GPS. I can't. You say "Turn left." Those words are highly informative. We both want to arrive at the same destination. You gain nothing by sending me the wrong way. Now we're in separate cars competing to get there first. You say "Turn left." Same words. Different strategic meaning. Now I have to ask what you gain if I believe you. The communication didn't change. The game did. Who in your life is in the same car as you? Here's the takeaway.
