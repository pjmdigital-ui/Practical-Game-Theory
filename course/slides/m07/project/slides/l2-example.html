Internomics · Module 7 — Separate Words From Credibility

Here's why cheap talk can work. Imagine you and I are driving to an unfamiliar location. You can see the GPS; I can't. You say, "Turn left." Those words cost you nothing. Yet they're highly informative, because we both want to arrive at the same destination. Our interests are aligned. Now change the game. We're competing to reach the destination first. You say, "Turn left." Same words. Different strategic meaning. Now I have to consider: what do you gain if I believe you? Notice what happened there. The communication didn't change at all. The game did. And that's exactly why you can't judge a sentence without looking at the game it was spoken inside.
