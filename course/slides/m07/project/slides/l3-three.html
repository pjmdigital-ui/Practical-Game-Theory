Internomics · Module 7 — Separate Words From Credibility

This is where I think ordinary thinking about credibility is too simple. We often reduce everything to one question: is this person honest? That's certainly important, but it isn't enough. There are at least three different questions. Are they honest: are they telling you what they genuinely believe? Are they informed: are they actually in a position to know? And what are their incentives: what do they gain or lose depending on what you believe? Those three can come apart. Someone can be completely honest and completely wrong. Someone can know far more than you and have a powerful incentive to steer your decision. And someone can want the very best for you and know nothing about the subject. Each of those is a different credibility problem.
