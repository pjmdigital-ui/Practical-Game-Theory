Signaling and commitment are different ideas

Signaling

What does my action tell you about me?

Commitment

How does my action change my future options?

"I won't accept less than $100,000." Make it impossible to accept less and you haven't just signaled. You've changed what you can rationally do later.

Internomics · Module 7 — Separate Words From Credibility

Suppose I tell you "I will not accept less than one hundred thousand dollars." Right now that's mostly cheap talk. Now suppose I take an action that makes it extremely costly for me to accept less. I haven't simply signaled what I'm likely to do. I've changed what I can rationally do later. That's commitment. Signaling asks "What does my action tell you about me?" Commitment asks "How does my action change my future options?" Sometimes one action does both. Module Eight is entirely about commitment. Before we get there, one ethical line for this module.
