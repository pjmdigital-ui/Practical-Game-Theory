Internomics · Module 7 — Separate Words From Credibility

A statement can change your behavior even if it isn't credible evidence. Someone shouts "If you don't agree you'll regret it." You may comply. That doesn't mean you believed it. Repetition and authority and social pressure all influence behavior. None of those is evidence about whether the claim is accurate. A message can influence without informing. So watch for two sentences people confuse. "It changed my mind." And "It gave me good reason to change my mind." When you notice you've changed your mind, ask which one is true.
