Credibility lives in the whole game

Payoffs

What does the speaker gain if I believe this?

Information

Are they actually in a position to know?

History

What happened when they made similar claims before?

Constraints

What prevents them from behaving differently later?

Evidence

Can the claim be independently verified?

Signals

Does their behavior carry information about the claim?

Reputation

What does their previous behavior suggest?

A much richer question than "Did they sound convincing?"
