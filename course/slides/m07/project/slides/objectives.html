Internomics · Module 7 — Separate Words From Credibility

Here's what you'll be able to do by the end of this module. You'll be able to separate how persuasive a message is from how credible the underlying claim is, because those are two different questions. You'll evaluate a claim along separate dimensions: is the person honest, are they informed, and what are their incentives? You'll have one of the most useful questions in the whole book: would they say this anyway? You'll understand why a costly signal is informative only when it's easier or more worthwhile for someone whose claim is true. And you'll learn to design ways to make your own truthful claims easier for other people to check.
