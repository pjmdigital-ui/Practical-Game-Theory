Internomics · Module 7 — Separate Words From Credibility

By the end of this module you'll be able to do five things. You'll separate how persuasive a message is from how credible the claim underneath it is. You'll ask about honesty and knowledge and incentives separately. You'll use one of the most useful questions in the book. Would they say this anyway? You'll know when a costly signal actually tells you something. And you'll make your own truthful claims easier to check. Let's begin.
