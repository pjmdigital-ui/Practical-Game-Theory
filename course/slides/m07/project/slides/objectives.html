Internomics · Module 7 — Separate Words From Credibility

Here's what you'll be able to do by the end of this module. First you'll separate how persuasive a message is from how credible the claim underneath it is. Those are two different questions. Second you'll evaluate a claim along separate lines. Is the person honest? Are they informed? What are their incentives? Third you'll have one of the most useful questions in the whole book. Would they say this anyway? Fourth you'll understand when a costly signal actually tells you something. The answer is only when it's easier or more worthwhile for someone whose claim is true. And fifth you'll learn to make your own truthful claims easier for other people to check. Let's start with the game around the sentence.
