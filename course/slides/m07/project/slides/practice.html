Internomics · Module 7 — Separate Words From Credibility

Let's practice. Dana runs a small marketing agency and is choosing a web developer for a large client project. Marcus is polished and energetic. He says, "I've never missed a deadline, and I'm the best React developer in this city." He never hesitates, he has a beautiful slide deck, and he offers ten percent off if Dana signs this week. When she asks for references, he says past clients are under NDA, "but trust me, they were thrilled." Priya is quieter and a bit awkward. She says, "I think I can hit your timeline, but the payment integration is the risky part, and I'd want a week to scope it." She sends three live sites she built, names two former clients Dana can call, and proposes milestone payments tied to working deliverables. And Dana's business partner, who knows Marcus socially, says, "Marcus is a great guy. He'd never lie to you." Pause the video and work through these four questions before we look at a model answer.
