Signals · Michael Spence

Sometimes behavior carries information that words alone do not

The problem

Employers can't directly observe an applicant's productivity before hiring.

The signal

Under certain conditions education helps employers tell workers apart.

The broader idea

Look for behavior that's harder to produce when the claim is false.

Internomics · Module 7 — Separate Words From Credibility

Michael Spence's work on signaling was recognized as part of the 2001 Nobel Prize in economics. His famous example was education and employment. Employers can't perfectly observe an applicant's productivity before hiring. And every applicant will say they're productive. Under certain conditions education can function as a signal. The claim is not that a degree proves you're good. It's that an observable action can carry information about something hidden. In your work, what's the equivalent of the work sample? But be careful. People take this idea and run straight into a trap.
