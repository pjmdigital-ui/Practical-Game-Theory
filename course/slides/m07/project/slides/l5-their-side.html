Internomics · Module 7 — Separate Words From Credibility

You know you're telling the truth. That doesn't mean the other person should automatically believe you. There are at least four reasons a reasonable person might hold back. You may have an incentive to say it. They may lack access to your information. They may have been misled before by someone saying these exact words. Or they may have competing evidence. None of those is an insult to you. So instead of becoming frustrated, ask what reason they have to believe you. If they've been burned, give them a way to check.
