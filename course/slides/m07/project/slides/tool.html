Internomics · Module 7 — Separate Words From Credibility

Your tool is the Credibility Test. It's in your workbook. Write the claim exactly as they said it and the decision it affects. Then go through eight questions. Do they know? What do they want me to believe? What happens if I believe them? Would they say this even if it were false? What evidence supports the claim? What does their behavior suggest? What does their history suggest? And what would make this claim costly to fake? You won't always have all eight answers. The blanks are information too. The workbook has three more parts.
