Internomics · Module 7 — Separate Words From Credibility

Your tool for this module is the Credibility Test. Use it for high-stakes claims, not for directions to the restroom. Write the claim in their words and the decision it affects. Then the eight questions. Do they know: are they in a position to have this information? What do they want me to believe? What happens if I believe them: how does my response affect their payoff? Would they say this even if it were false? What evidence supports it: separate assertion from verification. What does their behavior suggest, treating it as evidence, not proof? What does their history suggest? And what would make this claim costly to fake? In the workbook, Part Two rates honesty, knowledge and incentive alignment separately. Part Three separates how persuasive it felt from how credible it is. And Part Four flips it: when you're the speaker, what reason do they have to believe you?
