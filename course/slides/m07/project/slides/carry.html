The question to carry

What makes this message credible?

Internomics · Next: Module 8 — Commitment Changes the Game

So here's the question to carry with you. The next time someone makes a claim that matters, don't focus only on the sentence. Zoom out. Look at the game around it. Not "Did they sound sincere?" Not "Do I like them?" Ask what makes this message credible. What information did I actually receive? Words can inform. Words can coordinate. Words can persuade. Words can deceive. And their value depends on the game they're spoken inside. But there's one way to make a statement far more powerful than merely saying it. You can change your own future options so the other person has reason to believe you. That's Module Eight. It's where we'll see why sometimes having fewer options makes you stronger.
