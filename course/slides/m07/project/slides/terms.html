Internomics · Module 7 — Separate Words From Credibility

Here are the key terms from this module, and they're all in your workbook. Credibility: how much reason a claim gives you to update, given the speaker's knowledge, incentives, evidence, behavior and history. It's not the same as persuasiveness. Cheap talk: costless, nonbinding communication, which can still carry real information when interests are aligned. "Would they say this anyway?": a test of whether a statement distinguishes true cases from false ones. A signal: an observable action that conveys something hidden, especially one that's easier or more worthwhile for someone whose claim is true. Verification: inspections, audits, references, independent reviews. Reputation: a summary of previous rounds of the game. And commitment, which we'll study next: an action that changes what you can rationally do later. Two more in the workbook: sender and receiver, and the difference between truthfulness and accuracy.
