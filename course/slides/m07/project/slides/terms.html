Internomics · Module 7 — Separate Words From Credibility

Here are the key terms from this module. They're all in your workbook. Credibility is how much reason a claim gives you to update. It depends on the speaker's knowledge and incentives. It depends on evidence and behavior and history. And it's not the same as persuasiveness. Cheap talk is costless and nonbinding communication. It can still carry real information when interests are aligned. "Would they say this anyway?" is a test of whether a statement tells true cases from false ones. A signal is an observable action that conveys something hidden. The best ones are easier or more worthwhile for someone whose claim is true. Verification means checks that go beyond the speaker's word. Inspections. Audits. References. Independent reviews. Reputation is a summary of previous rounds of the game. And commitment is what we'll study next. It's an action that changes what you can rationally do later. Two more are in the workbook. Sender and receiver. And the difference between truthfulness and accuracy.
