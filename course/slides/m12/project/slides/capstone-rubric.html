Capstone · Scoring rubric

What a strong Capstone shows

CriterionWhat "Strong" looks like
Seeing the gameThe full structure with off-stage players. Facts kept apart from story.
Lens selectionA sharp rationale for the lenses chosen. Restraint about the rest.
HypothesesDistinct explanations. A way to tell them apart. A checkable prediction.
Move designFollows the analysis. Weighs vulnerability, walking away and a new game.
EthicsPasses all four checks with specific reasons. Respects their agency.
Observe / Update / AdaptTreats surprise as information. Revises the model. Names a lesson.

To pass: Proficient or better on all six. Ethics must be at least Proficient no matter what else you score.

Internomics · Module 12 — Strategic Judgment

Here's how the Capstone is scored. Six criteria are each rated Developing or Proficient or Strong. Seeing the game means the full structure with facts kept apart from story. Ethics means passing all four checks and respecting their agency. One rule matters most. A passing Capstone is Proficient or better on all six. Ethics must be at least Proficient no matter what. And notice what isn't on the list. Getting the outcome you wanted. This tests whether you saw clearly and acted with integrity and learned. Let's close with the question to carry.
