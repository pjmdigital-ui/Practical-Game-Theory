Internomics · Module 12 — Strategic Judgment

Throughout this course we've returned to four principles. Truth. Autonomy. Dignity. Informed choice. They're constraints on how this framework should be used. A strategy can "work" and still be wrong. You may be able to hide relevant information so someone makes the choice you prefer. That doesn't mean they made an informed choice. So we need two different questions. Will this work? And should I do it? Here's where people slip. They ask the first question carefully. Then they quietly assume that if it works, it must be fine. Next is the opposite mistake.
