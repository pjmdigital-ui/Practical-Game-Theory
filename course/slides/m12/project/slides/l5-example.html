The situation

Someone you care about says: "You never listen to me." The reflex is to grab a technique. Active listening. Mirroring. A question.

See the game first

Is this a one-off or a repeated pattern?

What payoff are they seeking? Understanding? Respect? Attention?

Do they criticize expecting me not to listen? Do I defend expecting criticism?

Are we failing to coordinate on what "being heard" means?

Internomics · Module 12 — Strategic Judgment

Here's the last example in the book. Someone you care about says "You never listen to me." The tactical response is to reach for a communication technique. But first see the game. Is this an isolated comment or a repeated pattern? What payoff are they seeking? Have your past responses taught them that raising a concern leads to defensiveness? Could you change the game by changing the pattern? You may still use a technique. But now it serves an understanding of the situation. That brings us to the final takeaway of the course.
