Internomics · Module 12 — Strategic Judgment

I want to make one distinction before we finish. The eleven models are not a procedure. You're not supposed to walk into every conversation and think, "Okay. Step one: identify the game. Step two: identify the payoffs. Step three." That's not how good judgment works. Think about an experienced driver. They aren't consciously saying, "Check mirror. Calculate distance. Estimate speed. Adjust steering angle." They've developed perception. They notice things. The same thing should happen here. At first, you'll ask the questions consciously, and that's fine while you're learning. Eventually, you'll start noticing the structure almost automatically. Not recitation. Recognition.
