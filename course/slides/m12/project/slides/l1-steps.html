Internomics · Module 12 — Strategic Judgment

The eleven models in this course are not a procedure. You're not supposed to walk into every conversation and think "Step one: identify the game. Step two: identify the payoffs." Think about an experienced driver. They aren't consciously saying "Check mirror. Calculate distance." They've developed perception. At first you'll ask the questions consciously. Over time someone makes a demand and the payoff question just shows up. The common misunderstanding is that the goal is to run the framework every time. The framework is scaffolding.
