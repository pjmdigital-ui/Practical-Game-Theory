Internomics · Module 12 — Strategic Judgment

Let me name the most common mistakes. Running all eleven models every time: full analysis of everything is slow and noisy. Pick the two or three that matter most. Analyzing interactions that don't need it: turning "Sure" into a signaling puzzle makes ordinary life needlessly suspicious. Falling in love with your diagnosis: your first model will often be wrong, so look for evidence that would disprove it. And labeling people irrational: that word often replaces thinking. Three more to watch for. Confusing strategic with suspicious: become less naive without becoming cynical. Collapsing "Will this work?" into "Should I do it?": ask both, every time. And trusting uncalibrated intuition: experience without feedback can produce confident error. That's what the Practice Log is for.
