Research spotlight · The audit's completeness test

Why eleven lenses and not more?

What it suggests

The test: does a concept give an ordinary person a genuinely different and broadly useful way to see interaction? Bargaining and coalitions were folded in as supporting ideas. Choosing lenses well matters more than owning more.

What it doesn't prove

Eleven is a design judgment. It's not an empirical discovery. The audit doesn't claim the models are exhaustive. It doesn't claim every situation fits neatly into one of them.

The goal: "the smallest useful latticework of game-theoretic lenses for human interaction."
