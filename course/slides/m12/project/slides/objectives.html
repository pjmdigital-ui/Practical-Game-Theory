Internomics · Module 12 — Strategic Judgment

Here's what you'll be able to do by the end of this module. You'll be able to pick the two or three lenses that matter most in an interaction, and justify why the others can wait. You'll be able to tell when a situation doesn't need analysis at all, because more analysis would only add suspicion and confusion. You'll separate facts from your story about the facts, state your confidence, and hold competing hypotheses. You'll run the learning loop, treating surprise as information. And you'll evaluate every move against two questions: will this work, and should I do it? Then you'll put all of it to work in the Capstone.
