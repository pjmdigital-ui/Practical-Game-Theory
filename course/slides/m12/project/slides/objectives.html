Internomics · Module 12 — Strategic Judgment

Here's what you'll be able to do by the end of this module. First, you'll pick the two or three lenses that matter most in an interaction. And you'll be able to say why the others can wait. Second, you'll tell when a situation doesn't need analysis at all. In those moments more analysis only adds suspicion and confusion. Third, you'll separate facts from your story about the facts. You'll state your confidence. You'll hold competing hypotheses instead of grabbing the first one. Fourth, you'll run the learning loop. Model. Act. Observe. Update. Adapt. You'll treat surprise as information instead of as an insult. And fifth, you'll judge every move against two questions. Will this work? Should I do it? Then you'll put all of it to work in the Capstone. Let's start with how these models are actually meant to be used.
