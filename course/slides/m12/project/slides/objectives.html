Internomics · Module 12 — Strategic Judgment

Here's what you'll be able to do by the end of this module. You'll pick the two or three lenses that matter most. You'll tell when a situation doesn't need analysis at all. You'll separate facts from your story and hold competing hypotheses. You'll run the learning loop and treat surprise as information. And you'll judge every move against two questions. Will this work?
