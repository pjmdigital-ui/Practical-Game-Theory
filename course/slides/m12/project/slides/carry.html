The question to carry

What did I learn from what just happened?

Internomics · Course complete

In Module Zero, the question to carry was "What did I assume before I spoke?" Here's the last one, and it's the one I hope you carry for the rest of your life: what did I learn from what just happened? Not "Was I right?" Not "Did I win?" What did I learn? Your intuition should become more trustworthy because it has survived contact with reality, not because you've repeated the same story often enough to believe it. Experience alone doesn't guarantee good judgment. You can repeat the wrong interpretation for twenty years. This question is how you don't.
