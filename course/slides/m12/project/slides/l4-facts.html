Separate the fact from your story about it

The fact

They didn't return your call.

Your story

"They're disrespecting me."

Possible, but not identical. Maybe they forgot, are overwhelmed, never got the message, are avoiding it, or are delaying on purpose.

Internomics · Module 12 — Strategic Judgment

Suppose someone doesn't return your call. The fact is: they didn't return your call. The interpretation is: they're disrespecting you. That's possible. But it's not the same thing. Maybe they forgot. Maybe they're avoiding the conversation. Maybe they're overwhelmed. Maybe they never got the message. Maybe they're strategically delaying. The skill is being able to say three things. Here's what I know. Here's what I think it means. Here's how confident I am. That's intellectual discipline. And it protects you from making your response to an assumption rather than to reality. The moment your story quietly turns into a fact, you've stopped seeing the game and started reacting to your own guess.
