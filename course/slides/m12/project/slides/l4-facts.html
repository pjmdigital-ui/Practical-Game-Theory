Internomics · Module 12 — Strategic Judgment

Separate the fact from your story about the fact. Suppose someone doesn't return your call. The fact is that they didn't return your call. The interpretation is that they're disrespecting you. That's possible but not the same thing. Maybe they forgot. Maybe they're overwhelmed. Maybe they never got the message. Each calls for a different response. Treat all five as disrespect and you'll get four wrong. Here's the misunderstanding. Separating fact from story doesn't mean having no story. The skill is noticing that it's a story. That connects to the pause before you respond.
