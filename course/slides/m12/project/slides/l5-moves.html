Strategy isn't always self-protection

Sometimes the correct move is vulnerability

The structure

A repeated relationship. Both sides protect themselves. Neither takes the first step because each fears exploitation.

The move

One person takes a measured risk. A disclosure. An offer of trust. A concession. An apology.

Why it works

It can change their beliefs and shift the equilibrium. The risk is exactly why it carries information.

You could stay protected indefinitely. Sometimes taking a risk transforms the game.

Internomics · Module 12 — Strategic Judgment

Sometimes the correct move is vulnerability. You're in a repeated relationship. Both sides are protecting themselves. Neither takes the first cooperative step because each fears exploitation. Think of two longtime co-founders who each hold back their real concerns. So the company drifts. One person can take a measured risk. They disclose something. They apologize. And there is risk. That's precisely why the action carries information. Costly signals are believable because they're costly. Measured means a calibrated first step. Is there a relationship where you're both waiting for the other to go first? Next is walking away.
