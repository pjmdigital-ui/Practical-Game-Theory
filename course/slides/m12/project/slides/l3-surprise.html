Internomics · Module 12 — Strategic Judgment

When another person surprises you, that's one of the most strategically useful moments you'll ever get. Surprise reveals gaps in your model. And every surprise comes with its own question. You thought the threat was empty. They followed through. Ask what made the commitment stronger than you realized. Think of the manager whose public late list makes deadlines worse. That's a reason to ask what the list is teaching people to do. A person committed to learning should become curious when surprised. Not defensive. Next is one more way people avoid learning.
