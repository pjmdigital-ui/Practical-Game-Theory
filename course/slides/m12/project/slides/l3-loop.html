Internomics · Module 12 — Strategic Judgment

Remember this loop from Module Zero? We've come full circle. This is the rule that sits above all eleven models. Model: based on the information you have, form your best understanding of the game. Act: make a move. Observe: what actually happens? Update: where did reality differ from your prediction? Adapt: change your strategy, or change your model. Then repeat. That is strategic thinking. Not perfect prediction. Not omniscience. Not always being right. Learning faster from what happens. And notice the Update step is a specific question. Not "Did it go well?" but "Where did reality differ from what I predicted?" That's why, in your Practice Log, you write the prediction down before you act.
