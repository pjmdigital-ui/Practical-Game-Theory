Internomics · Module 12 — Strategic Judgment

Suppose someone says to you, "I need your answer by tomorrow. This is absolutely the final offer." The old question is, "What should I say?" But now you have other lenses. See the Game: who actually has authority here? Is this person even making the decision? See the Payoffs: what happens to them if I say no? Do they genuinely need an answer tomorrow? Manage Predictability: have I taught them that artificial pressure makes me move? Credibility: what makes "final offer" believable? Commitment: are they actually constrained, or just saying they are? Shadow of the Future: will I negotiate with this person again, and what precedent does my response create? Change the Game: do I need to accept their yes-or-no structure at all? Every lens produces a useful question. But you probably don't need all eleven. That's the point. Judgment means recognizing which two or three matter most right now.
