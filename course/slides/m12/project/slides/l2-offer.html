Internomics · Module 12 — Strategic Judgment

Someone says "I need your answer by tomorrow. This is absolutely the final offer." Every lens produces a useful question. See the Game asks who actually has authority here. Manage Predictability asks if I've taught them that artificial pressure makes me move. Credibility asks what makes "final offer" believable. But you probably don't need all eleven. If you ran every one before replying, you'd freeze. Judgment means recognizing which two or three matter most right now. So which two would you pick? Next is knowing when to use the framework at all.
