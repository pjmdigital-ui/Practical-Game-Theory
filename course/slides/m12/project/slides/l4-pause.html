Internomics · Module 12 — Strategic Judgment

My favorite practical effect of this course is a tiny space between stimulus and response. Your first interpretation appears instantly. Before acting you ask one question. What game am I in? Someone makes a threat. You ask whether carrying it out is credible. Loud isn't the same as committed. A team fails. Instead of blaming the people, you inspect the system. People think the pause means being passive or slow. It doesn't. The point is that you choose the move. Now let's put this to work on a resistant customer.
