Internomics · Module 12 — Strategic Judgment

Here's why recognition matters so much. Nobody is going to walk into your office and say, "Hello, Paul. I am now presenting you with an incomplete-information bargaining game containing asymmetric outside options and a credibility problem." They're going to say, "We need an answer today." Your child isn't going to say, "I have detected that your repeated concessions have created an exploitable strategy." They're going to ask again. Your employees won't announce that the compensation mechanism creates a social dilemma. They'll just stop sharing leads. And your partner won't say, "We have entered a stable negative equilibrium reinforced by reciprocal expectations." You'll just have the same argument again. The games come disguised as ordinary life.
