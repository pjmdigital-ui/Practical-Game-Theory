Internomics · Module 12 — Strategic Judgment

The world will not label the game for you. Nobody walks into your office and says "I am now presenting you with an incomplete-information bargaining game containing asymmetric outside options and a credibility problem." They say "We need an answer today." What makes "today" believable? Games come disguised as ordinary life. You get the symptom. Your job is to see the structure. People often wait for a situation that looks like a game theory problem. Most strategic moments won't look strategic at all. Let's look at a few more disguises.
