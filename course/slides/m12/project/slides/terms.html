Internomics · Module 12 — Strategic Judgment

Strategic judgment means choosing which lenses matter and acting and learning. Recognition is noticing structure almost automatically. A provisional model is a hypothesis to test. Prediction error is the gap between what you expected and what happened. You only get it if you predicted first. Multiple hypotheses means holding several explanations and gathering evidence that tells them apart. Transfer means recognizing a familiar structure somewhere new.
