Internomics · Module 12 — Strategic Judgment

Here are the key terms for this final module, and they're all in your workbook. Strategic judgment: choosing which lenses matter in this situation, acting on your best model, and learning from what happens. Recognition: noticing the structure almost automatically, rather than reciting steps. A provisional model: your current best interpretation, held as a hypothesis to test, never as a conclusion to defend. Prediction error, or surprise: the gap between what you expected and what happened, the most useful signal that your model has a hole in it. Multiple hypotheses: holding several explanations and gathering evidence that tells them apart. Transfer: recognizing a familiar structure, like commitment or coordination, somewhere you've never seen it before. And calibration: making intuition trustworthy by checking it against outcomes, so your confidence rises only as your accuracy does.
