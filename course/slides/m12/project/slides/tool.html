The Strategic Judgment Worksheet

1. The situation

Facts only. My story. My confidence (0–100%). My immediate impulse.

2. The eleven questions

Scan all eleven. Mark the 2–3 that matter most. Note why the rest can wait.

3. Competing hypotheses

Three explanations. The evidence for each. What would tell them apart.

4. The move

My move and prediction. Will this work? Should I do it? The four ethics checks.

Then keep the Practice Log: Model → Act → Prediction → Observe → Update → Adapt.

Internomics · Module 12 — Strategic Judgment

Your tool is the Strategic Judgment Worksheet. Don't use it for "Sure." Part one is the situation. Facts only. Then your story and your confidence from zero to a hundred. Then your immediate impulse. Part two is the eleven questions. Mark the two or three that matter most. Part three is at least three competing hypotheses. Part four is the move and your prediction. Ask will this work. Then ask should I do it. Then there's the Practice Log. It's how your intuition gets calibrated. Let's try it on a real scenario.
