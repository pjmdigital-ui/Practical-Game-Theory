Internomics · Module 12 — Strategic Judgment

Your tool for this module is the Strategic Judgment Worksheet, and it's the last one you'll add to your workbook. Use it when the stakes matter, something doesn't make sense, a pattern keeps repeating, or trust is uncertain. Don't use it for "Sure." Part one is the situation: what happened, facts only; your story; how confident you are in that story; and your immediate impulse. Part two: scan all eleven questions and mark the two or three that matter most, and write down which ones you're setting aside and why. Part three: competing hypotheses, with what would tell them apart. Part four: the move. Your prediction, "Will this work?", and then "Should I do it?" with the four checks: truth, autonomy, dignity, informed choice. Plus: what are we cooperating toward, and is the right move vulnerability or walking away? Then the Practice Log: one entry per meaningful interaction. Over weeks, that log is how your intuition gets calibrated.
