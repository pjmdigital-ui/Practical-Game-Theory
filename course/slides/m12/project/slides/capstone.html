Capstone project · Two to three weeks

One real interaction. The full cycle.

1. Pick and frame · Days 1–2. Write the situation brief.

2. Scan wide · Day 3. All eleven questions. No editing.

3. Choose the lens · Day 3. Circle two to four.

4. Go deep · Days 4–5. Fully analyze the chosen lenses.

5. Hold hypotheses · Day 5. At least three. Plan how to tell them apart.

6. Design the move · Day 6. Alternatives. Ethics. A checkable prediction.

7. Act · Days 7–14. Log facts first. Interpretation second.

8. Observe → update → adapt · Days 7–21. Follow up and log it.

9. Debrief · Final days. Be honest about what you got wrong.

Internomics · Module 12 — Strategic Judgment

Now the Capstone. Over two to three weeks you'll take one real high-stakes interaction through the full strategic-judgment cycle. Days one and two you write the situation brief. A good outcome can include walking away. Day three you scan all eleven questions and circle two to four. Days four and five you build your hypothesis table. Day six you design the move and write a prediction you could be wrong about. Then act. In the debrief a clean prediction that came true teaches less than a surprise you learned from. Next is choosing your interaction.
