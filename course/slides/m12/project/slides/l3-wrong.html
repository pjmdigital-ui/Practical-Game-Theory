Internomics · Module 12 — Strategic Judgment

Everything we've done in this course depends on your interpretation of the game. And your interpretation can be wrong. You think the person cares about money. They care about status. You think they're angry. They're scared. You think they're bluffing. They're committed. You think the problem is free riding. It's actually confusion about ownership. You think everyone knows the rule. Half the group has a different understanding. You think an incentive will increase quality. People learn to game the metric instead. None of that means you were careless. It means you're human, working with incomplete information. So don't fall in love with your diagnosis. Test it.
