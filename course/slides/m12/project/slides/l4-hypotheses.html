Internomics · Module 12 — Strategic Judgment

Here are the five hypotheses. A price objection. They don't trust you yet. They need someone else's approval. They're improving their negotiating position. Or they've already chosen a competitor and they're being polite. If it's trust, talking about price is pointless. If it's approval, the person in front of you may not be the decision-maker. If it's negotiating position, a quick discount teaches them that resistance works. Your best move for one can be your worst move for another. Act in a way that teaches you the answer.
