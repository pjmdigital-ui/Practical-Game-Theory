Internomics · Module 12 — Strategic Judgment

You now have eleven lenses. The risk is that you try to use everything everywhere all at once. Strategic judgment is the skill that sits above the models. It has four parts. You choose the two or three lenses that matter right now. You know when a situation doesn't need analysis at all. You keep "Will this work?" apart from "Should I do it?" And you treat your read as provisional. Your first model will often be wrong. Let's look at what you'll be able to do by the end.
