Internomics · Module 12 — Strategic Judgment

You now have eleven lenses. And here's the honest truth: at this point, the risk isn't that you know too little. It's that you try to use everything, everywhere, all at once. The book is blunt about this. The eleven models are not eleven steps. Strategic judgment means choosing the two or three lenses that matter right now, knowing when a situation doesn't need analysis at all, keeping "Will this work?" separate from "Should I do it?", and treating your read as provisional. Because your first model will often be wrong. The person you thought cared about money cares about status. The person you thought was angry is scared. That's what this module is about.
