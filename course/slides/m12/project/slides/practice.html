Internomics · Module 12 — Strategic Judgment

Let's practice. Dana is the operations director at a regional logistics company. For the third quarter in a row, the sales team has promised clients delivery windows her warehouse can't meet, and her team absorbs the overtime and the angry calls. On Monday, the VP of Sales, Marcus, emails her and copies the CEO: "We just landed the Harlan account. They need three-day turnaround starting next month. This is non-negotiable. The CEO is aware." Dana's first impulse is to reply-all with a detailed list of every missed promise from the last nine months. A few more facts. Marcus's team is paid commission on signed contracts, not on retained or profitable accounts. Dana doesn't know whether the CEO actually approved three-day turnaround or was just told about the deal. She'll work with Marcus for years. Harlan would be good for the company if it could be served reliably. And two of her shift leads have privately told her they're close to quitting. Pause the video and work through the four questions.
