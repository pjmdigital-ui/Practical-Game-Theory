Internomics · Module 12 — Strategic Judgment

Here's your assignment this week. Run the loop five times. Choose five interactions that clear the bar for analysis. Remember the seven criteria. At least one should involve someone you'll deal with again. At least one should involve more than two people. For each one, start before you act. Take the pause between stimulus and response. Write your story. Write your confidence. Write the one or two lenses you're leading with. Then make a specific prediction. "If I do X, they'll do Y." Make it specific enough that you could be wrong. "It'll go fine" doesn't count. Then act. Then log in the Practice Log what actually happened. Facts first. Interpretation second. Then score your prediction. Right. Partly right. Or wrong. And fill in the Update and Adapt columns. There's one more piece and I don't want you to skip it. Record one ordinary interaction where you caught yourself starting to over-analyze. And you chose not to. That's judgment too. It deserves a line in the log. When you debrief next week, ask yourself three things. First, how many of your five predictions were right? In the ones that were wrong, was the error mostly about payoffs? Information? Credibility? The structure of the game? Second, which surprise taught you the most? How did you change your model because of it? Third, did any move tempt you toward something that would work but fail the ethics checks? What did you do instead? This week is also a warm-up.
