Internomics · Module 12 — Strategic Judgment

Here's your assignment this week: run the loop five times. Choose five interactions that clear the bar for analysis. At least one should involve someone you'll deal with again, and at least one should involve more than two people. For each one, before acting, take the pause between stimulus and response. Write your story, your confidence, and the one or two lenses you're leading with. Make a specific prediction: if I do X, they'll do Y. Act, then log what actually happened. Score your prediction: right, partly right, or wrong, and fill in the Update and Adapt columns. And record one ordinary interaction where you caught yourself starting to over-analyze and chose not to. When you debrief, ask: in the predictions you got wrong, was the error mostly about payoffs, information, credibility, or the structure of the game?
