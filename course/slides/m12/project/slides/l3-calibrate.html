Internomics · Module 12 — Strategic Judgment

People often talk about intuition as if it's almost mystical. I don't think it is. Sometimes it's simply patterns learned so deeply that we no longer experience the reasoning. An experienced salesperson notices "This objection isn't about price." Sales is where I learned about resistance and incentives long before I had names for them. But experience alone doesn't guarantee good intuition. You can repeat the wrong interpretation for twenty years. So intuition needs calibration. When did you last check your confidence about reading people against what actually happened? That's the takeaway for this lesson.
