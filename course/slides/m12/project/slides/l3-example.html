Internomics · Module 12 — Strategic Judgment

Here's the example from the book. You predict: "If I refuse this request, they'll become angry." So you refuse. And they say, "No problem. I completely understand." Good. You were wrong. That's valuable. Perhaps you overestimated their dependency. Perhaps you misunderstood their payoff. Perhaps you believed the relationship was more fragile than it was. Don't ignore the discrepancy because you want your model to be right. Update. Here's another. You think, "If I explain my reasoning clearly, they'll agree." You explain. They understand perfectly. They still refuse. That tells you something. Maybe this was never an information problem. Maybe your interests are genuinely opposed. Again: update the model.
