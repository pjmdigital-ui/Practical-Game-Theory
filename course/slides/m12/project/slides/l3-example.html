Example · The refused request

You predicted anger. You got understanding.

The situation

Your model: "If I refuse this request, they'll become angry." You refuse anyway.

What happened

They say: "No problem. I completely understand."

The lesson

Good. You were wrong. That's valuable. Don't ignore the gap because you want your model to be right.

Update: maybe you overestimated their dependency. Maybe you misread their payoff. Maybe the relationship was sturdier than you believed.

Internomics · Module 12 — Strategic Judgment

Here's the example from the book. The surprise is a happy one. And happy surprises are the easiest to ignore. Your model is "If I refuse this request, they'll become angry." So you've been saying yes for a while. Then you refuse. And they say "No problem. I completely understand." But this is exactly the moment the loop is built for. You were wrong. Good. Run the Update step. Perhaps you overestimated their dependency on you. Perhaps the relationship was less fragile than you believed. Next the surprise is less comfortable.
