Internomics · Module 12 — Strategic Judgment

Here are the questions as the book leaves them. If you remember nothing else from the course, remember these. See the Game asks what game I'm actually in. See the Payoffs asks what each player actually values. Think in Responses asks two words. Then what? It may be the one I use most. Manage Predictability is the child who asks again. See the Equilibrium is the same argument with your partner. You don't need these five every time. But often one is the key. Usually it's the one you skipped. Let's look at the other six.
