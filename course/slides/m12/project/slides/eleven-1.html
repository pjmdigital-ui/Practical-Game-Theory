Internomics · Module 12 — Strategic Judgment

Before we go to the practice, let's look back at the whole course. In Module Zero I showed you this table with the questions in their simplest form. Here they are as the book leaves them. If you remember nothing else from the course, remember these. Part One was about seeing the structure beneath the conversation. One is See the Game. What game am I actually in? Who are the players? What can they do? What are the outside options? Two is See the Payoffs. What does each player actually value? Not what they say they value. What they actually value. Three is Think in Responses. The question is just two words. Then what? It's the shortest question in the course and maybe the one I use most. Four is Manage Predictability. What should they be able to predict about me? What becomes exploitable if they can? Remember the child who asks again. Five is See the Equilibrium. What pattern are we producing? Why is it stable? That's the same argument with your partner. It's the team that fails the same way every quarter. Here's the thing I want you to notice now that you've been through all of it. You don't need these five every time. But you'll be surprised how often one of them is the key. Usually it's the one you skipped. So here's a question for you. Of these first five, which one do you most often forget to ask?
