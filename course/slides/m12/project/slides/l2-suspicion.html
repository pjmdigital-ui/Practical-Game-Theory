Strategic is not the same as suspicious

Suspicion asks

"What are they hiding?"

Strategic thinking asks

"What possibilities should I consider?"

Become less naive without becoming cynical.

Internomics · Module 12 — Strategic Judgment

This deserves emphasis, because the line can get blurry. Suspicion asks, "What are they hiding?" Strategic thinking asks, "What possibilities should I consider?" Suspicion assumes hostile intent. Strategic thinking tests hypotheses. Suspicion interprets ambiguity negatively. Strategic thinking simply recognizes that it's ambiguous. Suspicion makes trust difficult. Strategic thinking understands why trust can be enormously valuable. So if you notice that this course has made you more guarded, more cynical, quicker to assume the worst, that's a sign the tool is being misused. The framework should make you less naive. It should not make you cynical.
