Internomics · Module 12 — Strategic Judgment

Thinking strategically is not the same as thinking suspiciously. Suspicion asks "What are they hiding?" Strategic thinking asks "What possibilities should I consider?" Suspicion assumes hostile intent. Strategic thinking tests hypotheses. Suspicion reads ambiguity negatively. Strategic thinking says "I don't know yet." A suspicious person often destroys the very trust that would have protected them. If this course has made you more guarded or more cynical, something has gone wrong.
