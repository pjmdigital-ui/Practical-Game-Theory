Internomics · Module 12 — Strategic Judgment

So what does recognition actually sound like? It sounds like short internal prompts. Someone makes an aggressive demand, and you think: what payoff are they protecting? Someone makes a dramatic threat, and you think: would they actually want to carry that out? A team keeps failing in the same way, and you think: this is a pattern, not an incident. Someone tells you something that strongly benefits them: what makes that credible? Two people appear to be fighting, and you suddenly realize they're not actually opposed. They just don't know what the other is going to do. And a company keeps trying to motivate people, and you notice the incentive system rewards the exact opposite behavior. That's the goal.
