Internomics · Module 12 — Strategic Judgment

So what does recognition actually sound like? It sounds like short internal prompts. Each one is a lens firing on its own. Someone makes an aggressive demand. You think: what payoff are they protecting? That's See the Payoffs. Someone makes a dramatic threat. You think: would they actually want to carry that out? That's the credibility of a threat. A team keeps failing in the same way. You think: this is a pattern and not an incident. That's See the Equilibrium. Someone tells you something that strongly benefits them. You think: what makes that credible? Two people appear to be fighting. Then you suddenly realize they're not actually opposed. They just don't know what the other person is going to do. That's a coordination problem hiding inside what looks like conflict. And a company keeps trying to motivate people. You notice the incentive system rewards the exact opposite behavior. That's Change the Game. Notice how short these are. Nobody is filling out a worksheet in the middle of a meeting. It's one sentence that changes how you see the next five minutes. That's the goal. Not recitation. Recognition. Here's a question for you. Look at these six cards. Which one has already happened to you since you started this course? Which prompt came to you without being called? If none of them has yet, that's fine. They come with practice. Next I want to show you the real test of whether a lens has become yours.
