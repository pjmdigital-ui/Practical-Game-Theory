Internomics · Module 12 — Strategic Judgment

Recognition sounds like short internal prompts. Each one is a lens firing on its own. Someone makes an aggressive demand. You think: what payoff are they protecting? Someone makes a dramatic threat. You think: would they actually want to carry that out? A team keeps failing in the same way. You think: this is a pattern and not an incident. Nobody is filling out a worksheet in the middle of a meeting. Which one has already happened to you since you started this course? Next is the real test of whether a lens has become yours.
