Internomics · Module 12 — Strategic Judgment

Words like players and payoffs can make human interactions sound mechanical. They're not. People love. People forgive. People care about fairness. People sometimes do the right thing when it clearly costs them. A model that ignores those realities isn't sophisticated. It's incomplete. So why say "player" at all? It reminds you they are a decision-maker too. They have agency. They see the world differently. That should make us more respectful of their agency. Not less. Who have you been treating more like a piece than a player? Next are two moves people forget.
