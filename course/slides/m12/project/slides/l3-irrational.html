History

Don't use the word "irrational" as a substitute for thinking.

Internomics · Module 12 — Strategic Judgment

One of the easiest ways to stop learning is to say "They're irrational." It's a door closing. Before reaching for that word, ask one question. What would have to be true for this behavior to make sense? The book gives six possibilities. Maybe they have information you don't. Maybe they care about a different payoff. You assumed money. They're protecting their standing with their team. And yes, maybe they're simply making a mistake. Think of someone you've written off as irrational. Does any of these six make the behavior make sense? Now a concrete example.
