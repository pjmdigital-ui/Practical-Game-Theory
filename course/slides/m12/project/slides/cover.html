Internomics · Module 12

Strategic Judgment

What did I learn from what just happened?

Paul Mascetta

Welcome to Module Twelve, the final module of Internomics. Over the last eleven modules we've built eleven lenses, from See the Game all the way to Change the Game. This module isn't a twelfth lens. It's the skill that sits above all of them: strategic judgment. Knowing which lens matters right now, knowing when to put the lenses down, keeping your read of any situation provisional, and keeping your ethics in the room. And then we finish with the Capstone, where you take one real interaction from your own life through the whole framework. Let's bring it all together.
