Internomics · Module 12

Strategic Judgment

What did I learn from what just happened?

Paul Mascetta

Welcome to Module Twelve. This is the final module of Internomics. Over eleven modules we've built eleven lenses. We started with See the Game. We ended with Change the Game. This module isn't a twelfth lens. It's the skill that sits above all of them. I call it strategic judgment. It means knowing which lens matters right now. It means knowing when to put the lenses down. It means keeping your read of a situation provisional. And it means keeping your ethics in the room. Then we finish with the Capstone. You'll take one real interaction from your own life through the whole framework. Let's bring it all together.
