Example · The clear explanation

They understood perfectly. They still said no.

The situation

Your model: "If I explain my reasoning clearly, they'll agree." So you explain.

What happened

They understand perfectly. They can restate your case. They still refuse.

The lesson

Maybe this was never an information problem. Maybe your interests are genuinely opposed.

Explaining it again more slowly won't fix a payoff problem.

Internomics · Module 12 — Strategic Judgment

Here's the second example from the book. You think "If I explain my reasoning clearly, they'll agree." That model assumes disagreement comes from missing information. So you explain. They understand perfectly. And they still refuse. That surprise is information. Maybe your interests are genuinely opposed. In an information problem a better explanation helps. In a conflict of interests it does almost nothing. This is where people get stuck. They explain again more slowly. Then louder. Then they decide the other person is irrational. Is there someone you keep explaining things to who already understands? Next is how intuition fits in.
