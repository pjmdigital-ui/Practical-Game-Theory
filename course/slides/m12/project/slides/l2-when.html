Internomics · Module 12 — Strategic Judgment

There's a danger in a course like this. Once you learn to see games everywhere, you can start overanalyzing everything. The purpose of this framework isn't to turn human life into chess. It's to help when the stakes matter, when something doesn't make sense, when a pattern keeps repeating, when incentives are unclear, when trust is uncertain, when multiple people affect the outcome, or when your normal way of thinking just isn't working. Those are the moments to slow down and pick up the lenses. The rest of the time, put them down. Strategic thinking should reduce confusion. If your analysis is making ordinary life needlessly suspicious and complicated, you're using the tool badly.
