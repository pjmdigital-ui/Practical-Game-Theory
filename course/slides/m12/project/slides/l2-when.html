Internomics · Module 12 — Strategic Judgment

Once you learn to see games everywhere, you can start overanalyzing everything. The book lists seven moments when the framework helps. High stakes. Something doesn't make sense. A repeating pattern. Unclear incentives. Uncertain trust. Multiple people affecting the outcome. Or your normal way of thinking isn't working. The rest of the time put the lenses down. Strategic thinking should reduce confusion. Here's the test I use. Is my analysis making this clearer or murkier? How many of your interactions last week actually met one of these?
