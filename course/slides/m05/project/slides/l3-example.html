Internomics · Module 5 — See the Equilibrium

Here's how it happens to a real person. A new employee joins a company where everyone stays late. At first they think, "Why is everyone still here?" After a few weeks: "I guess this is normal." After a few months: "If I leave at five, people will think I'm not committed." And now look what's happened. Their own behavior reinforces the very norm they questioned when they walked in. Nobody wrote the rule. Nobody told them to stay. They're responding to what they expect everyone else to think, and in doing so they become part of the reason the next new hire stays late too. The equilibrium has become cultural. That's one reason social norms can be so powerful.
