Internomics · Module 5 — See the Equilibrium

A new employee joins a company where everyone stays late. Nobody hands them a rule. At first they think "Why is everyone still here?" After a few weeks they think "I guess this is normal." After a few months they think "If I leave at five, people will think I'm not committed." Now their own behavior reinforces the very norm they questioned. They're part of the reason the next new hire will stay late too. And that keeps the expectation true. The equilibrium has become cultural.
