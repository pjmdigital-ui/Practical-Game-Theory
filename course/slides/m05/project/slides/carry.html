The question to carry

What equilibrium have we created?

Internomics · Next: Module 6 — Map Information & Beliefs

The next time you find yourself stuck in a recurring conflict, ask: what equilibrium have we created? Then ask: why is it stable? What does each of us get from maintaining it? What makes changing it risky? Are there other equilibria available? And finally, what would have to change for us to move toward one of them? Because sometimes the problem isn't that you haven't found the right words. Sometimes you've both become very good at playing the same bad game. And if that's true, the answer isn't necessarily a better next move. It may be to change the pattern. Next time, we go somewhere new: if the game depends on what people believe, what happens when you don't know what the other person knows, or what they believe about you? That's Module 6.
