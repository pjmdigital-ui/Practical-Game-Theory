Internomics · Module 5 — See the Equilibrium

So how do you break an equilibrium? If a pattern is self-reinforcing, simply changing your intention may not be enough. You have to change something that affects the strategic structure. Maybe your response changes. Maybe the sequence changes: who moves first, and when. Maybe the incentives change. Maybe new information appears. Maybe someone makes a commitment. Maybe a third party gets involved. Maybe expectations change. Maybe the available choices change. Notice how many of those will become later modules. That's intentional. Equilibrium is the bridge between the individual models. It explains why the pieces we've already learned, the game, the payoffs, the responses, the predictability, can lock together into patterns that persist.
