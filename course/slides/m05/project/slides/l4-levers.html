Internomics · Module 5 — See the Equilibrium

So how do you break an equilibrium? If a pattern is self-reinforcing, changing your intention may not be enough. You have to change something that affects the strategic structure. These eight boxes are your levers. Ask what is holding the pattern in place right now. If it's fear of going first, look at sequence or commitment. If it's a misread, look at information. Remember the two-hour meeting? Good intentions didn't touch that. A lever might. Which lever have you never tried? Most of us only pull the first one. Now notice that many of these levers will become later modules.
