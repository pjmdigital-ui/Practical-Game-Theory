I want to name the mental shift this module asks of you. Up to now you've learned to see moves. Now I want you to see patterns. The left column points at one person and one moment. The right column points at the space between you. Here's a quick example from the book. You ask for help. They say they're too busy. Stay in the left column and you'll argue about who's right.
