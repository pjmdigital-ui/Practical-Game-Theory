Internomics · Module 5 — See the Equilibrium

Sometimes the most powerful equilibria are invisible. Think about workplace meetings. In private everyone complains that the meetings are too long. Yet every meeting lasts two hours. The manager keeps scheduling them. Nobody wants to be the person who says "I'm leaving after 30 minutes." Nobody chose it. Everyone keeps it alive. The misunderstanding to watch for is that if everyone dislikes something it will naturally go away. It won't. Shared dislike isn't enough. Someone has to change something about the structure. Maybe the expectations or the sequence or the available choices. Next, how these patterns turn into habits.
