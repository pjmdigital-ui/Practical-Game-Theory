Internomics · Module 5 — See the Equilibrium

Information. Commitment. Expectations. Third parties. Each becomes a later module. Equilibrium is the bridge between the individual models. In See the Game you looked at the players and their options. In See the Payoffs you asked what each player values. In Think in Responses you learned that your move changes the situation the other person responds to. Equilibrium is what happens when they all run at once over time. Here's the misunderstanding to avoid. Don't treat the models as a checklist you run once. A change in one piece can shift the whole pattern. Now an example of what doesn't work and why.
