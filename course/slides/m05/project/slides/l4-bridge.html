Internomics · Module 5 — See the Equilibrium

Let me step back and show you where this module fits. Notice how many of those levers will become later modules. Information. Commitment. Expectations. Third parties. That's intentional. Equilibrium is the bridge between the individual models. Look at the three cards. In See the Game you learned to look at the players. You looked at their options and constraints. You looked at their information and timing and alternatives. In See the Payoffs you learned to ask what each player actually values. In Think in Responses you learned that your move changes the situation the other person is responding to. Each of those was a separate lens. Equilibrium is what happens when they all run at once over time. The game sets the options. The payoffs make some options more attractive. The responses feed back into each other. And after enough rounds the whole thing locks into a pattern that persists. That's why this concept sits right here in the middle of the course. It explains why the pieces we've already learned can lock together. And it points to where we're going next. Every later module gives you another way to change the structure. Here's the misunderstanding I want you to avoid. Don't treat the models as a checklist you run once. They interact. A change in one piece can shift the whole pattern. That's good news. It means you have more than one way in. So think about your own stuck situation. Which piece do you understand best? The game, the payoffs or the responses? Which one have you ignored?
