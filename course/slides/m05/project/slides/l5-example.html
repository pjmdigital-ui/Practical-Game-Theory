Internomics · Module 5 — See the Equilibrium

Thomas Schelling illustrated coordination with a simple thought experiment. You've agreed to meet someone in New York City tomorrow. You haven't agreed on the place or the time. And you can't call them. Where do you go? A famous result of Schelling's experiments was that people frequently converged on noon at Grand Central Station. Nothing about those choices was mathematically unique. They simply stood out. That's a focal point. It helps people line up their expectations without explicit communication.
