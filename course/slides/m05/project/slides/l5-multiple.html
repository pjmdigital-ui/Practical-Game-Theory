Internomics · Module 5 — See the Equilibrium

Some games have more than one equilibrium. Imagine two people trying to coordinate. They can both choose Option A or both choose Option B. Each person's best response is simply to match the other. If both choose A, neither gains by switching alone. If both choose B, that's just as stable. So you have two equilibria. A is fine. B is fine. The problem is getting to the same one. That's called a coordination problem. When two people keep missing each other, we assume one is choosing wrong. Sometimes they're just aiming at different equilibria.
