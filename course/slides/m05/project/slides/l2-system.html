Internomics · Module 5 — See the Equilibrium

In Module 3 you learned to ask what they will do after you move. Now we ask something deeper. What pattern are our responses creating? On the left is one interaction. I push and you resist. On the right it happens again and again. I push harder. You resist harder. We're looking at a system. Look at the system and you see the push and the resistance feed each other.
