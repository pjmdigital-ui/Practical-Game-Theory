Internomics · Module 5 — See the Equilibrium

This is a major step up from Module 3. Back there you learned to ask one question. What will they do after I move? That was a big step. It got you thinking one move ahead. Now we ask something deeper. What pattern are our responses creating? That's a different level of analysis. Look at the left side of the slide. One interaction might look like this. I push and you resist. On its own that's just a move and a response. Now look at the right side. If it happens again and again, it becomes something else. I push. You resist. I push harder. You resist harder. I push harder still. Now we're not looking at individual decisions anymore. We're looking at a system. And systems can develop stable patterns. Here's why that matters so much. When you look at a single move, you'll naturally try to fix the move. You'll look for better words. When you look at the system, you start to see that the words were never the whole problem. The push and the resistance feed each other. Each one makes the other feel necessary. A common misunderstanding is to think that seeing the system means letting people off the hook. It doesn't. It means you finally see what you're both inside of. So here's a question for you. Pick a recurring tension in your life. Can you describe it as a chain instead of a single event? What's the first push? What's the resistance? What happens next? Let's look at how this changes the questions we ask.
