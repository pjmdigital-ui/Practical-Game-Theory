From a move to a system

Once

I push → you resist.

Repeated

I push → you resist → I push harder → you resist harder.

Not "What will they do after I move?" but "What pattern are our responses creating?"

Internomics · Module 5 — See the Equilibrium

This is a major step up from Module 3. There, we asked: what will they do after I move? Now we ask something deeper: what pattern are our responses creating? One interaction might look like "I push, you resist." But if it happens again and again, it becomes "I push, you resist, I push harder, you resist harder, I push harder still." Now we're not looking at individual decisions anymore. We're looking at a system. And systems can develop stable patterns. Most communication advice, when you keep having the same argument, asks: "What should I say differently?" That's not wrong. But game theory hands you another question, and it's the one this module is named for: "What equilibrium have we created?"
