Internomics · Module 5 — See the Equilibrium

This is the heart of the whole idea. Equilibrium is about unilateral change. That means one person changing while everyone else keeps doing what they were doing. Suppose we're in a stable pattern. If I change while you don't, I end up worse off. So neither of us moves alone. None of this says whether the pattern is any good. It might be harmful or unfair or merely acceptable. Here's the misunderstanding. Having no reason to move doesn't mean not wanting change. You can want change badly and still see no safe way to go first. That brings us to some advice that often backfires.
