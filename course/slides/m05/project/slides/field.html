Internomics · Module 5 — See the Equilibrium

Here's your assignment this week: catch one equilibrium in the wild and run one experiment. For the first three days, just observe. Take your situation from Exercise B, or a new one, and each time it happens, jot down the loop: their move, your response, their response. Don't fix anything yet. Notice when you reacted to what you expected them to do rather than what they actually did. On day four, update your Equilibrium Audit, including the equilibrium check. Then on days five through seven, pick one lever and try one small, honest change, preferably one that doesn't leave the other person carrying all the risk of going first. For example, name the pattern without blame: "I think we're in a loop where I ask late and you say no, and we both end up frustrated. Could we try a weekly check-in?" Don't use this to corner anyone. The aim is a better pattern both of you would choose freely. Then record what happened.
