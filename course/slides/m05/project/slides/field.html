Internomics · Module 5 — See the Equilibrium

Here's your assignment. Catch one equilibrium in the wild and run one experiment. For the first three days just observe. Each time it happens jot down the loop. Their move. Your response. Their response. Don't fix anything yet. On day four update your Equilibrium Audit. On days five through seven pick one lever and try one small and honest change. If you can, choose one that doesn't leave the other person carrying all the risk of going first. Then record what kept the pattern stable. Next week we'll debrief. Did your experiment change their expectations? Now the question to carry.
