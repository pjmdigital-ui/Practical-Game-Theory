Internomics · Module 5 — See the Equilibrium

Here's your assignment this week. Catch one equilibrium in the wild and run one experiment. For the first three days just observe. Take your situation from Exercise B or pick a new one. Each time it happens, jot down the loop. Their move. Your response. Their response. Don't fix anything yet. Notice when you reacted to what you expected them to do rather than what they actually did. On day four update your Equilibrium Audit from your notes. Include the equilibrium check. Then on days five through seven pick one lever. Try one small and honest change. If you can, choose one that doesn't leave the other person carrying all the risk of going first. For example, you could name the pattern without blame. "I think we're in a loop where I ask late and you say no. We both end up frustrated. Could we try a weekly check-in?" Don't use this to corner or pressure anyone. The aim is a better pattern both of you would choose freely. Then record what happened. What did you learn about what keeps the pattern stable? Next week we'll debrief. When you mapped the loop, what did you see that you hadn't seen before? What made changing alone risky for you? And as best you could tell, what made it risky for them? Did your experiment change their expectations? If not, what would you change about the structure next time?
