Internomics · Module 5 — See the Equilibrium

Your tool for this module is the Equilibrium Audit. Use it when you're stuck in a recurring interaction, and do it in writing, at a calm moment. First, what keeps happening? Forget the individual incident and write the pattern as a loop: my move, their response, my response. What does my behavior cause them to do? What does their behavior cause me to do? Why might neither of us want to change on our own: what would I risk, and what would they risk? Is this pattern actually good for either of us: what does each of us get from it, and what does it cost? Are there other patterns that could hold steady? Then the equilibrium check: is this really held in place by the cost of changing alone, or by habit, a rule, coercion, or lack of awareness? And the most important question: what would have to change for a different pattern to become attractive? Pick a lever and design your first small experiment.
