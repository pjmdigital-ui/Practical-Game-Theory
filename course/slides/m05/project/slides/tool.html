Internomics · Module 5 — See the Equilibrium

Your tool is the Equilibrium Audit in your workbook. Do it in writing at a calm moment. Question one asks what keeps happening. Write it as a loop. Then question four asks why neither of you would change alone. Five asks whether the pattern is good for either of you. Seven is the equilibrium check. Is it the cost of changing alone or just habit? Eight is the most important. What would have to change for a different pattern to become attractive? The common mistake is stopping after question three. Let's practice on a scenario.
