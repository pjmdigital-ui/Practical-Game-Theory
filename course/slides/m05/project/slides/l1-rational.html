Internomics · Module 5 — See the Equilibrium

This is one of the most important ideas in the book. You can be rational and still produce a bad outcome. When something goes wrong between people, we usually say someone must be acting irrationally. Sometimes that's true. But game theory adds something. Bad collective outcomes can emerge from individually rational responses. Nobody had to be foolish. So instead of asking only who's behaving badly, ask what interaction is producing this pattern. When you only hunt for the villain, the best you can do is win an argument about blame.
