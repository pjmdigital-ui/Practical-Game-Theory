Internomics · Module 5 — See the Equilibrium

Here's an important distinction. An agreement is something people explicitly decide. An equilibrium doesn't require an agreement at all. Two people can arrive at a stable pattern without ever discussing it. Think about your own relationships. You know who speaks first, who pays, who makes the decision, who apologizes, who gives in, who gets interrupted, who gets the last word. Nobody may have negotiated any of that. Yet everyone understands it. It's part of the game. And some equilibria are invisible. Everyone complains privately that meetings are too long, yet every meeting lasts two hours. The manager keeps scheduling them, employees keep attending, and nobody wants to be the person who says, "I'm leaving after 30 minutes." So the pattern continues.
