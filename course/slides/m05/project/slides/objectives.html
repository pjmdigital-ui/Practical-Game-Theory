Internomics · Module 5 — See the Equilibrium

Here's what you'll be able to do by the end of this module. First, you'll be able to say in plain language what a Nash equilibrium is. You'll also see why stable is not the same as good or fair. Second, you'll walk through the Prisoner's Dilemma. You'll see how two sensible players end up somewhere neither one wants. Third, you'll learn to map a recurring interaction as a loop of responses. No more string of separate incidents. Fourth, you'll understand why "just change" is often bad advice. And you'll know what has to change instead. Fifth, you'll learn to hold all of this honestly. Equilibrium is a lens for seeing patterns. It is not a crystal ball. Let's start with what the word actually means.
