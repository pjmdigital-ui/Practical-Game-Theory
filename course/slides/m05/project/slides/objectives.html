Internomics · Module 5 — See the Equilibrium

Here's what you'll be able to do by the end of this module. You'll be able to say in plain language what a Nash equilibrium is, and why stable is not the same as good, fair, or optimal. You'll walk through the Prisoner's Dilemma and see how two perfectly sensible players end up somewhere neither of them wants. You'll learn to map a recurring interaction as a loop of responses instead of a string of separate incidents. You'll understand why "just change" is often bad advice, and what you have to change instead. And you'll learn to hold all of this honestly: as a lens for seeing patterns, not a crystal ball.
