Example · "Can you help me with this?"

Who's right? Wrong question.

The situation

A request turns into a debate about busyness and planning. Every line answers the last one.

What happened

You ask late → they refuse → you criticize → they defend → you feel unsupported → you ask late again.

The lesson

Skip who's right. Ask "What pattern have we created?"

The interaction itself is producing the outcome.

Internomics · Module 5 — See the Equilibrium

Who's right is not the most useful question. Instead ask what pattern you've created. Step back and the loop looks like the middle card. You ask late. They refuse. You criticize. They defend. You feel unsupported. So you hold off asking until you really need help. Which means you ask late again. Neither of you is the cause on your own. The interaction itself is producing the outcome. And now you can see places to interrupt it. The ask could come earlier. You don't need to win the argument to change the outcome. Where does this loop close for you?
