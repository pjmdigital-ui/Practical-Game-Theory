Most advice stops at "What should I say differently?" That's not wrong. Game theory adds: "What equilibrium have we created?"

Internomics · Module 5 — See the Equilibrium

Let me show you how far we've come. First you asked what you should say. That's the level of words. Then you asked what they will do if you say it. That's responses. Now we ask what pattern our responses are creating. And eventually we'll ask how the pattern could change. That's systems. Better words can help. But if you only work at the level of words, you can polish every sentence and still land in the same place. So game theory asks something else. What equilibrium have we created? Which level do you usually work at? Now let's see how common these patterns really are.
