Internomics · Module 5 — See the Equilibrium

Let me show you how far we've come. Earlier in this course you asked a simple question. What should I say? That's the level of words. Then you learned to ask what they will do if you say it. That's the level of responses. Now we add a third question. What pattern are our responses creating? That's the level of patterns. And eventually we'll ask a fourth. How could the pattern change? That's the level of systems. Follow the arrows on the slide from left to right. That progression is the heart of the whole book. We're moving from words to responses to patterns to systems. Here's why this matters in practice. Suppose you keep having the same argument with someone. Most communication advice will ask what you should say differently. And that's not necessarily wrong. Better words can help. But game theory gives you another question to ask. What equilibrium have we created? That question is the name of this module. It's the one I want you to carry out of here. The misunderstanding to watch for is thinking you have to pick one level. You don't. Good words still matter. Predicting responses still matters. But if you only work at the level of words, you can polish every sentence and still land in the same place every time. The pattern will pull your better words right back into the old loop. So ask yourself which level you usually work at. Most of us live in the first box. This module moves you to the third. Now let's see how common these patterns really are.
