Practice · Exercise A

Priya and Marcus

Priya and Marcus co-lead a product team. Each Friday Priya emails Marcus a long list of risks in his area. He replies "Noted, thanks" and handles things without updating her. Hearing nothing, she writes longer and copies their director. He feels attacked. He shares less and reports to the director himself. He once skipped that update "to be the bigger person." The director asked why he'd gone quiet.

Pause the video and answer:

  1. Describe the pattern as a loop of mutual responses.
  2. Why does each person's behavior make sense given the other's?
  3. Why did Marcus's unilateral change backfire?
  4. Is this a formal Nash equilibrium? How would you describe it honestly?
  5. What would have to change for a better pattern to attract both?
