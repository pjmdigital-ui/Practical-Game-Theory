Priya and Marcus

Priya and Marcus co-lead a product team. Each Friday Priya emails Marcus a long list of risks in his area. He replies "Noted, thanks" and handles things without updating her. Hearing nothing, she writes longer and copies their director. He feels attacked. He shares less and reports to the director himself. He once skipped that update "to be the bigger person." The director asked why he'd gone quiet.

Pause the video and answer:

  1. Describe the pattern as a loop of mutual responses.
  2. Why does each person's behavior make sense given the other's?
  3. Why did Marcus's unilateral change backfire?
  4. Is this a formal Nash equilibrium? How would you describe it honestly?
  5. What would have to change for a better pattern to attract both?

Internomics · Module 5 — See the Equilibrium

Let's practice. Here's the scenario. Priya and Marcus co-lead a product team. Every Friday Priya sends Marcus a long status email. It flags risks she sees in his part of the project. Marcus has started replying with a short "Noted, thanks." Then he handles the issues on his own without updating her. Because she hears nothing, Priya assumes the risks aren't being handled. So next Friday's email is longer. And this time she copies their director. Marcus reads the cc as Priya trying to make him look bad. So he shares even less. He starts sending his own updates straight to the director. Both of them say they want a real partnership. Both of them say the other one started it. Here's the last piece. Last month Marcus tried something. He skipped his separate update to the director to be the bigger person. And the director asked him in a meeting why he'd gone quiet. That embarrassed him. Now here's what I want you to do. Pause the video. Work through the five questions on the screen. Write your answers down. Don't just think them. First describe the pattern as a loop. Then explain why each person's behavior makes sense given the other's. Then ask why Marcus's change backfired. Then decide whether this is a formal Nash equilibrium and how you'd describe it honestly. And finally ask what would have to change for a better pattern to attract both of them. Here's a hint. Resist the urge to pick a villain. Both of them will look reasonable if you read them closely. When you're ready, unpause and I'll walk you through my answer.
