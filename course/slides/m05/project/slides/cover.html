See the Equilibrium

What equilibrium have we created?

Paul Mascetta

Welcome to Module 5. So far we've built three pieces of the framework. See the Game is about the players and their options. See the Payoffs is about what each player actually values. Think in Responses is about how your move changes the situation the other person responds to. Now we're ready for one of the most important ideas in all of game theory. It's called equilibrium. This is where game theory gets really interesting for understanding people. Sometimes people aren't doing what they want. They're doing what makes sense given what everyone else is doing. Let's find out what that means for you.
