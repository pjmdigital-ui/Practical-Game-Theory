Equilibrium traps are everywhere

Rival companies

"If we don't lower our prices, they'll undercut us." Both cut. Both make less money. Neither wants to raise prices first.

Two coworkers

"If I stop this extra work, they'll take advantage of me." Both keep working unsustainable hours.

A couple

One feels unappreciated and complains. The other feels criticized and withdraws. Each fuels the other.

The pattern persists because each response makes sense given the other's. Self-reinforcing doesn't mean healthy.

Internomics · Module 5 — See the Equilibrium

Once you see this, you see it everywhere. I call these equilibrium traps. Two competing companies each fear being undercut. So both lower their prices and both make less money. Two coworkers each fear the other will take advantage. So both keep working unsustainable hours. And a married couple. One partner feels unappreciated and complains. The other feels criticized and withdraws. The withdrawal causes more complaining. Each behavior is a response to the other. They've created a loop that can become stable.
