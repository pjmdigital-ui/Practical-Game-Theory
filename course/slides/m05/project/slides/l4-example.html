Example · Waiting for senior management

"Why doesn't anyone take initiative?"

The situation

Everyone waits for senior management before making any decision.

What happens

Management: "Why doesn't anyone take initiative?" Employees: "Because whenever we do, management overrides us."

The lesson

Each side's behavior reinforces the other's. That's an equilibrium.

"Take more initiative" doesn't touch what holds the pattern in place.

Internomics · Module 5 — See the Equilibrium

Here's an example from the book. In one company everyone waits for senior management before making any decision. Management complains that nobody takes initiative. The employees answer that whenever they do, management overrides them. Management overrides so employees wait. Employees wait so management steps in. That's an equilibrium. Now imagine management's fix is to tell everyone to take more initiative. Probably not much changes. An employee who goes first alone still risks being overridden. So look back at the eight levers.
