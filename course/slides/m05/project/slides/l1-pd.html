The Prisoner's Dilemma: cooperate or defect

Both cooperate

A relatively good outcome for both

Only I defect

I do especially well. They do badly.

Only they defect

They do especially well. I do badly.

Both defect

Both worse off than mutual cooperation

Whatever the other does, defecting looks like each player's best response. They know cooperation is better. The structure pushes them apart anyway.

Internomics · Module 5 — See the Equilibrium

Two people each choose to cooperate or defect. If both cooperate they get a relatively good outcome. If only one defects, the defector does especially well. If both defect they both do worse than under mutual cooperation. Now put yourself in one player's shoes. If the other person cooperates, I'd be better off defecting. If they defect, I'd better defect too. So both defect. But both would rather cooperate. The problem is the structure of the game. Where have you seen two smart people both choose the safe move and both end up worse off? Next, what this says about rationality.
