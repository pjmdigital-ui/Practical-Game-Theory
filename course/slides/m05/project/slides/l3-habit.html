Internomics · Module 5 — See the Equilibrium

Once a pattern develops, everyone's expectations adjust to it. Picture two people who keep misunderstanding each other. You say "We need to talk." They think "Here comes another criticism." So they become defensive. You read that as proof they won't listen. So you push harder. People don't merely respond to incentives. They respond to what they expect the other person to do.
