Internomics · Module 5 — See the Equilibrium

A Nash equilibrium tells us something specific. If everyone is behaving this way, no one wants to deviate on their own. It doesn't tell you that this is exactly what real people will do. People make mistakes. They have limited information. And they learn over time. The book points to experimental work showing actual behavior can differ systematically from Nash predictions. So we use equilibrium as a mental model and not a crystal ball. Here's the misunderstanding to watch for. People say "He'll never change. It's an equilibrium." That's using the lens as a prophecy.
