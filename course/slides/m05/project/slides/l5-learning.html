Internomics · Module 5 — See the Equilibrium

Suppose you and I play a game again and again. We experiment. We observe. We adjust. Eventually we may settle into a stable pattern. We found our way into it one round at a time. The book points to experimental evidence that learning can produce convergence toward Nash equilibria under certain conditions. But convergence isn't universal. Think back to "We need to talk" from Lesson 3. That pattern was learned. And if a pattern was learned it can be relearned. What experience would it take to learn your way out of one of yours?
