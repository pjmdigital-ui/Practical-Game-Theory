Internomics · Module 5 — See the Equilibrium

This may be the most important thing to understand about equilibrium. Stable doesn't mean good. Equilibrium does not mean everyone is happy. It doesn't mean anyone got the best outcome. In everyday language we hear balance or harmony. In game theory it means one thing. The pattern is stable because changing alone doesn't look attractive. An equilibrium can be terrible for everyone involved. Here's the misunderstanding to watch for. When a pattern lasts a long time we assume somebody must want it. Often it lasts because each person is responding sensibly to the other. The classic example is the Prisoner's Dilemma.
