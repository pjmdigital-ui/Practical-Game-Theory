Why equilibrium matters in communication

Three loops worth recognizing

Pursue

You pursue → they withdraw → you pursue harder → they withdraw further

Criticize

They criticize → you defend → they read your defense as proof → you defend harder

Concede

You ask for a concession → they make one → you ask again → they resist → you push harder

Each move makes sense locally. The overall pattern is terrible.

Internomics · Module 5 — See the Equilibrium

Here are three loops from the book. The first is pursue and withdraw. You pursue. They withdraw. So you pursue harder and they withdraw further. The second is criticize and defend. They take your defense as proof they were right. So you defend harder. The third is the concession loop. You ask for one concession and then another. They resist. So you push harder. Any single step makes sense. But the overall pattern is terrible. The common mistake is trying to win one step of the loop. Winning a step usually just feeds the next round.
