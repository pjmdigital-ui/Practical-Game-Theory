Internomics · Module 5 — See the Equilibrium

Four more. A coordination problem is the challenge of getting everyone to the same equilibrium when several are possible. A focal point is something that stands out and helps people coordinate their expectations without explicit communication, like noon at Grand Central. The equilibrium-selection problem is knowing the possible stable outcomes without knowing which one people will actually land on. And the equilibrium lens is what we'll use most: equilibrium thinking applied to an everyday pattern, asking why it persists, without claiming it's a formally proven Nash equilibrium.
