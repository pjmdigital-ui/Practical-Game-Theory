Internomics · Module 5 — See the Equilibrium

Imagine two people who have been arguing for months. Neither likes it. Both would prefer things to be better. But neither changes. Why? Because each person's behavior is partly a response to the other's. You stop calling because "they never call me." They don't call because "I always have to be the one who reaches out." You get defensive because they're always criticizing you. They criticize because you never listen unless they push. And around and around you go. Nobody sat down and decided to create a miserable relationship. Yet you've arrived at a stable pattern. That's the basic intuition behind equilibrium. And here's the most important thing in this module: stable doesn't mean good. Sometimes the problem isn't that you haven't found the right words. Sometimes you've both become very good at playing the same bad game.
