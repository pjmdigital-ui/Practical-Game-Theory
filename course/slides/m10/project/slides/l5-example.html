Internomics · Module 10 — Diagnose the Coordination Problem

Let's run the test on the roommates. Everyone wants the apartment clean. But if three roommates are definitely going to clean it, the fourth may think: great, then I don't have to. That isn't a failure to coordinate. They coordinated just fine. Everyone knew exactly what was happening. The individual incentive is the problem. Same desired group outcome, different strategic problem. And that's why the clearest chore chart in the world might not fix this house. It needs something that changes the incentive or the accountability, not just the clarity.
