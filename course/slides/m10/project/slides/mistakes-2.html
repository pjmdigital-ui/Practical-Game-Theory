Internomics · Module 10 — Diagnose the Coordination Problem

Fifth, telling people privately what needs to be public. That's Dana's kickoff email. Sixth, over-optimizing among acceptable options. When failing to coordinate costs more than the difference stop optimizing. Seventh, prescribing "better communication" without a diagnosis. A thirty-second chat fixes the airport mix-up. No talking makes the hundred-thousand-dollar dispute disappear. Eighth, assuming a stable convention is a good one. "We've always done it this way" isn't proof of a good one. Sometimes the system itself needs redesigning and that's where Module Eleven picks up. Which of these does your team make most often? Aim your field assignment there.
