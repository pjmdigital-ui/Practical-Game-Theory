Internomics · Module 10 — Diagnose the Coordination Problem

Everyone knowing something is different from everyone knowing that everyone knows it. Game theorists call the stronger condition common knowledge. Here's the ordinary version. A manager tells you privately that the meeting moves to three o'clock. You don't know whether everyone else was told. Now the manager says it to the whole team in one room. You saw everyone hear it and they saw you hear it. Nothing about the meeting changed. The mutual awareness did. What expectation in your life lives only in private messages?
