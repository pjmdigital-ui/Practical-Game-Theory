The group shares the outcome. The individual incentive pushes each person to do less and hope others do more.

Internomics · Module 10 — Diagnose the Coordination Problem

Here's an important trap. Shared goals don't always mean coordination. Four roommates all prefer a clean apartment. That sounds like a job for a chore chart. But each privately thinks the apartment should be clean and somebody else should clean it. If everyone else cleans your best outcome may be enjoying it without the work. That's a free-rider problem. It's a social dilemma. In a coordination problem people are waiting for clarity. Here clarity isn't what's missing. Everybody knows the dishes need doing. So how do you tell which you're facing?
