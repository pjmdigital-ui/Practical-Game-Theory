Internomics · Module 10 — Diagnose the Coordination Problem

Here are your five questions. Pause the video and work through them. Write your answers down if you can, because writing forces you to commit. One. Is the calendar problem mainly conflict, coordination failure or social dilemma? Don't guess. Apply the simple test. If each person knew everyone else would use the calendar, would they use it too? Two. What is blocking adoption? And why didn't the kickoff email solve it? Look closely at the words "when you can." Three. Where exactly did the newsletter process break? Not who failed. Where. Four. Is a motivational speech the right intervention? If not, what would you do instead? Try to write the one sentence that makes the next move obvious to everyone. Five. Could any part of this turn out not to be coordination? This is the question people skip. A good diagnosis includes what would make you change your mind. Here's a hint for the whole exercise. Try to avoid every character word. Lazy. Resistant. Unmotivated. See how far you get by looking only at the structure. Take a few minutes now.
