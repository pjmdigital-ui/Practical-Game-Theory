Internomics · Module 10 — Diagnose the Coordination Problem

Every time a group lacks a convention people solve the same coordination problem again. Who runs the meeting? Where do files go? If they stay ambiguous everyone wastes energy predicting everyone else. A convention removes the ambiguity. It says this is what we do here. You don't wake up and decide which side of the road to use today. Repeated coordination is costly and the rule becomes a shortcut. What question does your team answer from scratch every week? Sometimes the fastest way to write a convention is just to pick something.
