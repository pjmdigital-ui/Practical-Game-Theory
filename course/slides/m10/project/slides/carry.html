Internomics · Next: Module 11 — Change the Game

So here's the question to carry this week. When people seem unable to get on the same page, ask: are we actually in conflict, or are we failing to coordinate? And if it's coordination, ask what expectation needs to become mutually clear. Sometimes the most strategic thing you can do is surprisingly simple: make the next move obvious to everyone. But what happens when you've done all of that, everyone knows the rules, the expectations are clear, and the system still keeps producing outcomes nobody wants? Maybe the problem isn't how people are playing. Maybe it's the game itself. That's Module Eleven: Change the Game.
