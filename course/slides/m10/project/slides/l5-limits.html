Internomics · Module 10 — Diagnose the Coordination Problem

First, focal points help when several outcomes would work and nobody knows which one others will choose. When players strongly prefer different outcomes the obvious option may not be enough. A focal point can help solve uncertainty. It cannot erase incompatible interests. Second, coordination is morally neutral. Groups can coordinate around harm as easily as around good. So our foundation stays where it was. Truth. Autonomy. Dignity. Informed choice. Does the plan respect the people affected? That's the difference between leading coordination and manipulating it.
