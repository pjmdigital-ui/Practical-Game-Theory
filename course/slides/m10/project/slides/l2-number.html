Internomics · Module 10 — Diagnose the Coordination Problem

Pick a positive whole number. If another person picks the same one you both win. Many people choose 1. It isn't better for matching. It stands out. It's first and simple and you can imagine the other person thinking the same thing. Players don't need to prefer the focal point. They only need to believe the other person will recognize it too.
