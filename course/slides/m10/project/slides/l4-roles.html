Internomics · Module 10 — Diagnose the Coordination Problem

A job title can be coordination technology. A role answers who does what. A deadline answers when we act. An agenda answers what we're coordinating around. A chain of command answers who decides when disagreement remains. Imagine ten people in a crisis who all want to help. Acting independently they get in each other's way. Then a leader says you handle this and you call them. Nobody's motivation changed. The leader created a shared coordination structure. Does your stuck team need inspiring or someone to hand out roles? Now what happens when nobody does.
