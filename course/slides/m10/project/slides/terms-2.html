Internomics · Module 10 — Diagnose the Coordination Problem

Four more. A focal point is an option that stands out so each player expects the other to recognize it too. A convention is a shared rule that solves a repeated coordination problem. It's the "this is what we do here" rule. Strategic uncertainty is uncertainty about what the other players will do. It's not about the world. It's about people. And common knowledge means everyone knows something. And everyone knows that everyone knows it.
