Internomics · Module 10 — Diagnose the Coordination Problem

A manager privately tells five employees "You're all responsible for making this change." Each wonders whether the others got the message. Now the manager says it to all five together. Same information. But now each can reason this way. They know I'm responsible. I know they're responsible. They know that I know. That chain lets people act without waiting. The common mistake is sending five careful private messages and thinking you're done. You've informed people. You haven't coordinated them.
