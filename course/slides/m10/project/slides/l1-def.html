Internomics · Module 10 — Diagnose the Coordination Problem

So what is a coordination game? It's a situation in which players benefit when their actions fit together. In a pure coordination game, which outcome you land on may matter less than the fact that everyone chooses the same one. Driving is the obvious example. You could build a perfectly good traffic system where everyone drives on the right. You could build one where everyone drives on the left. The disaster happens when half the people choose one convention and half choose the other. So the question often isn't "Which option is objectively better?" It's "Which option can we all reliably choose together?" That's a different kind of strategic problem, and it's the one this module is about.
