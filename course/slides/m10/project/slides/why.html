Why this module matters

Three different games can look alike from the outside.

Conflict

We genuinely want different outcomes.

Coordination failure

We could both benefit. We just aren't aligned on what each of us will do.

Social dilemma

We all like the outcome. Each of us would rather others carry the cost.

They don't have the same solution. Misdiagnose one and you spend your energy on the wrong fix.

Internomics · Module 10 — Diagnose the Coordination Problem

When an interaction goes badly most of us ask why they won't cooperate. This module trains you to separate three possibilities. Conflict means we genuinely want different outcomes. Coordination failure means we could both benefit but aren't aligned. A social dilemma means we all like the collective outcome but each of us would rather let others carry the cost. They are not the same game and they don't have the same solution. So think of one situation where people can't seem to get on the same page. Let's look at what you'll be able to do.
