Why this module matters

Conflict, coordination failure and social dilemma look alike from outside.

They are not the same game, and they do not have the same solution. Misdiagnose one, and you spend your energy on the wrong fix.

Internomics · Module 10 — Diagnose the Coordination Problem

Here's why this matters. When an interaction goes badly, most of us ask one question: why won't they cooperate? This module trains you to separate three possibilities instead. Conflict: we genuinely want different outcomes. Coordination failure: we could both benefit from compatible actions, but we aren't aligned on what each of us will do. And social dilemma: we all like the collective outcome, but each of us has an incentive to let others carry the cost. From the outside, those three look remarkably similar. But misdiagnose a coordination problem as a conflict, and you can spend enormous energy persuading someone who already wants what you want. Misdiagnose a free-rider problem as coordination, and you can write the clearest plan in the world and watch it fail, because everyone knows exactly what to do and still has a reason not to do it.
