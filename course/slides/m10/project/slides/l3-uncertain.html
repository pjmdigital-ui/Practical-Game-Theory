Two kinds of uncertainty

Ordinary

"Will it rain?"

Strategic

"What are the other players going to do?"

Internomics · Module 10 — Diagnose the Coordination Problem

There's a useful term for a problem we run into constantly: strategic uncertainty. Ordinary uncertainty is "Will it rain?" It's about the world. Strategic uncertainty is "What are the other players going to do?" It's about people whose choices depend on yours, just as yours depend on theirs. That second kind is what blocks coordination. Not a lack of motivation. Not a disagreement. Just not knowing whether anyone else is going to move.
