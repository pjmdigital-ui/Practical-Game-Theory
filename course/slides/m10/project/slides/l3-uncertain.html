Two kinds of uncertainty

Ordinary · about the world

"Will it rain?"

Strategic · about people

"What are the other players going to do?"

The second kind is what blocks coordination. Not a lack of motivation. Not a disagreement. Just not knowing whether anyone else will move.

Internomics · Module 10 — Diagnose the Coordination Problem

Ordinary uncertainty is "Will it rain?" The weather doesn't care what you decide. Strategic uncertainty is "What are the other players going to do?" I'm waiting to see what you do and you're waiting to see what I do. That loop blocks coordination. Not a lack of motivation. The Senegal farmers in our research spotlight understood the advantages of selling together. But each farmer's willingness depended on whether enough others would take part. So when a group is stuck ask this. Is anyone actually opposed? Or is everyone waiting for someone else? Let's see it inside a company.
