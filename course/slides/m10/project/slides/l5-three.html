Three games, three different fixes

Conflict

We want incompatible outcomes.

Needs: bargaining. Or a fair rule for choosing between options.

Coordination

We'd both benefit. We just aren't aligned.

Needs: clear expectations. Shared information. A focal point. A standard.

Social dilemma

Each would rather others pay.

Needs: incentives. Monitoring. Reputation. Repeated play. Commitments. New rules.

Diagnosing the game comes before choosing the intervention.

Internomics · Module 10 — Diagnose the Coordination Problem

This is why diagnosis comes before the intervention. If it's coordination people are willing but not aligned. Clearer expectations or a focal point or a standard can help. If it's free riding clarity may not be enough. It may need incentives or monitoring or reputation or commitments. If it's a real conflict people want incompatible outcomes and you're in bargaining territory. Here's the misunderstanding. Put monitoring on a coordination problem and you insult people who were willing all along. Put a clearer plan on a free-rider problem and nothing changes. Now the limits of focal points.
