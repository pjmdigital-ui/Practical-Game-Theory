Internomics · Module 10 — Diagnose the Coordination Problem

Remember cheap talk from Module Seven? Costless messages aren't credible evidence. But communication can do a different job. It can help people coordinate. Two people want to meet at the airport. One thinks Terminal A and the other Terminal B. A thirty-second conversation solves it. Now two people argue over a hundred-thousand-dollar payment. Talking may clarify the disagreement. It doesn't make the conflict disappear. So before you say "We need better communication" ask what it's supposed to accomplish. Information? Credibility? Persuasion? Bargaining? Or coordination?
