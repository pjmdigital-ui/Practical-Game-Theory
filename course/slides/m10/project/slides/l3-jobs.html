Internomics · Module 10 — Diagnose the Coordination Problem

Remember cheap talk from Module Seven? Costless messages aren't good evidence. But communication can do a different job entirely: it can help people coordinate. Cheap talk may be weak as proof and powerful as coordination. Those are different jobs. That explains why "just talk about it" feels miraculous in one situation and useless in another. Two people want to meet at the airport. One thinks Terminal A, the other thinks Terminal B. A thirty-second conversation solves the whole problem. Now two people are arguing over who receives a hundred-thousand-dollar payment. Talking may clarify the disagreement. It doesn't make the conflict disappear. So before you say "we need better communication," ask what communication is supposed to accomplish. Missing information? Credibility? Persuasion? Bargaining? Coordination? The answer matters.
