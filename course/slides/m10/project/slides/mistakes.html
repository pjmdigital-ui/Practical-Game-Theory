Common mistakes

Treating every disagreement as conflict

You end up persuading someone who already wants what you want.

Calling it a character flaw

First ask: was there a mutually understood assignment?

Assuming a shared goal settles it

It says where we want to end up. Not how we get there. It can also hide free riding.

Saying "everyone knows what to do"
