Internomics · Module 10 — Diagnose the Coordination Problem

Here are the key terms from this module, and they're all in your workbook. A coordination game: players benefit when their actions fit together. Coordination failure: we could both benefit from compatible actions, but we aren't aligned on what each of us will do. Conflict: we genuinely want different, incompatible outcomes. A social dilemma, or free-rider problem: we all like the collective outcome, but each of us has an incentive to let others carry more of the cost. And a mixed-motive game: we want to coordinate, but we disagree about which outcome to coordinate on, like the Battle of the Sexes.
