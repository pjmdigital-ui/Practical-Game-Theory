Internomics · Module 10 — Diagnose the Coordination Problem

Here are the key terms from this module. They're all in your workbook. A coordination game is a situation where players benefit when their actions fit together. Coordination failure means we could both benefit from compatible actions. We just aren't aligned on what each of us will do. Conflict means we genuinely want different and incompatible outcomes. A social dilemma is also called a free-rider problem. We all like the collective outcome. But each of us has an incentive to let others carry more of the cost. And a mixed-motive game is one where we want to coordinate but disagree about which outcome to coordinate on. The Battle of the Sexes is the classic case. Notice that the first four line up with the three games we've been diagnosing all module.
