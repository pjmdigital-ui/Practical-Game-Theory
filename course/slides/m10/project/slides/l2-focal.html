Internomics · Module 10 — Diagnose the Coordination Problem

Thomas Schelling asked a wonderfully simple question. You must meet someone in New York City tomorrow and you cannot communicate. Where do you go? People often pick something obvious. A famous landmark. A central station. Noon. They're asking what place the other person will think I will think is obvious. It's a guess about a guess. And it often works. Schelling called these focal points. In a coordination problem the hard part is predicting the other person. A focal point gives everyone the same prediction. So where would you go?
