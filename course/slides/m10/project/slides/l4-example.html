Example · The project nobody owned

"Did anyone call them?"

The situation: In a meeting everyone agrees. "We need to follow up with that customer today."

What happened: Nobody called. Sales thought customer service would. Customer service thought sales owned the relationship. The owner thought the sales manager had assigned it.

The misdiagnosis: "Nobody takes initiative." Or "Management is disorganized."

The lesson: Before judging character, ask: was there a mutually understood assignment?

Internomics · Module 10 — Diagnose the Coordination Problem

In a small company's meeting everyone agrees to follow up with a customer today. Tomorrow someone asks "Did anyone call them?" Silence. The sales manager thought customer service would do it. Customer service thought sales owned the relationship. The owner thought the sales manager had assigned it. The group failed to establish who was expected to act. Before making a character judgment like nobody takes initiative ask whether there was a mutually understood assignment. The fix is one sentence. "Jordan owns the call by 3 PM" said in front of the team. Let's put this on one line.
