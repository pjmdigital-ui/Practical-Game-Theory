Internomics · Module 10 — Diagnose the Coordination Problem

Picture a small company. In a meeting, everyone agrees: we need to follow up with that customer today. The meeting ends. Tomorrow someone asks, "Did anyone call them?" Silence. The sales manager thought customer service would do it. Customer service thought sales owned the relationship. The owner thought the sales manager had assigned it. Nobody opposed calling the customer. Nobody was unwilling to do the work. The group simply never established who was expected to act. And look how easy it is to misdiagnose. The owner says, "Nobody takes initiative." The employees say, "Management is disorganized." There might be some truth in both. But before you make a character judgment, ask: was there actually a mutually understood assignment?
