Internomics · Module 10 — Diagnose the Coordination Problem

You'd think a shared goal makes cooperation easy. But a shared goal tells you where people want to end up. It doesn't tell them how everyone gets there. Groundnut farmers in Senegal saw the benefits of selling through their cooperative. Yet beliefs about whether others would take part held them back. When researchers revealed what the group intended to sell behavior changed. Nobody got a new incentive. They got information about everyone else.
