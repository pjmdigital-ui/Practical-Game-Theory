Internomics · Module 10 — Diagnose the Coordination Problem

Two people have three equally acceptable options. They spend twenty minutes debating which is best. But failing to coordinate costs far more than the difference among the options. So stop optimizing and coordinate. Alternate. Flip a coin. Rotate. The rule only needs to be a mutually understood way of selecting one option. Here's the story from the chapter I love. A couple renegotiates every week who's picking up the kids Thursday. Almost any workable convention beats that. Whoever doesn't do Tuesday handles Thursday. What are you renegotiating every week?
