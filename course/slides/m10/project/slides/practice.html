Internomics · Module 10 — Diagnose the Coordination Problem

Let's practice. Dana runs a twelve-person marketing team at a regional credit union. Three months ago, leadership approved a shared content calendar to replace the team's spreadsheets and email threads. Dana's kickoff email said, "Everyone should start using the new calendar when you can." Today only two people use it. The others say they like the tool, but keep their spreadsheets because that's where everyone else's dates are. Last week two staff members scheduled competing promotional emails for the same morning. And a member newsletter went out without its compliance review: the writer assumed the designer had sent it for sign-off, and the designer assumed the writer had. Dana's director says, "Your team doesn't take ownership and resists change. Motivate them." Dana is drafting a speech about accountability. Pause the video and work through these five questions.
