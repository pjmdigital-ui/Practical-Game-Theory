Internomics · Module 10 — Diagnose the Coordination Problem

Your tool is the Coordination Diagnostic in your workbook. Write down the bad outcome that keeps happening without character judgments. Then work through the questions. Do we want incompatible things? Would I act if I knew the others would too? Commit to a diagnosis. What am I uncertain about? What are they uncertain about? What expectation needs to become mutually clear? Is there a focal point or convention? Does everyone know that everyone knows? The common mistake is jumping straight to fixing expectations. If the second question says social dilemma that won't be enough. So go in order. Then you choose the fix.
