Internomics · Module 10 — Diagnose the Coordination Problem

Your tool for this module is the Coordination Diagnostic. Use it when a group or relationship keeps producing an outcome nobody seems to want. Start by writing down the bad outcome without any character judgments. Then: do we actually want incompatible things? If so, it may be conflict or bargaining. Would I take the action if I knew the others would too? Answer honestly. Then commit to a diagnosis: conflict, coordination failure, social dilemma, or mixed. What am I uncertain about, and what are they uncertain about? Your intentions may be part of the problem. What expectation needs to become mutually clear: who acts, when, to what standard, and where's the handoff? Is there a focal point or convention you can use? Does everyone know that everyone knows? Then choose the intervention: a shared plan, a public commitment, a clear owner, a standard, a deadline, a focal point, or information about what everyone else intends. Write one sentence that makes the next move obvious to everyone, and run the ethics check.
