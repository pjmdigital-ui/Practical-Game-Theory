Internomics · Module 10 — Diagnose the Coordination Problem

Managers say it constantly. They know what they need to do. But the strategic question isn't "Have I said it?" It's "Do we share the same expectation about what happens next?" Here's the chapter's example. You believe the work must be finished Thursday. Someone else believes Thursday is when we review the draft. You both heard Thursday. You both nodded. And you are not coordinated. Thursday arrives and it feels like a conflict. The word was shared. The expectation wasn't. Think of your last deadline. Would everyone define "done" the same way you do? Sometimes the fix is remarkably boring.
