Clarity can be a strategic intervention

"Paul owns this by 3 PM."

1. Paul knows he owns it.

2. Everyone else knows Paul owns it.

3. Paul knows everyone else knows he owns it.

Not motivation. Not persuasion. Not incentives. Just one clear sentence. The ambiguity is gone.
