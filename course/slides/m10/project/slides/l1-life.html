Internomics · Module 10 — Diagnose the Coordination Problem

This structure is everywhere. Two founders both want the company to succeed but each wants their own strategy adopted. Two parents both want consistency but each prefers a different parenting rule. Two departments both want the project finished but each wants the other to absorb more work. That's why "We all want the same thing" doesn't settle it. Ask where exactly our interests align and where they diverge. The aligned part needs coordination. The divergent part needs fair bargaining or a rule for choosing. Can you name both for one disagreement in your life? Now the example the chapter opens with.
