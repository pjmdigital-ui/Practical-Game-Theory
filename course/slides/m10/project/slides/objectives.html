Internomics · Module 10 — Diagnose the Coordination Problem

Here's what you'll be able to do by the end of this module. You'll be able to tell conflict, coordination failure and social dilemma apart, using one simple test from the chapter. You'll be able to spot focal points and conventions, the things that help a group land on one workable option when several would do. You'll understand the difference between everyone knowing something and everyone knowing that everyone knows it. You'll know where coordination tends to break: at handoffs, at ambiguous deadlines, at tasks nobody owns. And you'll be able to match the intervention, whether that's a shared plan, a public commitment, a clear owner, a standard, a deadline, a focal point or information about what others intend, to the problem you've actually diagnosed.
