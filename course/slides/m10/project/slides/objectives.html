Internomics · Module 10 — Diagnose the Coordination Problem

Here's what you'll be able to do by the end of this module. First, you'll tell conflict apart from coordination failure and social dilemma. You'll do it with one simple test from the chapter. Second, you'll spot focal points and conventions. Those are the things that help a group land on one workable option when several would do. Third, you'll understand the difference between everyone knowing something and everyone knowing that everyone knows it. That sounds abstract. It turns out to be very practical. Fourth, you'll know where coordination tends to break. It breaks at handoffs. It breaks at ambiguous deadlines. It breaks at tasks nobody owns. And fifth, you'll match the intervention to the problem you've actually diagnosed. Sometimes that's a shared plan or a public commitment. Sometimes it's a clear owner or a deadline. Sometimes it's simply telling people what everyone else intends to do. Let's start with lesson one.
