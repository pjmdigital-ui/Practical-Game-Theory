Diagnose the Coordination Problem

Are we actually in conflict? Or are we failing to coordinate?

Paul Mascetta

Welcome to Module Ten. In the last module we saw how the expectation of a next time changes trust and cooperation. Now we deal with a different problem. Sometimes two people end up with a bad outcome neither of them wanted. Not because one betrayed the other. They simply failed to coordinate. This module is about telling the difference. Let's start with why that matters.
