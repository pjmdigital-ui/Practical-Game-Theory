Internomics · Module 10

Diagnose the Coordination Problem

Are we actually in conflict, or are we failing to coordinate?

Paul Mascetta

Welcome to Module Ten. In the last module we learned to think about the future: how the expectation of a next time changes trust, reputation and cooperation. Now we're going to deal with a different kind of problem. Sometimes two people end up with a bad outcome even though neither of them wanted it. Not because one betrayed the other. Not because their interests are opposed. Sometimes the problem is much simpler: they failed to coordinate. This module is about learning to tell the difference, and the question we'll keep coming back to is this one: are we actually in conflict, or are we failing to coordinate?
