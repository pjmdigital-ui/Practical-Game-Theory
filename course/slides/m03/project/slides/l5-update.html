Internomics · Module 3 — Think in Responses

So how do you hold a prediction without getting stuck on it? Model. Act. Observe. Update. You compare what they actually did with what you predicted. Then comes the step most people skip. Update. If you were wrong, don't conclude they're irrational. That's the lazy conclusion. Ask what you misunderstood about the game. Look at the four possibilities. Maybe their payoff was different. Maybe they had information you didn't. Maybe you misunderstood their options. Or maybe they were simply unpredictable. Check the first three before the fourth. The response is data. Now a loop you've almost certainly lived through.
