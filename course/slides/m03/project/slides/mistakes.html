Internomics · Module 3 — Think in Responses

A few mistakes I see all the time. First, judging a move by your intention. "I was only being honest" may be true, but strategy asks what your move makes the other person want to do. Second, projecting your preferences. "If I were them, I'd never" uses your payoffs, not theirs. And related: assuming perfect rationality. People get emotional and misread the game, so separate what's optimal for them from what's likely. Third, judging threats by how forcefully they're made instead of by the incentives when the moment comes. And fourth, overthinking and overpredicting. You don't need the 47-step tree, your model is a hypothesis, and they're predicting you too. One last one: don't mistake influence for control. You can shape the environment. Other people still choose their own responses.
