Common mistakes

Judging a move by your intention

"I was only being honest" may be true. Strategy asks what your move makes them want to do.

Projecting your preferences

"If I were them" uses your payoffs. Use their goals and information and constraints.

Assuming perfect rationality

People get emotional and misread the game. Separate what's optimal for them from what's likely.

Judging threats by forcefulness

Check whether carrying it out would be their best response when the moment arrives.

Internomics · Module 3 — Think in Responses

Here are the first four mistakes I see with this model. The first is judging a move by your intention. It isn't what they respond to. The second is projecting your preferences. "If I were them, I'd never do that" uses your payoffs. Put their preferences into their position. The third is assuming perfect rationality. People get emotional and miscalculate. So separate what's optimal for them from what's likely. The fourth is judging threats by how forcefully they're made. Check whether carrying it out would really be their best response.
