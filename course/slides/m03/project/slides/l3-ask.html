Internomics · Module 3 — Think in Responses

So how do you predict a response well? Look at the three grey cards. "What should they do?" is about your standards. "What would a reasonable person do?" is about an imaginary person. "What would I do?" is about you. None of them is really a prediction. Ask this instead. Given their goals and information and options and constraints, what is the most likely response? Constraints are things like a boss or money or time or reputation or emotions. Fill in all four and the prediction gets much sharper. Next let's look at the trap hiding inside the easy questions.
