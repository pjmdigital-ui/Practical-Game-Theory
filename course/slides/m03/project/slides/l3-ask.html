Ask the harder question

From

"If I were them, I'd never do that."

To

"Given what they want, know and can do, what's most likely?"

Internomics · Module 3 — Think in Responses

So how do you predict a response well? Before you act, pause. But notice which question you ask. Not "What should they do?" Not "What would a reasonable person do?" And not "What would I do?" Ask: given their goals, their information, their options and their constraints, what is the most likely response? That's a much harder question, and much more useful. The trap here is one we all fall into. "If I were them, I'd never do that." That sentence puts your preferences into their position. Remember Module Two: their payoffs may be completely different from yours. You want to put their preferences into their position. That's where game theory and ordinary empathy start to overlap.
