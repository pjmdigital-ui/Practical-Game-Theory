Internomics · Module 3 — Think in Responses

Once you predict their response, ask how you'll respond to that. Then ask how they'll respond to yours. You're anticipating their anticipation of your action. You already do this. Imagine you're negotiating over price. "If I offer ninety dollars, they'll probably counter at a hundred." "They probably expect me to counter again." So you might start at eighty-five. "But they know I'm going to start low." And the other person is doing the same thing. They're a player thinking about you. When did you last change your opening because of what they expected?
