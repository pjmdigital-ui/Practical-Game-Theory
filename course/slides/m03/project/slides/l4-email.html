Internomics · Module 3 — Think in Responses

Someone sends you an angry email. Your impulse is to reply immediately. But pause and ask one question. If I respond angrily, what will they probably do? Maybe they'll escalate. Then what? Now both of you are defending yourselves. And nobody is solving the original problem. Look at the chain on the screen. Four steps and you only chose one. But that choice made the next three much more likely. A common mistake is to judge your reply on its own. The better question is where the chain is going. Game theory has a tool for this. Backward induction.
