Definition · Best response

The action that gives a player the best outcome, given what the other player is doing.

Nash equilibrium is built from it: each player's strategy is a best response to the others, so no one gains by changing alone.

Internomics · Module 3 — Think in Responses

This is where a formal game-theory idea becomes incredibly useful. A best response is, roughly, the action that gives a player the best outcome given what the other player is doing. Imagine two friends choosing a restaurant. If you know your friend is definitely going to the Italian place, perhaps your best response is to go there too. If they're definitely going to the steakhouse, your best response changes. Your best move isn't determined in isolation. It depends on their move. Nash equilibrium is built from exactly this: an outcome where each player's strategy is a best response to the others. You don't need to calculate anything. You just need two habits. If I do this, what are they likely to do next? And if they do that, what will I do?
