Definition · Best response

The action that gives you the best outcome given what they're doing.

Your friend is set on the Italian place

Your best response may be to go there too.

Your friend is set on the steakhouse

Your best response changes.

Your best move isn't decided in isolation. It depends on their move.

Internomics · Module 3 — Think in Responses

Game theory calls this a best response. It's the action that gives a player the best outcome given what the other player is doing. A best response is never defined in a vacuum. Imagine you and a friend are choosing a restaurant. If your friend is definitely going to the Italian place, perhaps your best response is to go there too. Now your friend is set on the steakhouse. But your best response did. Because their move changed. So when you catch yourself searching for the perfect line, stop. Your best line depends on how they're likely to respond.
