Internomics · Module 3 — Think in Responses

Here's a simple example you've definitely lived. Two people are walking toward each other in a hallway. You move slightly to your right. They move slightly to their right. You both move again. Now you're awkwardly bouncing side to side. Neither of you wanted that. Both of you were just responding to the other's movement. Your action changed their best response, and their response changed yours. Now scale it up. Someone speaks aggressively to you. You interpret it as disrespect, so you respond aggressively. They interpret your response as confirmation that you're hostile, and they escalate. You escalate. Soon both of you are behaving in ways neither of you intended at the start. Nobody woke up and decided, "Let's make this interaction terrible." The pattern created itself.
