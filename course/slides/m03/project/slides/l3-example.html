Internomics · Module 3 — Think in Responses

Here's what this looks like in practice. Suppose a customer refuses your offer. The easy thought is, "They're being unreasonable." Instead, try: what response would make sense if I had their objectives and their constraints? Maybe they don't trust the delivery date. Maybe they have another supplier. Maybe they're worried about looking foolish. Maybe their boss has to approve the purchase. Maybe they don't understand the value yet. Or maybe they're testing how flexible you are. You don't know which one is true. But now you're looking for explanations in terms of the game, instead of immediately assigning a character judgment. You're searching the game instead of judging the person, and each of those explanations points to a different next move.
