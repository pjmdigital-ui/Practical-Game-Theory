Internomics · Module 3 — Think in Responses

Suppose a customer refuses your offer. The easy thought is: "They're being unreasonable." Instead ask what would make sense if you had their objectives and constraints. Maybe they don't trust the delivery date. Maybe their boss has to approve the purchase. Maybe they're testing how flexible you are. What matters is that you're looking for explanations in the game. Not a character judgment. And each explanation points to a different next move. "They're being unreasonable" points to no next move at all. So when someone says no, write down three reasons it might make sense for them. Here's the takeaway.
