Internomics · Module 3 — Think in Responses

Threats show exactly why backward reasoning matters. Suppose someone says, "If you do that, I'm going to walk away." Should you believe them? You can't tell from how forcefully they say it. You need to ask: if I actually do that, will walking away be their best response? A threat isn't strategically meaningful just because someone made it. You have to look at the incentives at the point where it would actually be carried out. Game theory uses this kind of backward reasoning to separate credible future actions from threats that wouldn't make sense once the decision point arrives. And it works in the other direction too. Before you make a threat, ask whether you'd actually want to carry it out when the moment comes.
