Internomics · Module 3 — Think in Responses

Someone says: "If you do that, I'm going to walk away." Should you believe them? But you can't determine credibility from how forcefully they say it. Look at the first card. If I actually do that, will walking away be their best response at that moment? It might leave them worse off than staying. You examine the incentives at the point it would be carried out. The second card turns it around. Before you make a threat, ask whether you'd actually want to carry it out. What threat have you made lately that you wouldn't?
