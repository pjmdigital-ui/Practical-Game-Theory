That doesn't make game theory useless. Understanding this difference is one reason experimental game theory exists.

Internomics · Module 3 — Think in Responses

Game theory often begins with models where players choose whatever best serves their preferences. Real human beings don't always do that. We get emotional. We miscalculate. So hold two things apart. What someone would choose if they were optimizing. And what they're actually likely to choose. Think about someone exhausted and embarrassed in a tense meeting. The optimal move might be to stay calm and ask a question. The likely move might be to snap. Good prediction holds both in view. Who in your life do you keep predicting as perfectly rational?
