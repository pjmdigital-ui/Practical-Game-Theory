The question to carry

What is this likely to make them do?

If they do that, what will I do? And if the stakes are high, where does that sequence lead?

Internomics · Next: Module 4 — Manage Predictability

So the next time you're about to say something important, don't stop at "What should I say?" Ask what it's likely to make them do. Then ask what you'll do if they do that. And if the stakes are high, ask where that sequence leads. Because once another person is involved, your move doesn't end when you stop talking. Your move becomes their situation. And their response becomes yours. Once you see it that way, you'll start to notice something else. Sometimes the most dangerous thing you can do is become completely predictable. That's Module Four.
