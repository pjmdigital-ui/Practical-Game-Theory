Here are your five questions. Pause the video here and work through them before you see my answer. Write your answers down. Don't just think them. First question. What did Dana intend her reaction to produce? And what did it actually make attractive for the team? That's intention versus incentive from lesson two. Second question. Write the sequence of moves. Who did what and in what order? Then find the point where it changed direction. That's the rewind from the Response Map. Third question. Why is "I specifically told them to come to me" beside the point? Think about the difference between words and the game those words enter. Fourth question. Reason backward. If Dana wants early disclosure, what must the team expect will happen when they bring her bad news? And where does that expectation come from? That's lesson four. Fifth question. What could Dana do now? And what responses should she anticipate when she does it? Think in branches here. Not certainties. Here's a hint if you get stuck. Don't ask what Dana should have said.
