Internomics · Module 3 — Think in Responses

Here's what you'll be able to do by the end of this module. You'll be able to explain what a best response is, and why the value of any move depends on how the other person is likely to reply. You'll be able to tell the difference between what you intend a move to produce and what it actually makes attractive for the other person. You'll predict a range of plausible responses using their goals, information, options and constraints, not your own preferences. You'll reason backward from where a sequence is likely to end, and use that to test whether a threat is credible. And you'll map an interaction as a chain of decisions, find the point where it turned, and spot escalation loops before they run away with you.
