Internomics · Module 3 — Think in Responses

Here's what you'll be able to do by the end of this module. Explain what a best response is. Separate what you intend a move to produce from what it makes attractive for the other person. Predict plausible responses from their goals and information and options and constraints. Reason backward from where a sequence ends and test whether a threat is credible. Map an interaction as a chain and spot escalation loops early. Let's start with lesson one.
