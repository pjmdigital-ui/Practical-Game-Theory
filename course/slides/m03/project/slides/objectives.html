Internomics · Module 3 — Think in Responses

Here's what you'll be able to do by the end of this module. You'll be able to explain what a best response is. You'll see why the value of any move depends on how the other person is likely to reply. You'll be able to separate what you intend a move to produce from what it actually makes attractive for the other person. You'll predict a range of plausible responses using their goals and information and options and constraints. Their preferences. Not yours. You'll reason backward from where a sequence is likely to end. And you'll use that to test whether a threat is credible. Finally you'll map an interaction as a chain of decisions. You'll find the point where it turned. And you'll learn to spot escalation loops before they run away with you. Let's start with lesson one.
