Internomics · Module 3 — Think in Responses

Let's go back to our employee asking for a raise. Suppose you tell your boss you have another job offer. It seems like a strong move. Game theory asks one more question. Then what? Look at the list. Your boss might offer you more money. But they might call your bluff. They might say they can't compete. They might decide you're no longer committed and start preparing for your departure. So the value of your statement depends on which response you expect. If you said this to your own boss tomorrow, which would you bet on? Let's see why.
