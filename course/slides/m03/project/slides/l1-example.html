Internomics · Module 3 — Think in Responses

Let's go back to the employee asking for a raise. Suppose you decide, "I'm going to tell my boss I have another job offer." At first glance that seems like a strong move. It communicates leverage. But game theory asks you to go one step further. Then what? Your boss might offer more money. They might ask to see the offer. They might call your bluff, or tell you they can't compete. They might start worrying that you're leaving, decide you're no longer committed, or quietly start preparing for your departure. The value of your statement depends on which response you expect, and that expectation should depend on what you know about your boss. A boss who desperately wants to keep you and a boss who's already been thinking about replacing you will hear the same sentence very differently. Same sentence. Different game.
