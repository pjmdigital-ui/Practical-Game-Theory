From a straight line to a chain

From

I say it → they hear it → outcome

To

I act → they respond → I respond to their response

Internomics · Module 3 — Think in Responses

Most of us naturally think about communication as a straight line. I say something, they hear it, and there's an outcome. But real interaction doesn't look like that. It looks like a chain. I act, they respond, I respond to their response, they respond to mine. And sometimes it keeps going. That means your decision shouldn't be based only on "What do I want to do?" It should also be based on "What will this cause the other person to do?" That sounds obvious. But it's remarkably easy to forget, especially when you're nervous, or angry, or in a hurry.
