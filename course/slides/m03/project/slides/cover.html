Think in Responses

If I do this, what are they likely to do next? And if they do that, what will I do?

Paul Mascetta

Welcome to Module Three. In Module One we learned to see the game. In Module Two we learned to see the payoffs. Now we get to the idea that sits at the heart of this whole course for me. Your move is only half of the move. The other half is what the other person does in response. And their response depends at least partly on what you did. This module is built around two simple questions. If I do this, what are they likely to do next? And if they do that, what will I do? That's where strategic thinking really begins. Let's get into it.
