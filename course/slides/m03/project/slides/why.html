Why this module matters

Once another person is involved, your move doesn't end when you stop talking.

Your move becomes their situation. Judge it by what it causes, not by how it feels to make.

Internomics · Module 3 — Think in Responses

We think about what we're going to say. We think about what we're going to do. We think about what we want to happen. But if another person is involved, the outcome isn't determined by our decision alone. They get a move too. When we forget that, we start judging our moves by how they feel to make. "I was just being honest." Maybe you were. But strategy asks a different question: what did your move cause the other person to do? We confuse intention with incentive. So in this module, I want you to stop at "What should I say?" a little less often, and ask what it's likely to make them do. Because once you speak, your move becomes their situation.
