Internomics · Module 3 — Think in Responses

Here are the key terms from this module, and they're all in your workbook. A best response: the action that gives a player the best outcome, given what the other player is doing. Nash equilibrium: an outcome where each player's strategy is a best response to the others, so no one benefits by changing alone. Intention versus incentive: the gap between the response you want and the response your action actually makes attractive. Rationalizability, which the textbooks also call iterated elimination of dominated strategies: ruling out moves that make no strategic sense, like a choice that's worse for you no matter what the other person does. And backward induction: starting from the final decision in a sequence and reasoning back to what you should do now.
