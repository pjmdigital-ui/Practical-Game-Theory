Example · The negotiation

Decide the last move first

The situation

You make an offer. They accept or reject. After a rejection you get one final response.

How you reason · Backward

1 · The end

If they reject, what will I do with my final response?

→

2 · Their move

Knowing that, will they accept or reject?

→

3 · Now

So what should my opening offer be?

The lesson

The game runs forward. You reason through it backward.

Internomics · Module 3 — Think in Responses

Here's the example from the book. You make an offer. They can accept or reject it. If they reject it, you get one final response. Most of us only ask "What should I offer?" Backward induction starts at the other end. Start with the first box. If they reject, what will I do? Decide that first. Then the second box. What will they do knowing that? If they expect you to cave, rejecting looks attractive. If they expect you to hold firm, accepting may look better. Only then do you reach the third box. What should I offer now? Deciding your last move first changes your first move. Here's the takeaway.
