Not "What do I want them to do?" Wanting a response and making it attractive are two different things.

Internomics · Module 3 — Think in Responses

Before an important move, ask one question. What does this make the other person want to do? Look at the four cards. You may want cooperation. Does your action make cooperation more attractive for them? You may want trust. Does your behavior give them a reason to trust you? And you may want them to stop pushing. If pushing gets them what they want from you, they'll keep pushing. Think of one thing you keep wanting from someone. What in your behavior makes it attractive to them? Let's name that gap.
