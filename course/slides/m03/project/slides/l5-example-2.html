Example · The escalation trap

The same loop, scaled up

The situation

Someone speaks aggressively to you. You read it as disrespect. You respond aggressively.

What happens

They read your response as proof you're hostile. They escalate. You escalate. Neither of you is acting as you intended.

The lesson

Each move was understandable. The whole interaction got worse. Nobody had to decide to make it terrible.

Internomics · Module 3 — Think in Responses

This is the escalation trap. Someone speaks aggressively to you. You interpret it as disrespect. So you respond aggressively. They read your response as confirmation that you're hostile. So you both escalate. Soon both of you are behaving in ways neither intended. Each step is an understandable response to the last one. Yet the whole thing keeps getting worse. Nobody has to decide to make it terrible. But if the pattern is built from responses, a different response can change its direction. Think of an argument that got out of hand. Where could a different response have turned it? That's our takeaway.
