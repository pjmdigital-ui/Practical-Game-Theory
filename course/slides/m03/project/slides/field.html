Internomics · Module 3 — Think in Responses

Here's your assignment this week. Pick three moves that matter. A request. A reply to a frustrating message. Some feedback. A "no." Before each one take sixty seconds and write four lines. My move. What I want them to do. Their two or three most plausible responses. Mark each one likely or possible or unlikely. And if they do the most likely thing, what will I do? Afterward record what they actually did. Then note whether your prediction was right or partly right or wrong. Once this week also notice a feedback loop while it's happening. Note whether it was escalating or settling. Next week we'll debrief. When your prediction missed, what had you misunderstood about the game? Did asking "Then what?" change any move before you made it? And in the loop you noticed, where could one well-chosen response have changed its direction?
