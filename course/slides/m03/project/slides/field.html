Internomics · Module 3 — Think in Responses

Here's your assignment this week. Before three moves that matter, a request, a reply to a frustrating message, some feedback, a "no", take sixty seconds and write four lines. My move. What I want them to do. Their two or three most plausible responses, each marked likely, possible or unlikely. And if they do the most likely thing, what will I do? Afterward, record what they actually did, and whether your prediction was right, partly right, or wrong. And once this week, notice a feedback loop while it's happening, and note whether it was escalating or settling. Next week we'll debrief: when your prediction missed, what had you misunderstood about the game? And did asking "Then what?" change any move before you made it?
