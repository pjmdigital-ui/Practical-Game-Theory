Internomics · Module 3 — Think in Responses

Best responses are the building block of a famous idea. Nash equilibrium. It's an outcome where each player's strategy is a best response to the others. So no one benefits by changing alone. If you're both at the Italian place and neither of you would rather be elsewhere, you've landed on an equilibrium. You don't need the math. What you need are two habits. If I do this, what are they likely to do next? And if they do that, what will I do? Let's put these habits to work.
