The gap

You can intend a response without creating the conditions that make it rational.

The test isn't what you meant. It's whether your move makes the response you want more attractive than their alternatives.

Internomics · Module 3 — Think in Responses

This is the difference between intention and incentive. You can sincerely intend to produce a particular response without creating the conditions that make that response rational for the other person. And when it doesn't come, it's tempting to defend the move by its intention. "I was only being honest." "I just wanted to set a standard." That may be completely true. But the other person doesn't respond to your intention. They respond to the situation your move puts them in. So the test isn't what you meant. The test is whether your move made the response you wanted more attractive than the other things they could do.
