The gap

You can intend a response without making it rational for them.

What we say after

"I was only being honest." "I just wanted to set a standard." Both may be true.

What they respond to

Not your intention. The situation your move puts them in.

The real test

Did your move make the response you wanted more attractive than their alternatives?

Internomics · Module 3 — Think in Responses

This is the difference between intention and incentive. Intention is what you hope your move will produce. Incentive is what it actually makes attractive for them. You can sincerely intend a response without making it rational for them. When it doesn't come, it's tempting to defend the move by its intention. "I was only being honest." But they can't see inside your head. They respond to the situation your move puts them in. So the test is whether your move made the response you wanted more attractive than their alternatives.
