Internomics · Module 3 — Think in Responses

Four more. A credible threat is one the person would actually want to carry out when the moment arrives. How forcefully it was stated doesn't matter. A feedback loop is a cycle in which each person interprets and responds to the other's last move. The escalation trap is a worsening pattern built from responses that each seem understandable on their own. The last one is model then act then observe then update. It means treating your prediction as a hypothesis. You compare it with what actually happens. Then you revise your understanding of the game.
