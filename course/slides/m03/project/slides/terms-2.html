Internomics · Module 3 — Think in Responses

Four more. A credible threat: one the person would actually want to carry out when the moment arrives, regardless of how forcefully it was stated. A feedback loop: a cycle in which each person interprets and responds to the other's last move. The escalation trap: a worsening pattern built from responses that each seem understandable on their own. And model, act, observe, update: treat your prediction as a hypothesis, compare it with what actually happens, and revise your understanding of the game.
