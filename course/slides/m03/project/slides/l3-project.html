From

To

"What would make sense for them?"

Your preferences in their position

A prediction about you. Not about them.

Their preferences in their position

What they want. What they know. What they can do.

Internomics · Module 3 — Think in Responses

Here's a trap we all fall into. "If I were them, I'd never do that." It sounds like perspective-taking. But it puts your preferences into their position. So the prediction is really about you. Remember Module Two. Their payoffs may be completely different from yours. What feels costly to you may be cheap for them. So put their preferences into their position. Empathy at its best is understanding how they see the game. Here's the misunderstanding. This doesn't mean agreeing with them. You can understand their position and still disagree.
