Internomics · Module 3 — Think in Responses

There's a danger in everything I've taught you so far. Once people discover this way of thinking, some of them get obsessed. They try to predict every possible move. That's not the goal. So here are three limits. The first is don't overthink. You don't need a 47-step decision tree every time your spouse asks what you want for dinner. The goal is to find the strategically relevant responses. Ask which response could materially change the outcome. Then ignore the rest. Strategic thinking isn't about thinking more. It's about thinking about the right dependencies. The second limit is don't overpredict. You can become so convinced you understand someone's strategy that you stop listening. You think you know exactly what they're going to do. And then they surprise you. That's dangerous. Your model of the other person is a hypothesis. It's not a fact. We'll look at how to handle that on the next slide. The third limit is one people forget. They're predicting you too. People aren't passive objects waiting for you to predict them. Suppose you always respond to pressure by giving in. Eventually someone may learn that pressure works on you. Your predictable response becomes part of their strategy. And now your own behavior is shaping the behavior of the other player. So don't just predict the other player. Remember that they may be predicting you. That's where the next module takes us.
