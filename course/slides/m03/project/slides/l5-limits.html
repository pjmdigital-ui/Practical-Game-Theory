Internomics · Module 3 — Think in Responses

Once people discover this way of thinking, some of them get obsessed with predicting every possible move. That's not the goal. You don't need a 47-step decision tree every time your spouse asks, "What do you want for dinner?" Ask what response could materially change the outcome, and ignore the rest. Strategic thinking isn't about thinking more. It's about the right dependencies. Second, don't overpredict. Your model of the other person is a hypothesis, not a fact. When they surprise you, don't conclude they're irrational. Ask what you misunderstood about the game. Maybe their payoff was different, or they knew something you didn't. The response is data. And third, they're predicting you too. If you always give in to pressure, people learn that pressure works. That's where Module Four takes us.
