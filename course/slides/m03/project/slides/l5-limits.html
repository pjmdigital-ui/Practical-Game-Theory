Internomics · Module 3 — Think in Responses

Some people discover this way of thinking and get obsessed. So here are three limits. First don't overthink. You don't need a 47-step decision tree when your spouse asks what you want for dinner. Ask which response could materially change the outcome. Second don't overpredict. You can become so sure you understand someone that you stop listening. Your model of them is a hypothesis. Not a fact. Third remember they're predicting you too. If you always give in to pressure, someone may learn that pressure works on you. Which limit do you hit most often?
