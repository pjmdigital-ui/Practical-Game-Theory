Internomics · Module 3 — Think in Responses

Your tool for this module is the Response Map. Part one is a rewind. Pick a recent interaction that didn't go the way you wanted. Don't analyze whether you were right or wrong yet. Just write down the sequence. What did I do? What did they do? What did I do next? Then ask two questions. Where did the interaction change direction? And what could you have predicted about their response before you moved? The common mistake is skipping straight to blame. Blame can wait. The sequence is where the learning is. Part two turns the same thinking forward.
