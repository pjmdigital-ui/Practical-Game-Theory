Internomics · Module 3 — Think in Responses

Here's a way to feel why the end of the sequence matters. Imagine two people having a disagreement, and they both know this is their last conversation. There's no tomorrow. No future relationship. No reputational consequences. No chance to repair things later. Now imagine the same two people expect to work together for the next twenty years. Same disagreement. Same people. Same immediate issue. But a completely different strategic environment. The things you'd say, and the risks you'd take, change because of what comes after. We'll go much deeper into repeated interaction in Module Nine. For now, notice the principle: the best response to a move depends partly on what comes after it.
