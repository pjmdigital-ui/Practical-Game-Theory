I call this the final conversation. Two people have a disagreement. They both know it's their last conversation. Look at the first card. They'd probably speak more bluntly. Now the second card. The same two people expect to work together for twenty years. Same disagreement. A completely different strategic environment. A cheap shot today becomes part of the next twenty years.
