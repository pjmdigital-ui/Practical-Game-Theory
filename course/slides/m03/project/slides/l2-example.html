Example · The sales team

"I want you to collaborate more."

The message

The manager sincerely wants the team to work together.

The pay plan

Compensation rewards each person for outperforming colleagues.

The best response

Protect your own leads. They heard the message perfectly.

Internomics · Module 3 — Think in Responses

Here's where this gets very practical. Suppose you manage a sales team and you tell everyone, "I want you to collaborate more." But you've structured compensation so that each salesperson gets rewarded individually for outperforming their colleagues. You may sincerely want collaboration. But you've created a situation in which each person's best response may be to protect their own leads. The problem isn't that your team didn't hear you. They may have heard you perfectly. The problem is that their incentives make another response more attractive. Saying it louder, or more often, won't fix that. People don't respond only to your words. They respond to the game those words are entering.
