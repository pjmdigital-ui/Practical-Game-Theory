Internomics · Module 3 — Think in Responses

Your tool for this module is the Response Map, and it has two parts. Part one is a rewind. Pick a recent interaction that didn't go the way you wanted. Don't judge who was right. Just write the sequence: what I did, what they did, what I did next, what they did next. Then ask where it changed direction, and what you could have predicted before you moved. Part two is a pre-move check. Write your planned move and what you want them to do. Fill in their side of the board, from their preferences, not yours. List several plausible responses and mark each likely, possible or unlikely. Check whether your move really makes the response you want attractive. Write what you'll do for each. For high stakes, ask where the sequence leads, cross off anything that's worse for you no matter what, check any threats, and ask what pattern this creates. Then afterward, update.
