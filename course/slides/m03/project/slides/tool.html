Internomics · Module 3 — Think in Responses

Part two of the Response Map is a pre-move check. You use it before a move that matters. It's in your workbook with space to write. Let me walk you through it. First write your planned move and what you want them to do. That's your intention. Second fill in their side of the board. Their goals. What they know and don't know. Their options. Their constraints. That means things like a boss or money or time or reputation or emotions. And do it from their preferences. Not yours. Third list several plausible responses. Mark each one likely or possible or unlikely. Replace certainty with branches. Fourth is the intention check. Does your move actually make the response you want more attractive than their alternatives? Fifth write what you'll do for each response. Sixth ask where the sequence leads. Save the deep version of that for high stakes. Also cross off any option that's worse for you no matter what they do. Seventh check any threats. Theirs or yours. Would carrying it out be the best response when the moment comes? And ask what pattern this move will create. Which response could materially change the outcome? Eighth comes afterward. What did they actually do? Where was your prediction off? What did you misunderstand about the game? Here's the mistake people make with a tool like this. They think they need to fill in every box for every conversation. You don't. Use the full map when the stakes are real. For everyday moves the first five are plenty.
