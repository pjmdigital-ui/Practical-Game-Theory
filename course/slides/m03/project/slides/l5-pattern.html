Internomics · Module 3 — Think in Responses

We'll fully explain equilibrium in Module Five. But you can see where we're heading. My behavior causes your response. Your response causes my next behavior. Eventually we may reach a pattern where neither of us has much reason to change. That's the loop settling down. Look at the four cards. Some equilibria are terrible. Some are stable but inefficient. Think about a couple where one nags and the other withdraws. Each response invites the other. But it holds. So also ask what pattern your action will create.
