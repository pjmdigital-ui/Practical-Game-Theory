Internomics · Module 3 — Think in Responses

Many human interactions are essentially loops. I act. You respond. I interpret your response. I act again. You interpret my action, and you respond again. The important word there is interpret. You're not responding only to what the other person objectively did. You're responding to what you believe their action means. We'll dig into that in Module Six, on information and beliefs. And here's where we're heading in Module Five. If my behavior causes your response, and your response causes my next behavior, we can settle into a pattern where neither of us has much reason to change. That's an equilibrium, and sometimes it's a terrible one. So asking "What's the best thing for me to do?" isn't enough. Also ask, "What pattern will my action create?"
