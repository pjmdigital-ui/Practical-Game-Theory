Internomics · Module 3 — Think in Responses

Many interactions are loops. Look at the four boxes. I act. You respond. I interpret your response. I act again. The most important word there is interpret. You're responding to what you believe their action means. Think about a one-word reply to a text. "Fine." Busy? Annoyed? Hurt? Your next move depends on which one you believe. So the loop is interpretations bouncing back and forth. Here's the common misunderstanding. People think they're reacting to the facts. Usually they're reacting to their story about the facts.
