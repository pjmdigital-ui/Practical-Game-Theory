Internomics · Module 3 — Think in Responses

Before you predict what someone will do, you can sometimes eliminate actions that don't make sense. Game theory calls this iterated elimination of dominated strategies. More broadly it's called rationalizability. Don't waste energy predicting moves that make no strategic sense. Look at the first card. Choice A gives you a worse outcome than Choice B no matter what the other person does. Then you don't need to predict them at all. Cross off A. So first ask: is there anything I shouldn't do regardless of what they do? Now let's go one level deeper.
