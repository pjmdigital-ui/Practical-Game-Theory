Example · The loophole

An extra $1,000 at your partner's expense

The situation: Two business partners have $10,000 on the table. One finds a loophole worth an extra $1,000 at the other's expense.

If it's their last deal: The choice looks like $1,000 versus $0.

If millions in business lie ahead over ten years: Taking the $1,000 puts the whole future of the partnership in play.

The lesson: The extra $1,000 is no longer worth just $1,000. It comes bundled with its future consequences.

Internomics · Module 9 — The Shadow of the Future

Let me make this concrete. The point is that a gain today is never just a gain today when there's a tomorrow. Imagine we're business partners. There's ten thousand dollars on the table. I discover a loophole that would let me take an extra thousand at your expense. Now run it two ways. First, suppose this is the last interaction we'll ever have. I might weigh the choice mainly as a thousand dollars versus nothing. That's a one-shot calculation. It's simple. It's also fairly tempting. Now suppose we expect to do millions of dollars of business together over the next ten years. Same loophole. Same thousand dollars. But now taking it may also produce a whole chain of other things. We'll see that chain on the next slide. The key insight is this. The extra thousand dollars is no longer worth just a thousand dollars. It comes bundled with its possible future consequences. Nothing about the loophole changed. What changed is the length of the shadow. Here's the misunderstanding to watch for. People assume the partner who leaves the money alone is just the more ethical one. Maybe. But a purely self-interested partner who can see ten years ahead might leave it alone too. And notice the other side. If you're the partner who might get exploited, you want the other person to see the future clearly. A long shadow protects you. So ask yourself this. When was the last time you grabbed a thousand dollars that cost you more later? Let's look at what that thousand dollars really comes bundled with.
