Example · The loophole

An extra $1,000 at your partner's expense

The situation: Two business partners have $10,000 on the table. One finds a loophole worth an extra $1,000 at the other's expense.

If it's their last deal: The choice looks like $1,000 versus $0.

If millions in business lie ahead over ten years: Taking the $1,000 puts the whole future of the partnership in play.

The lesson: The extra $1,000 is no longer worth just $1,000. It comes bundled with its future consequences.

Internomics · Module 9 — The Shadow of the Future

Let me make this concrete. We're business partners with ten thousand dollars on the table. I find a loophole that lets me take an extra thousand at your expense. If this is our last deal, it's a thousand dollars versus nothing. Now suppose we expect millions of dollars of business over the next ten years. Same loophole. But the extra thousand is no longer worth just a thousand. It comes bundled with its future consequences. People assume the partner who leaves the money alone is just more ethical. But a self-interested partner who can see ten years ahead might leave it too. Let's see what it's bundled with.
