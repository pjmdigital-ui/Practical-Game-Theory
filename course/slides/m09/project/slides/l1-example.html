Internomics · Module 9 — The Shadow of the Future

Imagine we're business partners. There's ten thousand dollars on the table, and I discover a loophole that would let me take an extra thousand at your expense. If this is the last interaction we'll ever have, I might weigh it mainly as a thousand dollars versus nothing. But suppose we expect to do millions of dollars of business together over the next ten years. Now taking that thousand may also produce less trust, more monitoring, tighter contracts, fewer opportunities, resentment, retaliation, or the end of the partnership. The extra thousand dollars is no longer worth just a thousand dollars. It comes bundled with its future consequences.
