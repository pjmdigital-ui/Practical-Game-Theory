Easy to deal with versus hard to deal with

Known as someone who

Keeps agreements

Handles disagreements fairly

Owns their own mistakes

Responds firmly but proportionately

Result: people trust you and share information.

Known as someone who

Squeezes every penny

Exploits every mistake

Changes terms when they can

Uses private information opportunistically

Result: people add protection or walk away.

The most valuable reputation: "This person is safe to play repeated games with."

Internomics · Module 9 — The Shadow of the Future

Here's a consequence people underestimate. Being easy to deal with increases the value of every future game. If you keep agreements and handle disagreements fairly, people trust you. They introduce you to others and share information. Now suppose you squeeze every penny out of every transaction. You win a lot. But people learn that dealing with you requires protection. So they add contract language and deposits. Or they refuse to deal with you. You optimized every round and made the game less valuable. The hard bargainer thinks they're the smart one.
