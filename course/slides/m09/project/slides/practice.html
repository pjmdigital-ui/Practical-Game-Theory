Internomics · Module 9 — The Shadow of the Future

Let's practice. Dana runs a small marketing agency. For four years her biggest client has been a regional furniture retailer, and her contact there is Marcus. Last month Marcus signed off on an ad with the wrong sale dates, and the client lost a weekend of sales. Dana's contract lets her bill for the rework, and her account lead, Priya, wants to bill the full four thousand dollars and send Marcus an email quoting his approval line by line, "so it never happens again." Meanwhile, Dana's freelance designer, Theo, has missed three of his last four deadlines, each time with an apology and a promise, and Dana has absorbed the overtime to keep the relationship good. Theo just asked for a raise. And Dana has learned that Marcus is leaving the retailer in two months. Pause the video and work through these five questions before you see my answer.
