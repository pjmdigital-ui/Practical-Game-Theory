Internomics · Module 9 — The Shadow of the Future

Let's practice. One person can be playing several repeated games at once. Dana runs a small marketing agency. For four years her biggest client has been a furniture retailer. Last month her contact Marcus signed off on an ad with the wrong sale dates. Her account lead Priya wants to bill the full four thousand dollars for the rework. She also wants to quote his approval line by line. Meanwhile her designer Theo has missed three of his last four deadlines. Dana absorbed the overtime each time. Now he wants a raise. And Marcus is leaving in two months. Ask how long each shadow is. Ask whether it's noise or a pattern. Pause the video and work through the five questions. Then I'll walk you through my answer.
