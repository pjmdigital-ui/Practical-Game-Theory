Internomics · Module 9 — The Shadow of the Future

Let's practice. Here's the point of this exercise. One person can be playing several repeated games at once. Each one may need a different move. Dana runs a small marketing agency. For four years her biggest client has been a regional furniture retailer. Her main contact there is Marcus. Last month Marcus signed off on an ad with the wrong sale dates. The client lost a weekend of sales. Dana's contract technically lets her bill for the rework. Her account lead Priya wants to bill the full four thousand dollars. She also wants to send Marcus an email quoting his approval line by line. Her reason is "so it never happens again." Meanwhile Dana has a freelance designer named Theo. He has missed three of his last four deadlines. Each time he apologized and promised to do better. Each time Dana absorbed the overtime "to keep the relationship good." Theo just asked for a raise. And separately Dana has learned that Marcus is leaving the retailer in two months. His replacement is unknown. So look at the three cards. There's a client relationship with an honest mistake in it. There's a designer relationship with a pattern in it. And there's an endpoint coming for one player. Here's a hint as you work. Don't treat these as one game. Ask how long each shadow is. Ask what each response would teach. And ask whether you're looking at noise or at a pattern. Pause the video now. Work through the five questions on the slide.
