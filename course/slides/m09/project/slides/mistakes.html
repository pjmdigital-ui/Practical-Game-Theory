Internomics · Module 9 — The Shadow of the Future

A few mistakes to watch for. The first is treating a relationship like a transaction. Optimize every round and you can make the game itself less valuable. Give the future a place in the decision. The second runs the other way: assuming "long term" means "always be nice." Unconditional cooperation can make exploitation profitable, and using "the long term" to justify endless losses is the same trap. Repeated exploitation is data. Change your strategy, change the rules, or leave. The third is treating every apparent defection as intentional. In a noisy world, rigid retaliation turns mistakes into feuds. And remember, forgiveness isn't forgetting. It just means not letting every deviation make cooperation impossible. And the fourth is treating cooperation as automatically good. Cartels and corrupt groups cooperate too. Game theory explains which patterns can persist. Ethics decides whether they deserve to.
