Internomics · Module 9 — The Shadow of the Future

Here's the point. Every important repeated interaction contains a learning process. The people around you are always learning what happens when they deal with you. Look at the questions on the slide. What happens when I tell this person the truth? What happens when I disagree? What happens when I say no? What happens when I make a mistake? What happens when I keep my word? Or when I break it? What happens when I cooperate? And what happens if I exploit them? You're answering every one of those questions through your behavior. You're doing it whether you intend to or not. Nobody hands out a rulebook. People watch what happens and they adjust. That's the shadow of the future in action. Today's response teaches tomorrow's expectation. And here's the key insight. When your words say one thing and your behavior says another, behavior usually wins. You can tell your team you value honesty. You can tell your kids they can always come to you. But they'll believe what happened the last time they tried it. This applies to far more than leadership. It applies to parents and partners. It applies to customers and employees. It applies to friends and spouses. And it applies to you. You're also learning from how other people treat you. Here's a question for you. Pick one of these six moments. Think about the last time someone close to you did it. How did you respond? And what do you think they learned? Next let's look at how this training plays out in everyday moments.
