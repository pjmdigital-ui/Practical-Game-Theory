Internomics · Module 9 — The Shadow of the Future

Every important repeated interaction contains a learning process. The people around you are learning: what happens when I tell this person the truth? What happens when I disagree? When I say no? When I make a mistake? When I keep my word, or break it? You're answering those questions through your behavior, whether you mean to or not. Take a parent. The child asks for something, you say no, they complain, they escalate, and eventually you give in. You solved the immediate problem. But the child learned that escalation works. Or someone makes an honest mistake and you punish them mercilessly. They learn: never admit mistakes. And when your words say one thing and your behavior says another, behavior usually wins. This applies to leaders, parents, partners, friends, and yourself.
