Internomics · Module 9 — The Shadow of the Future

Here's the point. The people around you are always learning what happens when they deal with you. What happens when they tell you the truth? When they disagree? When they make a mistake? You answer through your behavior whether you intend to or not. And when your words say one thing and your behavior says another, behavior usually wins. You can tell your kids they can always come to you. But they'll believe what happened the last time they tried. Think about the last time someone close to you made a mistake. What did they learn?
