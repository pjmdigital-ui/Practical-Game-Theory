Internomics · Module 9

The Shadow of the Future

What changes because there is a next time?

Paul Mascetta

Welcome to Module Nine. This one is called The Shadow of the Future. In the last module we saw that a commitment can change today's behavior. It does that by changing what becomes possible tomorrow. Now we're going to look at the other side of time. Sometimes you don't have to eliminate any future option. The mere fact that there is a future can change the game. Think about the people you'll deal with again next week and next year. That covers most of what matters in your life. So the question for this module is simple. What changes because there is a next time?
