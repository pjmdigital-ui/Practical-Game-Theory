Internomics · Module 9 — The Shadow of the Future

But punishment creates its own problem. Suppose you betray me accidentally. I think it was intentional, so I punish you. You believe my punishment was unprovoked, so you punish me. Now I see your punishment as a further betrayal, and I punish you again. What began as a mistake has become a feud. This is where real relationships get much harder than the clean models. We don't observe each other perfectly. We misunderstand. We forget. We mishear. We misread motives. Game theorists call that noise, and noise changes which strategies work well. In a noisy world, a rule that punishes every apparent defection as if it were deliberate will eventually punish an honest mistake, and start a cycle nobody wanted.
