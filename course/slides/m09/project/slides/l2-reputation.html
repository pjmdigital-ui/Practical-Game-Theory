Internomics · Module 9 — The Shadow of the Future

Here's the point. Reputation isn't a vague cloud of opinion. It's a prediction. It helps people answer one question. What is this person likely to do next? If you've kept your promises for twenty years, that history becomes evidence. So does breaking every agreement. People use it because they can't see inside your head. Reputations can be wrong. I'm not claiming they're accurate. I'm claiming they work. The common mistake is asking what people think of you. That's about image.
