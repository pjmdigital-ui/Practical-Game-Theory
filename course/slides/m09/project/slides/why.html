Internomics · Module 9 — The Shadow of the Future

Imagine buying something from a street vendor in a city you'll never visit again. Now imagine buying it from the store around the corner where you'll shop every week for twenty years. Same transaction. But not necessarily the same game. In the second one whatever happens today may affect what happens next week. The future has entered the payoff. Game theorists call this the shadow of the future. So the new question is "What happens next time?" Here's a question to hold as we go. Which of your important relationships have you been treating like a one-time transaction? Let's look at what you'll be able to do.
