Internomics · Module 9 — The Shadow of the Future

Imagine you're buying something from a street vendor in a city you'll never visit again. Now imagine buying the same thing from the store around the corner from your house, a store you expect to visit every week for the next twenty years. Same transaction. Same product. Same price. But not necessarily the same game. In the second one, your behavior today can influence how they treat you tomorrow, and their behavior today can influence whether you ever come back. Trust matters. Reputation matters. Retaliation matters. Repair matters. Earlier in the course you learned to ask, "Then what?" Now we're expanding the horizon to, "What happens next time?" Because a choice can be excellent in a one-shot game and terrible in a repeated one. And most of the important parts of your life are repeated games.
