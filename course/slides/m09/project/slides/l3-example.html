Example · Words versus history

The lesson nobody meant to teach

The situation: A manager tells the team: "Bring me bad news early."

What happened: An employee reports a serious problem right away. The manager becomes furious. They humiliate and blame the employee. Next month nobody reports anything.

The words said: "Report problems."

The behavior said: "Reporting problems is dangerous."

The lesson: people respond to the history of the game. Not to your words.

Internomics · Module 9 — The Shadow of the Future

Remember our manager from earlier modules? People respond to what you did last time and not to what you said. The manager tells employees to bring bad news early. Then an employee reports a serious problem right away. The manager becomes furious and blames them. Next month nobody says anything. The words said "Report problems." The behavior said "Reporting problems is dangerous." The second message wins. And everyone who watched learned it too. The manager thinks the team lacks courage. It doesn't. The team is reading the game accurately. Now let's see the same pattern at home.
