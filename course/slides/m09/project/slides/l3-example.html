Internomics · Module 9 — The Shadow of the Future

Remember our manager from earlier modules? The manager tells employees, "Bring me bad news early." An employee finds a serious problem and reports it right away. The manager becomes furious, humiliates them, and blames them. Next month, when someone else finds bad news, the manager is surprised that nobody says anything. But the employees aren't responding to the manager's words. They're responding to the history of the game. Now take a spouse in an argument. Their partner once told them something painful and deeply personal, and they realize they could use it right now. It would work. They might "win." But the partner learns that vulnerability is dangerous with them. So next time they disclose less and trust less. The immediate move succeeded. The repeated-game strategy failed.
