Internomics · Module 9 — The Shadow of the Future

Your tool is the Repeated-Game Audit. Use it whenever an interaction is likely to continue. Write down the interaction and the move you're tempted to make. Then work the prompts. Is there likely to be a next round? Is this a transaction or a relationship? What is today's move teaching them to expect? What does their history tell you? What reputation are you each building? What behavior are you rewarding? If a mistake happens, is there a path back? Then the hundred-round test. If you keep playing this way for a hundred rounds, where does it lead? Then write your revised move. The common mistake is stopping after the first prompt. Let's practice with a scenario.
