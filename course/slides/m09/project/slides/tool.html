Internomics · Module 9 — The Shadow of the Future

Your tool for this module is the Repeated-Game Audit. Here's the point. Use it whenever an interaction is likely to continue. It stops you from evaluating it as though it ends today. Start by writing down the interaction. Then write the move you're tempted to make. Now work through the prompts. One. Is there likely to be a next round? How valuable is it to you? How valuable is it to them? Is an endpoint in sight, like a last day or a final contract? Two. Is this a transaction that ends when the exchange ends? Or is it a relationship that creates another round? Three. What is today's move teaching them to expect from you? And what are you learning about them? Four. What does their history tell you? Have they been reliable for years? Or is this "here we go again"? Five. What reputation are you each building? Is it useful predictability or exploitable predictability? And what reputation do you want to be bound by? Six. What behavior are you rewarding? What behavior are you making costly? Seven. If a mistake happens, is there a path back to cooperation? How would you tell a mistake from exploitation? And eight. This is the hundred-round test. If you keep playing this way for the next hundred rounds, where does it lead? That last question can reveal things the immediate interaction hides. Then write your revised move. Here's the common mistake. People fill in the first prompt and stop. The power is in the last one. So which relationship will you run through this audit first? Let's practice with a scenario.
