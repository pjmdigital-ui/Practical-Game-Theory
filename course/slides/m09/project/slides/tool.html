Internomics · Module 9 — The Shadow of the Future

Your tool for this module is the Repeated-Game Audit. Use it whenever an interaction is likely to continue, so you stop evaluating it as though it ends today. Write down the interaction and the move you're tempted to make. Then: is there likely to be a next round, how valuable is it to each of you, and is an endpoint in sight? Is this a transaction or a relationship? What is today's move teaching them to expect, and what does their history tell you? What reputation are you each building, and is it useful predictability or exploitable predictability? What behavior are you rewarding, and what are you making costly? If a mistake happens, is there a path back, and how would you tell a mistake from exploitation? And finally the hundred-round test: if we keep playing this way for the next hundred rounds, where does it lead? Then write your revised move.
