Internomics · Module 9 — The Shadow of the Future

Here's the point. Tit for Tat teaches us something real. But it is not a rule for living. Its success depended on the other strategies present and the payoffs. It depended on how mistakes were handled. Picture Tit for Tat meeting an honest mistake. You meant to cooperate. I see a defection and defect. We can end up trading blows over something nobody intended. The lesson worth keeping is broader. Once people expect a future together, reciprocity becomes strategically possible. The common mistake is to match every slight exactly. Next let's look at another thing a shared future makes possible. Reputation.
