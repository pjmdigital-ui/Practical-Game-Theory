Reputation can trap you too

What reputation do I want to be bound by?

"I never back down." "I'm always the nice one." A reputation changes how others expect you to act. It can also change how you feel obligated to act.

"Keeps promises"

Useful predictability.

"Always gives in eventually"

Exploitable predictability.

"Retaliates wildly"

People may avoid you altogether.

Internomics · Module 9 — The Shadow of the Future

Here's the point. A good reputation can create value. It can also create constraints. If your identity is "I never back down" every conflict pressures you to keep it. That's true even when backing down would be wise. You become a prisoner of your own track record. Reputation is a form of strategic predictability. "Keeps promises" is useful predictability. "Always gives in eventually" is exploitable. People learn to push harder and wait. So the strategic question isn't just how to build a strong reputation. What reputation do I want to be bound by?
