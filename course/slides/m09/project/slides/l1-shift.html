Internomics · Module 9 — The Shadow of the Future

Let's go back to the Prisoner's Dilemma. In the basic one-shot version, the payoffs make defection individually attractive. If you cooperate, I can do better by defecting. If you defect, I protect myself by defecting. So we can end up at defect, defect, even though cooperate, cooperate would leave both of us better off. Now change one thing. Instead of playing once, we're going to play again tomorrow, and the day after, and perhaps after that. The immediate payoff hasn't necessarily changed. What's changed is the continuation of the relationship. And that can completely change the strategy.
