Internomics · Module 9 — The Shadow of the Future

Here's the point. A repeated game is not just the same game played more often. In the one-shot Prisoner's Dilemma defection looks individually attractive. So we can end up at defect and defect even though cooperating would leave both of us better off. Now change one thing. We play again tomorrow. The immediate payoff is exactly the same. What changed is the continuation of the relationship. People hear this and think repeated games make people nicer. That's not the claim. The claim is that the structure now contains a tomorrow. Let's look at what today's move can now change.
