Internomics · Module 9 — The Shadow of the Future

Here's your assignment this week. Pick one ongoing relationship and pay attention to what you're teaching. Each day note one moment that tested it. Maybe someone told you the truth or disagreed with you. Maybe someone made a mistake. Maybe someone kept or broke their word. Write down four things. What did they do? How did you respond? What did your response probably teach them to expect next time? And was it a mistake or a pattern? Was there a path back to cooperation? At least once this week, deliberately choose the move that makes tomorrow's game better over the one that gives you the most today. Notice what happens. Next week we'll talk about where your words and your behavior taught different lessons. We'll also look at what the hundred-round test says about your most important relationship.
