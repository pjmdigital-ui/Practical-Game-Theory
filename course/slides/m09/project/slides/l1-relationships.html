Transaction or relationship?

A transaction may end when the exchange ends. A relationship creates another round. And another. Most important parts of life are repeated games:

Your marriage

Your children

Your closest friends

Your employees and employer

Your business partners

Your community

Why it matters: Even your reputation among people you haven't met yet can create future rounds.

Internomics · Module 9 — The Shadow of the Future

Here's the simplest way to carry this model into your life. Before an important move ask whether it's a transaction or a relationship. A transaction may end when the exchange ends. A relationship creates another round. Your marriage. Your children. Your closest friends. Your work and your community. Even your reputation among people you haven't met yet creates future rounds. This doesn't mean every interaction should be calculated. It means the future deserves a place in the decision. So which relationship have you been handling like a transaction lately?
