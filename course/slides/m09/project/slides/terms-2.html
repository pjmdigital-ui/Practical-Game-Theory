Internomics · Module 9 — The Shadow of the Future

A few more. Reputation is accumulated information about how someone is likely to behave next, a form of strategic predictability. Noise is the misunderstandings, errors, and imperfect observation that make it hard to tell mistakes from exploitation. Repair is a route back to cooperation after a mistake: apology, clarification, restitution, forgiveness, renewed commitment. The end-game effect is the change in incentives when players believe there will be no next round. And strategic uncertainty is uncertainty about what the other player is going to do. Cooperation tends to flourish when it's robust to that uncertainty, which brings us to the research.
