Here's the point. Cooperation can come from structure and not only from character. Look at the mini-example. I could make a hundred dollars by betraying you today. But it costs me a thousand dollars of future business. Even if I cared only about my own payoff, cooperation could make sense. Some people hear this and say the cooperation is fake. It isn't. There's more than one path to cooperative behavior. Generosity. Morality. Loyalty. Friendship. Future consequences. Real people often have several at once.
