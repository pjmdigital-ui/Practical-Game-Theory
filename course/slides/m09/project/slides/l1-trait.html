More than one path to cooperation

Cooperation isn't only a personality trait. It can be a response to structure.

That doesn't make it fake. Generosity, loyalty and future consequences can all be at work at once.

Internomics · Module 9 — The Shadow of the Future

When people talk about cooperation, they usually explain it with personality. "She's cooperative." "He's selfish." Those things may matter. But repeated-game theory shows us another possibility. Suppose I could make a hundred dollars by betraying you today, but doing it costs me a thousand dollars of future business with you. Even if I cared only about my own payoff, cooperation could make sense. That doesn't make the cooperation fake. It just means there's more than one path to cooperative behavior. Someone might cooperate out of generosity, morality, loyalty, friendship, or because future consequences make cooperation strategically valuable. Real people often have several of those motives at once.
