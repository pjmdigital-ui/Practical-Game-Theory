Internomics · Module 9 — The Shadow of the Future

There's an opposite mistake to everything we've said about patience. Someone treats you badly, and you tell yourself, "I'm thinking long term." They do it again. And again. At some point, the long-run data are telling you something. Picture a business relationship that keeps producing broken promises, distrust, escalating monitoring, conflict, and poor outcomes. You could keep optimizing each new round. Or you could ask, "Why am I still playing this repeated game?" If the other player repeatedly exploits cooperation and never reciprocates, the lesson may not be to cooperate harder. It may be to change your strategy, change the rules, or leave the game. The shadow of the future only matters if the future game is worth playing. We'll come back to that in Change the Game.
