Internomics · Module 9 — The Shadow of the Future

Here's what repetition gives us. In a one-shot game, today's move is just today's move. In a repeated game, my choice today doesn't only determine today's payoff. It can change what you expect from me, how you treat me next time, whether you trust me, whether you punish me, and whether you cooperate with me again. Today becomes history, and history can affect tomorrow. If you cooperate with me, I can reward that later. If you exploit me, I can protect myself later. If you keep your promise, I can trust your next one more. If you keep breaking them, I can stop relying on them. Repeated interaction creates memory, and memory creates consequences.
