Internomics · Module 9 — The Shadow of the Future

So what does repetition add? My choice today doesn't only determine today's payoff. It can change what you expect from me and how you treat me next time. It can change whether you trust me or punish me or cooperate again. None of those show up today. Suppose the store around the corner overcharges you. They won that exchange. But next week you check the receipt and trust them less. Maybe you start shopping somewhere else. So pick a relationship you care about. Which of these five did your last important move touch?
