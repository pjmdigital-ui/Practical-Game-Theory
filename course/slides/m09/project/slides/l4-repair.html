Internomics · Module 9 — The Shadow of the Future

This gives us a concept that matters enormously in real relationships: repair. Suppose I fail you. If there's no route back to cooperation, then once the relationship breaks, it stays broken. But if the game has a way to tell mistakes from exploitation, and a way to restore cooperation after a mistake, the relationship becomes more robust. That might be an apology, a clarification, restitution, forgiveness, or a renewed commitment. Those aren't just soft skills. In a noisy repeated game, they stop errors from becoming permanent retaliation loops. But be careful with the word forgiveness. It doesn't mean pretending nothing happened, and it doesn't mean letting someone exploit you indefinitely. It means not letting every deviation make future cooperation impossible. A good strategy does two things at once: it discourages exploitation, and it allows recovery from mistakes. That tension is very real.
