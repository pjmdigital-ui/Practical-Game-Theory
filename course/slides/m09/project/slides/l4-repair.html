Internomics · Module 9 — The Shadow of the Future

Here's the point. In a noisy world the ability to recover from mistakes is a strategic asset. With no route back to cooperation every mistake becomes permanent. Repair might be an apology or a clarification. It might be restitution or forgiveness. It might be a renewed commitment about next time. These aren't just soft skills. They stop errors from becoming retaliation loops. Remember the chain on the last slide? Somebody says "Wait. That wasn't intentional." The chain stops right there. Repair isn't about being nice. It's about keeping a valuable game alive. But one of these words needs care. Let's talk about forgiveness.
