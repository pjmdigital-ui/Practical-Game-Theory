Internomics · Module 9 — The Shadow of the Future

Here's the point. The same action can mean completely different things depending on what came before. Suppose someone cancels dinner with you. If they've been reliable for ten years you think something must have come up. If they've cancelled the last six times you think "Here we go again." Same action. Different history. Different response. A long record of reliability buys you room for honest errors. A pattern of letting people down makes even honest mistakes look like more of the same. One cancellation in ten years is probably noise. Six in a row is data. What history will your next move arrive attached to? Let's pull this lesson together.
