Internomics · Module 9 — The Shadow of the Future

We need to be careful with the word forgiveness. Strategically it means one thing. Don't let every deviation make future cooperation impossible. It doesn't mean pretending nothing happened. It doesn't mean letting someone exploit you indefinitely. Be too forgiving and you become exploitable. Be too unforgiving and you get trapped in endless conflict. A good strategy discourages exploitation and allows recovery from mistakes. History helps. One slip from someone reliable for years probably deserves room. So which way do you usually lean? Now let's see what happens when nobody repairs anything for years.
