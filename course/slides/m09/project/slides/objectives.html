Internomics · Module 9 — The Shadow of the Future

Here's what you'll be able to do by the end of this module. First, tell a one-time transaction from a repeated relationship. Second, explain why repeated interaction makes reciprocity possible without guaranteeing cooperation. Third, see what a response is teaching someone to expect next time. Fourth, design responses that discourage exploitation and still leave a path back after honest mistakes. And fifth, recognize when "thinking long term" has become a reason to tolerate endless losses.
