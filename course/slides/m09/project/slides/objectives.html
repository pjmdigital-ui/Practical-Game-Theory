Internomics · Module 9 — The Shadow of the Future

Here's what you'll be able to do by the end of this module. You'll be able to tell a one-time transaction from a repeated relationship, and estimate how long the shadow of the future really is. You'll be able to explain why repeated interaction makes reciprocity possible, without pretending it guarantees cooperation. You'll be able to see what a specific response is teaching someone to expect next time, including the lessons you never meant to teach. You'll design responses that discourage exploitation while leaving a path back after honest mistakes. And you'll recognize when "thinking long term" has quietly become a reason to tolerate endless losses.
