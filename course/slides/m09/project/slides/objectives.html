Internomics · Module 9 — The Shadow of the Future

Here's what you'll be able to do by the end of this module. First, you'll tell a one-time transaction from a repeated relationship. You'll also estimate how long the shadow of the future really is. Second, you'll explain why repeated interaction makes reciprocity possible. You'll do it without pretending it guarantees cooperation. Third, you'll see what a specific response is teaching someone to expect next time. That includes the lessons you never meant to teach. Fourth, you'll design responses that discourage exploitation and still leave a path back after honest mistakes. And fifth, you'll recognize when "thinking long term" has quietly become a reason to tolerate endless losses. Sometimes changing the rules or leaving is the long-term strategy.
