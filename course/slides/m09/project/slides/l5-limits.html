Internomics · Module 9 — The Shadow of the Future

Three limits. First, the future only matters if players believe there is a future. Imagine someone who's been at a company for twenty years and plans to stay ten more. Their reputation inside matters. Now they know Friday is their final day. They may still behave well because of values, conscience, or relationships. But certain incentives have changed. The game has an endpoint. Second, it would be easy to conclude that people who interact repeatedly will cooperate. That's false. The future creates possibilities. It doesn't guarantee outcomes. And third, we shouldn't romanticize repeated games. A cartel can cooperate. A corrupt organization can cooperate. Two people can cooperate to deceive someone else. Cooperation is not the same thing as goodness. Game theory tells us how patterns become sustainable. Ethics tells us whether they're worth sustaining, and our standard stays the same: truth, autonomy, dignity, informed choice.
