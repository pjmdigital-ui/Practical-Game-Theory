Internomics · Module 9 — The Shadow of the Future

Suppose your strategy loses today. Does that make it bad? Not necessarily. You refuse to exploit someone and leave five hundred dollars on the table, and later they send you fifty thousand dollars of business. You enforce a boundary and create an uncomfortable conversation, and the relationship improves afterward. You admit a mistake and lose face for ten minutes, and gain credibility that lasts for years. The repeated-game lens teaches us that a strategy should sometimes be evaluated across the relationship, not the incident. That's a huge mental shift. And it's why strategic thinking sometimes means resisting a short-term win. Winning the round can lose the game.
