Internomics · Module 9 — The Shadow of the Future

Here are the key terms from this module, and you'll find all of them in your workbook. The shadow of the future: the influence that expected future interaction has on today's choices. The longer and more valuable the future, the longer the shadow. A one-shot game is one both sides expect to happen only once. A repeated game is the same interaction played again over time, so today's behavior can shape tomorrow's treatment. Tit for Tat cooperates first, then copies whatever the other player did last round: simple and successful in Axelrod's tournaments, but not universally best. Reciprocity means rewarding cooperation and responding to exploitation over time. And conditional cooperation means cooperating as long as the other side does, with a credible response if they don't.
