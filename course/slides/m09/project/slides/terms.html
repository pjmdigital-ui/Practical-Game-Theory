Internomics · Module 9 — The Shadow of the Future

Here are the key terms from this module. You'll find all of them in your workbook. The shadow of the future is the influence that expected future interaction has on today's choices. The longer and more valuable the future, the longer the shadow. A one-shot game is one both sides expect to happen only once. Today's move carries no future consequences between them. A repeated game is the same interaction played again over time. Today's behavior can shape tomorrow's treatment. Tit for Tat cooperates first. Then it copies whatever the other player did last round. It was simple and successful in Axelrod's tournaments. But it isn't universally best. Reciprocity means rewarding cooperation and responding to exploitation over time. A shared future is what makes it strategically possible. And conditional cooperation means cooperating as long as the other side does. You keep a credible response ready if they don't. It's often more durable than unconditional cooperation.
