Example · Two players, two patterns

Trapped vs. protected

The situation

Player one always answers a price cut with another price cut.

Player two is asked: "What's the lowest price you'll accept?"

What happened

Player one can be trapped into a predictable escalation.

Player two answers: "I'm not prepared to disclose my bottom line."

The lesson

One is trapped by a fixed rule.

The other protects information without saying anything false.

Internomics · Module 4 — Manage Predictability

Here are two cases side by side. The first is a competitor who always answers a price cut with another price cut. Everyone knows it. So somebody else gets to decide when the price war starts. Their own rule has become someone else's weapon. The second is a negotiator asked for the lowest price they'll accept. They answer "I'm not prepared to disclose my bottom line." No lie. No fake number. One player is trapped by a pattern anyone can read. The other is managing predictability. Do you have an automatic response someone could trigger on purpose? Let's close with one line.
