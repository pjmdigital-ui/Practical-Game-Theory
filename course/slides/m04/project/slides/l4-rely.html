Rebuilding trust

Sometimes the best strategy is to become more predictable

If you say you'll call

Call.

If you say you'll show up

Show up.

If you say you'll do it

Do it.

Over time they learn: his words predict his actions.

The lesson isn't "hide your intentions." It's "make your predictability work for you."

Internomics · Module 4 — Manage Predictability

Here's a situation where predictability is exactly what you need. Imagine you're trying to rebuild trust with someone. Right now they don't know whether you'll keep your word. You want to become more predictable. If you say you'll call, call. If you say you'll show up, show up. Over time they learn that your words predict your actions. Here's the misunderstanding to watch for. People rebuilding trust often make five commitments and keep three. It's better to promise less and keep every one.
