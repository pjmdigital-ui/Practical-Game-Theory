Example · The interrupter

Three meetings, one rule

The situation

Someone keeps interrupting you in meetings. The first time you let it go. Then the second time. Then the third.

What happened

They learned something. Interrupting you has no cost. You never decided to set that rule.

The lesson

Your repeated behavior set one anyway. Once they learn the rule they act on it.

Internomics · Module 4 — Manage Predictability

Here's a simple example. Someone repeatedly interrupts you in meetings. The first time you let it go. The second time you let it go again. And the third. Now they've learned something. Interrupting you has no cost. You never consciously decided to establish that rule. Your repeated behavior established one anyway. But they aren't experiencing three separate choices. They're experiencing a pattern. So ask "What rule am I teaching if I let this go again?" Is there a rule like this running in a meeting you sit in every week? Let's put the lesson into one line.
