Internomics · Module 4 — Manage Predictability

Here's the core idea of this module. If someone can reliably predict your action, they may be able to choose a response that takes advantage of it. Think about what that does to their side of the interaction. Normally, the other person has to wonder. Should I ask him? Should I push? Will she let this go? But once they know your pattern, they don't have to wonder anymore. They already know the answer. Your behavior has become part of their strategy. And notice: you didn't have to say anything for this to happen. You didn't announce a policy. You just did the same thing enough times that they could count on it. That's the habit they learn.
