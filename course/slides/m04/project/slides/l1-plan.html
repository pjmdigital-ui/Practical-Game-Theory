Internomics · Module 4 — Manage Predictability

Here's the core idea of this module. If someone can reliably predict your action, they may be able to choose a response that takes advantage of it. Normally the other person has to wonder. Should I ask him? Should I push? That uncertainty keeps them careful. Once they know your pattern, the uncertainty is gone. So they can plan around you. And you never had to say a word.
