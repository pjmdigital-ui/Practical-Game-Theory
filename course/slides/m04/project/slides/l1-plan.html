Internomics · Module 4 — Manage Predictability

Here's the core idea of this whole module. If someone can reliably predict your action, they may be able to choose a response that takes advantage of it. That's it. That's the engine underneath everything else we'll cover. Think about what that does to the other person's side of the interaction. Normally they have to wonder. Should I ask him? Should I push? Will she let this go? Every one of those questions carries some risk for them. They might ask and get turned down. They might push and lose the deal. That uncertainty keeps them careful. But once they know your pattern, the uncertainty is gone. They don't have to wonder anymore. They already know the answer. So they can plan. And when they plan around you, your behavior becomes part of their strategy. Notice something important here. You didn't have to say anything for this to happen. You didn't announce a policy. You didn't sign a contract. You just did the same thing enough times that they could count on it. That's why this is so easy to miss. It doesn't feel like a decision. It feels like just being yourself. Here's a question for you. Who in your life never seems to wonder how you'll respond? That person may have learned your pattern.
