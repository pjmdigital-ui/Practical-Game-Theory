Internomics · Module 4 — Manage Predictability

Let's play it out. Grab a partner if you can, or just imagine it. You and I are playing rock-paper-scissors, and I choose rock 70 percent of the time. If you know that, you have useful information. You don't need to know exactly what I'll throw next. You only need to know enough about my pattern to adjust: lean toward paper. My predictable behavior has created an advantage for you. That's what strategic predictability means. It isn't about someone reading your mind. It's about someone knowing your tendencies well enough to tilt the odds. And it's why optimal play in certain competitive games involves randomization. If your opponent can observe your pattern and exploit it, the pattern itself becomes a weakness.
