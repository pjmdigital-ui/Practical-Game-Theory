Example · Play it live

Rock, 70 percent of the time

The situation

You and a partner play rock-paper-scissors. You favor rock 70 percent of the time.

What happened

Your partner notices the tilt. They don't know your next throw. They just lean toward paper.

The lesson

Your predictable behavior created an advantage for them.

That's what strategic predictability means.

Internomics · Module 4 — Manage Predictability

Let's play it out. Say I choose rock 70 percent of the time. If you know that, you have useful information. You don't need to know exactly what I'll throw next. So you lean toward paper. You won't win every round. But over many rounds my tilt starts paying you. That's strategic predictability. Someone knows your tendencies well enough to tilt the odds their way.
