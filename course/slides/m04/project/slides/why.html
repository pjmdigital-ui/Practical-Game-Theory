Internomics · Module 4 — Manage Predictability

Here's why this module matters. If someone knows exactly how you'll respond, they can start planning around you. Your behavior becomes part of their strategy. Think about a coworker who knows that you say yes to every favor. Eventually they learn something. If I ask Paul, he'll probably say yes. Now I want to be clear right at the start. This is not a module about becoming erratic or deceptive. Predictability is also what makes trust possible. So practice one new question. What are they learning about me from what I repeatedly do?
