Why this module matters

If someone knows exactly how you'll respond, they can plan around you.

You never announced a rule, but they learned one: Ask, and he'll say yes. Push, and he'll move.

Internomics · Module 4 — Manage Predictability

Imagine a coworker who knows that every time they ask you for a favor, you say yes. At first, that's just your personality. You're helpful. But eventually they learn something: if I ask, he'll probably say yes. Now your predictability has become part of their strategy. The same thing happens in negotiation. If the other side knows you always concede after their first counteroffer, they don't have to wonder whether to push. Push, and he'll move. You've taught them the game. Now, this isn't a module about becoming erratic or hard to read. Predictability is also what makes trust and coordination possible. The skill is knowing when each is valuable. And the shift you'll practice is one new question: what are they learning about me from what I repeatedly do?
