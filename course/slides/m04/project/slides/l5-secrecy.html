Where the ethical line falls

Two kinds of unpredictability

Strategic: legitimate

Your competitor knows when you launch every year. They know your pricing pattern and your promotional schedule.

Changing those patterns so they can't optimize against you is competing.

Manipulative: out of bounds

Unpredictability used to deceive another person.

Unpredictability used to destabilize another person.

You can surprise a competitor without deceiving a customer.

Internomics · Module 4 — Manage Predictability

I initially hesitated to include unpredictability in this framework at all. I don't anymore because there are two different kinds. Strategic unpredictability is the first. Suppose your competitor knows you always launch new products at the same time every year. There may be real strategic value in changing that pattern. You can surprise a competitor without deceiving a customer. Manipulative unpredictability is the second. It's used to deceive or destabilize someone. That's not what we're doing here.
