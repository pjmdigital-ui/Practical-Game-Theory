Example · The concession pattern

$100 → $90 → $80

The situation

A negotiator always makes three concessions. First $100. Then $90. Then $80.

What happened

The other side learns the pattern. They push. You move. They push again. You move again.

The lesson

They never needed your true minimum. Only that you'll move again.

Your pattern told them: there's more room.
