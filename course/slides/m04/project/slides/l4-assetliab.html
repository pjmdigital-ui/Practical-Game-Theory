The strategic value of a pattern

Asset or liability?

An asset when it...

  • creates trust
  • makes cooperation easier
  • establishes credible commitments
  • reduces uncertainty
  • helps others coordinate with you

A liability when...

  • people know exactly how to pressure you
  • your threats are never followed through
  • your concessions are predictable
  • your boundaries are predictable but unenforced
  • competitors can exploit it

Same phenomenon. Different game.

Internomics · Module 4 — Manage Predictability

How do you tell whether a pattern is helping or hurting you? A pattern is an asset when it creates trust or makes cooperation easier. It's a liability when people know exactly how to pressure you. It's a liability when your boundaries are predictable but unenforced. The underlying thing is identical. Someone can predict you. The difference is what that prediction lets them do. Here's the misunderstanding to watch for. People judge a pattern by how it feels. But the question isn't how it feels. It's what it enables. Next let's meet two equally predictable managers.
