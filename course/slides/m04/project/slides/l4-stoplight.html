Internomics · Module 4 — Manage Predictability

Think about a stoplight. At an intersection you want the other drivers to know what you'll do. Red means stop. Green means go. Now imagine everyone became unpredictable. Traffic would become dangerous almost immediately. Coordination requires predictability. So predictability can create trust and coordination. Or it can create exploitation and vulnerability. Same phenomenon. Different game. Here's the misunderstanding to watch for. Some people now decide predictability is a weakness. But most of your daily life is closer to the stoplight than to the poker table. Next let's see what happens when you get this wrong the other way.
