Internomics · Module 4 — Manage Predictability

Secrecy is not deception. You don't owe every competitor complete information about your plans. In a negotiation you don't have to reveal your reservation price. On one side is withholding information you are not obligated to disclose. On the other side is deliberately providing false information to induce someone into a decision. Inventing a competing offer you don't have is on the wrong side. Game theory doesn't teach you to manipulate people. Have you ever felt pressured to answer a question you didn't owe an answer to?
