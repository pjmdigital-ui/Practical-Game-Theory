Internomics · Module 4 — Manage Predictability

Now the balance. Surprise is useful only when the uncertainty it creates is valuable. If your employee doesn't know whether you'll approve their vacation, that's just stressful. If your spouse doesn't know whether you'll keep your commitment, that's a relationship problem. Who benefits from knowing what I'm going to do? If the answer is "both of us" then keep the pattern. If they can use it to consistently take advantage of you, some uncertainty may be valuable. Here's the misunderstanding to watch for. Someone benefiting isn't the same as someone exploiting.
