Internomics · Module 4 — Manage Predictability

Remember the sequence from the last module? I act, you respond, I respond, you respond. Now add learning. I act, you respond, I observe, you observe, and we both update our expectations. The game is no longer just action and response. It's action, response, learning, adaptation. And that means your behavior today can change the game tomorrow. Now bring back the Prisoner's Dilemma. Two players may have an incentive to defect even though mutual cooperation would be better. But play it repeatedly, and today's choice affects tomorrow's. If you know I'll always retaliate against defection, my response is predictable, and that predictability may actually support cooperation. Here's the paradox: the same predictability that makes you exploitable in one game can make you trustworthy in another. That's why we can't turn any strategic concept into a universal rule.
