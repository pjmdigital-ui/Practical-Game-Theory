Internomics · Module 4 — Manage Predictability

Remember the response chain from the last module? I act. You respond. Now add learning. I act. You respond. I observe. You observe. And we both update our expectations. Your behavior today can change the game tomorrow. Every move does two jobs. It gets a result now. And it teaches the other person something for next time. So keep the question "What will they do if I do this?" Then add "What are they learning about me from what I repeatedly do?" Pick one daily relationship. What has it learned from you this month?
