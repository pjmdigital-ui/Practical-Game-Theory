Internomics · Module 4 — Manage Predictability

Remember the sequence from the last module? I act. You respond. I respond. You respond. That was the response chain. Now I want to add one more thing to it. Learning. Here's how it looks now. I act. You respond. I observe. You observe. And we both update our expectations. So the game is no longer just action and response. It's action and response. Then it's learning and adaptation. That changes everything about how you should think about a single move. Your behavior today can change the game tomorrow. Every move is doing two jobs at once. It's getting a result right now. And it's teaching the other person something they'll use next time. This is one of the reasons repeated interaction is so powerful. The people you deal with every week are learning from you every week. Your coworkers. Your family. Your regular clients. Think back to the interrupter from Lesson One. The pattern didn't stay the same size. It grew because the other person kept learning and kept adapting. So here's the mental shift for this whole module. Before this you learned to ask "What will they do if I do this?" Keep that question. Now add another one. "What are they learning about me from what I repeatedly do?" You're no longer thinking only about the immediate response. You're thinking about the information your behavior creates. And that information can change the future game. Here's a question for you. Pick one relationship you're in every day. What has it learned from you this month? Next let's revisit an old friend from the last module.
