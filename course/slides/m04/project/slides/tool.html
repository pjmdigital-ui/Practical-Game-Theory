Internomics · Module 4 — Manage Predictability

Your tool for this module is the Predictability Audit. Use it on one recurring interaction where a pattern might be shaping the game. First, name the person and the recurring situation: a meeting, a negotiation, a household routine. Then find the pattern. What do I repeatedly do here? What rule has this person learned? Finish the sentence: "If I do this, they will do that." Did I choose that rule, or did my behavior establish it? Next, calculate the tax: what does this pattern reveal that I didn't mean to reveal, like my limits, my availability, or whether my boundaries are real? Then ask who benefits from knowing what I'll do, and whether the pattern is an asset or a liability. Choose one of the four moves and the specific behavior you'll change or reinforce. And finish with the ethics check: am I protecting legitimate information, or providing false information?
