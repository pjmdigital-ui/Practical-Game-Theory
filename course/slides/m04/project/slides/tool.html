Internomics · Module 4 — Manage Predictability

Your tool for this module is the Predictability Audit. It's in your workbook. Use it on one recurring interaction where a pattern might be shaping the game. Here's how it works. Step one is to name the interaction. Who's the person or group? What's the recurring situation? It might be a weekly meeting. It might be a negotiation or a household routine or a regular request. Step two is to find the pattern. What do I repeatedly do in this situation? What have my past actions taught this person to expect from me? Then finish the sentence "If I do this, they will do that." And ask yourself honestly whether you chose that rule or whether your behavior established it. Step three is to calculate the tax. What does this pattern reveal that I didn't mean to reveal? Maybe it's my limits. Maybe it's my availability. Maybe it's whether my boundaries are real. Step four is the asset-or-liability test. Who benefits from knowing what I'm going to do? Is it both of us? Mainly them? Mainly me? Could they exploit this pattern? How? Step five is to choose your move. Pick one of the four. Then write down the specific behavior you'll change or reinforce. And step six is the ethics check. Am I protecting legitimate information or providing false information? Could my change destabilize someone? Could it make me harder to trust where reliability matters? Here's the mistake to watch for. People rush straight to step five. Don't. The first four steps are where you actually see the game. Let's practice the audit on a real-feeling case.
