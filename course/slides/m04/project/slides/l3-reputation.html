Internomics · Module 4 — Manage Predictability

Let's zoom out. When people watch your patterns over time, what are they really building? They're building a reputation. Here's how I want you to think about that word. Reputation is accumulated evidence about your future behavior. It isn't a vague feeling people have about you. It's their running estimate of what you'll do next. Suppose you consistently follow through on commitments. People learn something. "His promises mean something." Your past behavior has become information. Now suppose you repeatedly make threats you don't carry out. People learn something there too. "His threats don't mean much." Again your behavior has become information. Notice that both of these are predictable patterns. One makes your words more valuable. The other makes your words almost worthless. Same mechanism. Opposite result. That's why this matters so much. Your words only carry the weight your track record gives them. If your record says your promises hold, a single sentence from you can settle a question. If your record says your threats are empty, you can shout and nobody moves. We'll explore reputation much more deeply later in the course. For now I want you to hold one line. Every repeated pattern teaches the other player something about you. So here's a question for you. If someone close to you had to describe your track record in one sentence, what would it be?
