Internomics · Module 4

Manage Predictability

What does my predictability enable the other player to do?

Paul Mascetta

Welcome to Module Four: Manage Predictability. In the last module, we learned to think in responses. Before you make a move, ask what it's likely to make the other person do. This module turns that question around. What happens when the other person can predict you? By the end of it, you'll be able to see the patterns other people have learned about you, tell when those patterns help and when they hurt, and choose on purpose how readable you want to be. The question running through all of it: what does my predictability enable the other player to do?
