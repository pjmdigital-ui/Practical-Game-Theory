Internomics · Module 4

Manage Predictability

What does my predictability enable the other player to do?

Paul Mascetta

Welcome to Module Four. This one is called Manage Predictability. In the last module we learned to think in responses. Before you make a move, you ask what it's likely to make the other person do. This module turns that question around. What happens when the other person can predict you? If someone knows exactly how you'll respond, they can start planning around you. Your behavior becomes part of their strategy. By the end of this module you'll see the patterns people have learned about you. You'll know when those patterns help you and when they hurt you. And you'll choose on purpose how readable you want to be. Here's the question that runs through all of it. What does my predictability enable the other player to do?
