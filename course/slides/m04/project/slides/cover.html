Manage Predictability

What does my predictability enable the other player to do?

Paul Mascetta

Welcome to Module Four. In the last module we learned to think in responses. What happens when the other person can predict you? If someone knows exactly how you'll respond, they can start planning around you. By the end you'll choose on purpose how readable you want to be. Here's the question that runs through it all. What does my predictability enable the other player to do?
