Internomics · Module 4 — Manage Predictability

Here's what you'll be able to do by the end of this module. You'll be able to spot the patterns in your own behavior that other people have learned to predict, including rules you never meant to create. You'll be able to tell when predictability builds trust and coordination, and when it creates exploitation. You'll be able to explain the mixed-strategy idea in plain language, and why simply doing different things isn't the same as being unpredictable. You'll know the difference between withholding information you don't owe anyone and giving someone false information. And you'll be able to choose among four moves: be predictable, protect your predictability, vary your behavior, or be deliberately consistent.
