Internomics · Module 4 — Manage Predictability

Here's what you'll be able to do by the end of this module. First, you'll spot the patterns in your own behavior that other people have learned to predict. That includes rules you never meant to create. Second, you'll tell the difference between predictability that builds trust and predictability that invites exploitation. Third, you'll be able to explain the mixed-strategy idea in plain language. You'll also see why simply doing different things isn't the same as being unpredictable. Fourth, you'll know the line between withholding information you don't owe anyone and giving someone false information. And fifth, you'll choose among four moves. Be predictable. Protect your predictability. Vary your behavior. Or be deliberately consistent. Let's start with the habit they learn.
