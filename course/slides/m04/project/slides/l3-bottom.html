Here's where the tax gets expensive. You're negotiating and the other person asks "What's the lowest price you'll accept?" You give them the number. Now they know your boundary. You've told them how far they need to push. This doesn't mean you should lie. You might legitimately say "I'm not prepared to disclose my bottom line." Every word of it is true. Here's the misunderstanding to watch for. Declining to answer isn't dishonest. Withholding information you don't owe someone is very different from giving them false information.
