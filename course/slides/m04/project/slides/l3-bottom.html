Protecting your limits

Giving away your bottom line tells them how far they need to push.

You can decline without lying: "I'm not prepared to disclose my bottom line."

Internomics · Module 4 — Manage Predictability

Here's where the tax gets expensive. Imagine you're negotiating and the other person asks, "What's the lowest price you'll accept?" You give them the number. Now they know something extremely valuable: your boundary. You may think you've simply answered a question. Strategically, you've changed the information structure. You've told them how far they need to push. That doesn't mean you should lie. You might legitimately say, "I'm not prepared to disclose my bottom line." That's not deception. It's protecting strategically valuable information. And now the other player has to make their decision without knowing exactly where your boundary lies, which is exactly where they'd be if you'd never been asked.
