Common mistakes

"Keep them guessing"

Game theory doesn't say that. Where trust matters, excess unpredictability is destructive.

Variety mistaken for uncertainty

Rock then paper then scissors is still a pattern. If they can learn it they can exploit it.

Unenforced boundaries

A boundary you state but don't enforce teaches the opposite of what you said.

Secrecy sliding into deception

Declining to share your bottom line is legitimate. Giving false information is not.

Internomics · Module 4 — Manage Predictability

Here are the first four of seven common mistakes. The first is treating "keep them guessing" as the lesson. The second is confusing variety with unpredictability. Rock then paper then scissors every time is just as exploitable as rock every time. The third is stating a boundary but never enforcing it. Dana's "I won't keep doing this" is the textbook case. The fourth is letting secrecy slide into deception. Declining honestly is fine but providing false information to push someone into a decision is not. Which of these have you made in the last month?
