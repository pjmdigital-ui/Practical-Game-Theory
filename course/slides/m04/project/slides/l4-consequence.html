Making a consequence real

Sometimes you want them to know exactly what you'll do

The rule: "If you falsify customer records, you will be terminated."

The follow-through: You enforce it consistently. Every time.

What they gain: They can anticipate the consequences of their choices. They don't have to wonder.

A predictable response changes behavior before the violation occurs.
