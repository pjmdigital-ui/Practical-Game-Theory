Internomics · Module 4 — Manage Predictability

Here are two managers. The first one says, "I really don't like firing people." That's a personal preference, and a pretty human one. But if employees learn, "He never actually fires anyone," the statement has become part of the strategic environment. Some employees may begin taking risks they otherwise wouldn't. His predictability has changed their incentives. Now the opposite manager. Her employees know: if you violate this rule, there will be consequences. That predictability can actually improve cooperation. Notice what's going on. Both managers are perfectly predictable. The trait is the same. The strategic effect is opposite, because of what each pattern allows the other players to do. This is a reputation neither of them may have meant to create, and it's shaping behavior every day.
