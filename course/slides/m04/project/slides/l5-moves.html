The four moves

Be predictable

When trust or coordination is valuable. When credibility or cooperation is valuable.

Protect your predictability

When revealing your pattern gives another player leverage.

Vary your behavior

When someone could otherwise exploit a fixed pattern.

Be deliberately consistent

When you want them to understand a consequence is real.

Ask first: what does my predictability enable the other player to do?

Internomics · Module 4 — Manage Predictability

So how do you choose? There are four moves. First, be predictable when trust or coordination matters. That's the stoplight. Second, protect your predictability when revealing your pattern would give another player leverage. You're just not handing over the map. Third, vary your behavior when someone could otherwise exploit a fixed pattern. That's rock-paper-scissors. Fourth, be deliberately consistent when you want someone to understand that a consequence is real. The question that picks between them is always the same.
