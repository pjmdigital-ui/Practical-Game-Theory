The question to carry

Have I taught this person how to respond to me?

What have my past actions taught them to expect from me?

Internomics · Next: Module 5 — See the Equilibrium

Here's the question to carry this week. When you notice a repeated pattern, ask yourself this. Have I taught this person how to respond to me? What have my past actions taught them to expect? Your behavior doesn't disappear after the moment. It becomes information. In Module Five we'll look at equilibrium. Those are patterns that stay stable even when nobody is happy with them.
