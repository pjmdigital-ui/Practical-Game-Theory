The question to carry

Have I taught this person how to respond to me?

What have my past actions taught them to expect from me?

Internomics · Next: Module 5 — See the Equilibrium

So here's the question to carry this week. The next time you notice a repeated pattern in an interaction, ask: have I taught this person how to respond to me? And then: what have my past actions taught them to expect from me? Because your behavior doesn't disappear after the moment is over. It becomes information. It becomes expectation. And eventually it can become part of the game itself. In Module Five, we'll look at equilibrium: the patterns of behavior that can become stable even when nobody is particularly happy with them. I'll see you there.
