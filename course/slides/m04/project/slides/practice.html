Internomics · Module 4 — Manage Predictability

Let's practice. Dana is a senior designer at a marketing agency. For two years, whenever the account manager, Rob, brings her an "urgent" client revision on Friday afternoon, she stays late and finishes it. Lately the Friday requests have multiplied, and several of them had been sitting in his inbox since Tuesday. Dana has complained in meetings that people need to plan better, and she once told Rob she wouldn't keep doing this. Then she stayed late anyway. Dana also reviews the junior designers' work every Thursday morning, and the juniors have built their weeks around it. Now she's frustrated, and she's thinking about becoming harder to read across the board so nobody takes her for granted. Pause the video and work through these five questions.
