The Prisoner's Dilemma again

When predictability supports cooperation

Played once

Each player may have an incentive to defect. Mutual cooperation would be better.

Played repeatedly

Today's decision affects tomorrow's. Defect today and I may punish you next time.

Known retaliation

You know I'll always retaliate against defection. That predictability can support cooperation.

Internomics · Module 4 — Manage Predictability

Remember the Prisoner's Dilemma? Two players may each have an incentive to defect even though mutual cooperation would be better for both. Now imagine they play repeatedly. Suppose you know I will always retaliate against defection. My response is completely predictable. That sounds like a weakness. But here it may support cooperation. You know exactly what defecting will cost you. So you don't do it. The same predictability that makes you exploitable in one game can make you trustworthy in another.
