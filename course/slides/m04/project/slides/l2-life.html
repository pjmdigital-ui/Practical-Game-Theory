Internomics · Module 4 — Manage Predictability

This is where we need to be careful. The rule people reach for is "always keep people guessing." That's not what game theory says. In many relationships excessive unpredictability is destructive. Imagine your spouse never knew whether you'd keep your promises. Imagine your employees never knew whether you'd enforce a rule. That's not strategic sophistication. That's a lack of reliability. So the better question is this. Where does predictability help cooperation? And where does it create an opportunity for exploitation? Hold that question. Now let's go back to rock-paper-scissors and play it out.
