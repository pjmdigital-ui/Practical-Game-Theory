Internomics · Module 4 — Manage Predictability

Before we go any further, I want to be clear about what this module is not. It's not about being deceptive. It isn't about lying. It isn't about manipulating people. And it certainly isn't about becoming erratic just so nobody knows what you're going to do. The principle is much more subtle than that: don't allow your patterns to become so predictable that other people can consistently use them against you. Sometimes that means being unpredictable. Sometimes it means being extremely predictable. The skill we're building is knowing when each one is strategically valuable.
