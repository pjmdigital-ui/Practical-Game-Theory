Internomics · Module 4 — Manage Predictability

Let me be clear about what this module is not. It isn't about lying. It isn't about manipulating people. And it isn't about becoming erratic so nobody knows what you'll do. The principle is this. Don't allow your patterns to become so predictable that other people can consistently use them against you. The word that matters is "against." Sometimes the answer is to become less predictable in one narrow area. Sometimes it's to become extremely predictable. Here's the misunderstanding to watch for. The goal is not to keep everyone guessing. Now let's see how a rule gets created.
