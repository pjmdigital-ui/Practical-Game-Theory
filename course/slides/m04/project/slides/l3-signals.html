Internomics · Module 4 — Manage Predictability

Here's one of the hidden costs of predictable behavior. Your actions can reveal information you didn't intend to reveal. I call that the predictability tax. Every action you take communicates something. If you always answer emails immediately, people may infer you're highly available. If you always agree to last-minute requests, they infer your schedule is flexible. If you always threaten to quit but never do, people learn your threats aren't credible. And think about a poker player who bluffs a lot. At first, opponents fold. The bluff works. But eventually they notice, "He's bluffing a lot," and they start calling. The same bluff stops working. What changed? Not the words. Not the cards. The other players' beliefs changed. They learned your pattern.
