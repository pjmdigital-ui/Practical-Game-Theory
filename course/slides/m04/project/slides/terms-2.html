Internomics · Module 4 — Manage Predictability

Four more. Strategic unpredictability: varying your behavior so another player can't simply optimize against a fixed pattern. That's legitimate in competition, and it's compatible with honesty. Manipulative unpredictability: unpredictability used to deceive or destabilize another person, rather than to prevent legitimate exploitation. That's the one we don't do. Reputation: accumulated evidence about your future behavior. Every repeated pattern teaches the other player something about you. And the feedback loop: action, response, learning, adaptation. Today's behavior changes tomorrow's game.
