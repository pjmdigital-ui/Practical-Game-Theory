Internomics · Module 4 — Manage Predictability

Here are four more. Strategic unpredictability means varying your behavior so another player can't simply optimize against a fixed pattern. That's legitimate in competition. It's also compatible with honesty. Manipulative unpredictability is unpredictability used to deceive or destabilize another person. It isn't about preventing legitimate exploitation. That's the one we don't do. Reputation is accumulated evidence about your future behavior. Every repeated pattern teaches the other player something about you. And the feedback loop runs from action to response. Then it runs to learning and adaptation. Today's behavior changes tomorrow's game.
