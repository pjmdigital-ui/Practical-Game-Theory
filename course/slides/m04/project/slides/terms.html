Internomics · Module 4 — Manage Predictability

Here are the key terms from this module. They're all in your workbook. Strategic predictability is when someone knows enough about your pattern to adjust and gain an advantage. They don't need to know your exact next move. A mixed strategy means choosing among actions by carefully chosen probabilities so an opponent can't exploit your pattern. It does not mean doing something random. The predictability tax is the hidden cost of predictable behavior. Your actions reveal information you never meant to reveal. That might be your limits. It might be how far someone needs to push. And an unintended rule is a rule other people learn from your repeated behavior. You never consciously chose it. "Interrupting her has no cost" is a perfect example.
