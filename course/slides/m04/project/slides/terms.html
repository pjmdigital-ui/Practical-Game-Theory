Internomics · Module 4 — Manage Predictability

Here are the key terms from this module, and they're all in your workbook. Strategic predictability: when someone knows enough about your pattern to adjust and gain an advantage, even without knowing your exact next move. A mixed strategy: choosing among actions by carefully chosen probabilities so an opponent can't exploit your pattern. Not doing something random. The predictability tax: the hidden cost of predictable behavior, where your actions reveal information you never meant to reveal, like your limits or how far someone needs to push. And an unintended rule: a rule other people learn from your repeated behavior, even though you never consciously chose it. "Interrupting her has no cost."
