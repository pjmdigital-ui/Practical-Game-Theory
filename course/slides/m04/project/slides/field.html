Internomics · Module 4 — Manage Predictability

Here's your assignment for this week. Run a pattern log. Pick one recurring interaction. It could be a weekly meeting. It could be a regular request from a colleague. It could be a household routine or a client relationship. Each time it happens, write down three things. What you did. How the other person responded. And what they might have learned about you from that exchange. By midweek, complete the Predictability Audit. Choose one of the four moves. Then carry it out deliberately for the rest of the week. Stay honest the whole way. No false statements. At the end of the week record three more things. First, the rule you think they'd learned before the week started. Second, any sign that they noticed the change. That might look like testing or surprise. It might look like adjustment or relief. Third, whether the change made things better for you or for them or for both of you. Next week we'll debrief. How accurate was your guess about what they'd learned? Did they adapt to your new pattern? And where in your life would more consistency create trust? Where would more flexibility create strategic room?
