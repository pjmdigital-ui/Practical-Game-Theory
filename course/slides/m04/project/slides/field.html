Internomics · Module 4 — Manage Predictability

Here's your assignment for this week: run a pattern log. Pick one recurring interaction. A weekly meeting, a regular request from a colleague, a household routine, a client relationship. Each time it happens, write down what you did, how the other person responded, and what they might have learned about you from that exchange. By midweek, complete the Predictability Audit, choose one of the four moves, and carry it out deliberately for the rest of the week. Stay honest the whole way. Then record the rule you think they'd learned before the week started, any sign they noticed the change, whether that was testing, surprise, adjustment, or relief, and whether the change made things better for you, for them, or for both of you.
