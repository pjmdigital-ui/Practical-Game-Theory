Internomics · Module 4 — Manage Predictability

Let me show you the tax being collected. Imagine a poker player who bluffs frequently. At first his opponents fold. So he does it again and again. Eventually the other players notice. Now they call more often. The same bluff stops working. What changed? The other players' beliefs changed. They learned his pattern. Here's the misunderstanding to watch for. When a tactic stops working, most of us blame the tactic. Often it simply became predictable. So ask a different question. What have they learned about me?
