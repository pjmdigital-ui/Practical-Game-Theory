Internomics · Module 11 — Change the Game

Here's a simple principle. Incentives speak louder than mission statements. An organization can say quality matters but reward volume. It can say tell us the truth but retaliate against bad news. People live inside the payoffs. The reward shows up in every paycheck and every performance review. Think about that fourth row. If you've seen what happened to the last person who delivered bad news, what's your smart move? Silence. The game made silence the safe choice.
