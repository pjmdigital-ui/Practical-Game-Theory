Internomics · Module 11 — Change the Game

Most of the game theory we've explored so far asks something like: given the game, what are the players likely to do? Mechanism design reverses the question. It asks: given the outcome we want, how should we design the game? This reversal became important enough that Leonid Hurwicz, Eric Maskin, and Roger Myerson received the 2007 Nobel Prize in Economic Sciences for laying the foundations of mechanism design theory. Now, you don't need the mathematics. The mental shift is what matters. Instead of "Here are the rules, what should I do?", we ask, "Here is the outcome we want. What rules would make that outcome more likely?" That's a radically different way to think, and it's the thread running through this whole module.
