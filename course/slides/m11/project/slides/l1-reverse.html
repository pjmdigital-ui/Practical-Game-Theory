Mechanism design reverses the question

From

"Given the game, what will the players do?"

To

"Given the outcome we want, how should we design the game?"

You don't need the mathematics. The mental shift is what matters: here is the outcome we want. What rules would make it more likely?

Internomics · Module 11 — Change the Game

Most of the game theory we've explored so far asks one question. Given the game, what are the players likely to do? Mechanism design reverses it. Given the outcome we want, how should we design the game? On the left the rules are a given. On the right the outcome is the given and the rules become the variable. And mechanism design isn't only for economists and governments. You design games every time you set a deadline or a bonus or a house rule. This idea mattered enough to earn a Nobel Prize.
