Example · Designer and player

Stop fighting a willpower contest

The situation

"I need more discipline." Your phone sits beside you with notifications on. The hard work takes six steps to begin.

What happened

Remove the app. Turn off notifications. Make the productive action easier. Schedule someone to work with you.

The lesson

You no longer need a better decision every five minutes. You redesigned the choice environment.

Internomics · Module 11 — Change the Game

Sometimes you're the designer and the player. You keep telling yourself you need more discipline. But inspect the game first. Your phone is beside you. Notifications are on. The difficult work takes six steps just to begin. Distraction costs nothing. Focus costs effort before you've even started. Instead of a willpower contest, change the environment. Turn off notifications. Make the productive action easier and the distracting action harder. Use a commitment device like we discussed in Module Eight. Design for the behavior you're likely to have. Not the behavior you wish you had. Let's pull these examples together.
