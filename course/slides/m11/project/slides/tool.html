Internomics · Module 11 — Change the Game

Your tool for this module is the Game-Change Audit. Use it when the same bad outcome keeps returning after better arguments, better wording, more motivation, and more pressure haven't fixed it. Step one: name the recurring problem, what you've already tried inside the current game, and your current character explanation. Step two: what does the game actually reward, not what the policy claims, and what does it punish? What metric are people optimizing? Step three: inspect the levers in the table: rules, payoffs, information, who decides, timing, options, players, observability and feedback, and the default. Note what's easy, what's hard, and whether something should be removed. Step four: stress-test. How could a smart player game your new version? Then what? Step five: the ethics check. Step six: name the mechanism you think is producing the behavior, and make the smallest change that tests it. And if exit is on the table, write down what you'd lose.
