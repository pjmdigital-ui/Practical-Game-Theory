Observability

Change what can be seen. Trace defects to the stage that made them.

Defaults

Internomics · Module 11 — Change the Game

Players first. Two people can't agree on the value of a property. So bring in an independent appraiser. Observability is next. If defects can be traced back to the stage where they happened, that's a different game. Feedback is about distance. A lesson that arrives six months later is much harder to learn from. The two dark cards subtract. Sometimes the better design is removing the distorting incentive. And sometimes the best move is to stop playing. That's exit. When you last fixed a recurring problem, did you add something or remove something? Let's start with defaults.
