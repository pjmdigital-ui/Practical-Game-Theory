Internomics · Module 11 — Change the Game

We have a strong tendency to explain behavior through character. Someone isn't cooperating so we say they're selfish. Employees aren't taking initiative so we say they're lazy. But strategic thinking asks one more question. What does the game make attractive? Behavior emerges from the interaction between people and structure. I'm not saying structure explains everything. I'm saying inspect the game before you decide the player is the problem. If you replace the person and keep the game, you may get the same behavior from someone new.
