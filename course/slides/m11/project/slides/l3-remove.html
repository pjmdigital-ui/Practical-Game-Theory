Internomics · Module 11 — Change the Game

We often assume game design means adding rewards. Sometimes the better design is removing the thing that's creating the distortion. Before, employees take pride in good work and help each other. Then management introduces a competitive bonus for every tiny output. After, coworkers stop helping one another. The organization didn't need stronger motivation. It introduced the wrong game. So the solution could be to simplify. Remove the competition. Reduce the measurement. Restore discretion. What rule or reward would you remove tomorrow if you could? Now for the most dramatic way to change a game.
