Internomics · Module 11 — Change the Game

Throughout this course we've used four principles. Truth. Autonomy. Dignity. Informed choice. When you design the game you usually have more power than the people playing it. So a good mechanism asks four more questions. Clarity. Does the player understand the game? Choice. Do they keep meaningful choice? Openness. Are important facts hidden? Respect. Does it treat people as decision-makers or as objects to be manipulated? A dark pattern is still game design. It's just serving the wrong ethical objective. If people could see exactly how your system works, would they feel respected? Now informed choice in practice.
