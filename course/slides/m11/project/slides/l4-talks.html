Internomics · Module 11 — Change the Game

Remember Module Three and its question. Then what? You change a rule. People respond. They learn how the rule works. They adapt. They may find a way to produce the metric without producing the outcome you wanted. So game design isn't set rule and get outcome. Designers are players too. Once you create rules, everyone responds strategically to them. In the practice exercise engineers learn that reopened tickets count as new. They adapt by splitting issues into extra tickets. Announcing a policy is the start of a new round. Let's see how the system responds.
