Internomics · Module 11 — Change the Game

Remember Module Three: then what? Mechanism design needs the same discipline. You change a rule. Then what? People respond. Then what? They learn how the rule works. Then what? They adapt, and they may find a way to produce the metric without producing the outcome you wanted. So design isn't "set rule, get outcome." It's set rule, players respond, players learn, players adapt, system evolves. Which means designers are players too. You don't stand outside the game controlling everything. The system talks back. You redesign compensation expecting more collaboration, and people form small alliances instead. You introduce a productivity metric, productivity rises, and quality collapses. That's data. The wrong response is "Why aren't people behaving according to my design?" The right one is "What did I misunderstand about the game?" Then make the smallest change that tests your new hypothesis: design, observe, test, update, adapt.
