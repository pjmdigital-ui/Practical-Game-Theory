Internomics · Module 11 — Change the Game

Here are the key terms from this module; they're all in your workbook. Mechanism design: starting from the outcome you want and asking what rules would make it more likely, instead of taking the rules as given. Incentive compatibility: a design where following the desired strategy lines up with each participant's own incentives, so people don't have to fight their self-interest to do the right thing. A strategy-proof mechanism: a system designed so that truthfully reporting your preferences is a good strategy, so you don't need to outsmart the rules. Metric versus goal: the measurement, like test scores, call time, or clicks, isn't necessarily the outcome you care about, and once payoffs attach to it, people may play the metric. And the principal-agent problem: the person acting on your behalf has somewhat different incentives and often knows more about their own actions than you do.
