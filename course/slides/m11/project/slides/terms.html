Internomics · Module 11 — Change the Game

Here are the key terms from this module. They're all in your workbook. Mechanism design means starting from the outcome you want. Then you ask what rules would make it more likely. You stop taking the rules as given. Incentive compatibility is a design where the desired strategy lines up with each participant's own incentives. People don't have to fight their self-interest to do the right thing. A strategy-proof mechanism is a system where truthfully reporting your preferences is a good strategy. You don't need to outsmart the rules. Metric versus goal reminds you that the measurement isn't necessarily the outcome you care about. Test scores aren't learning. Call time isn't a solved problem. Once payoffs attach to a metric, people may play the metric. And the principal-agent problem is when the person acting on your behalf has somewhat different incentives. They also often know more about their own actions than you do.
