Internomics · Module 11 — Change the Game

Here's your assignment for this week: run one small, ethical game redesign. Choose a recurring problem you actually have the authority to influence: a household routine, a team process, a client arrangement, or your own work environment. Complete the Game-Change Audit, identify the mechanism you believe is producing the unwanted behavior, and implement the smallest change that tests it. If other people are affected, explain the new game clearly, or better, design it with them. Each day, write a few lines. What behavior did you see under the new structure? Did anyone respond in a way you didn't predict, including finding ways to game the new rule? What became easier, and what became harder? Next week, come back ready to say what the system talked back with, and what it revealed that you'd misunderstood.
