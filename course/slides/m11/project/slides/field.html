Internomics · Module 11 — Change the Game

Here's your assignment for this week. Run one small and ethical game redesign. Choose a recurring problem you actually have the authority to influence. It could be a household routine. It could be a team process or a client arrangement. It could be your own work environment. Complete the Game-Change Audit. Identify the mechanism you believe is producing the unwanted behavior. Then make the smallest change that tests it. If other people are affected, explain the new game clearly. Better still, design it with them. Each day write a few lines. What behavior did you see under the new structure? Did anyone respond in a way you didn't predict? That includes finding ways to game the new rule. What became easier? And what became harder? Next week come back ready to say what the system talked back with. And what it revealed that you'd misunderstood.
