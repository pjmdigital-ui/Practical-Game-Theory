The overlooked move

Exit is also a strategic move, but exit has payoffs too.

Not every game deserves another move. Before you leave, count what you'd lose: money, relationships, status, future upside.

Internomics · Module 11 — Change the Game

There's one more form of game change that people often overlook. You can sometimes stop playing. Imagine you've spent years optimizing your behavior inside a relationship, an organization, or a market where the incentives stay destructive, commitments aren't credible, trust can't be restored, and you lack the authority to redesign the rules. At some point, ask: why am I trying to become better at this game? If a negotiation can't produce an acceptable agreement, use your outside option. If a conversation becomes abusive, end it. But leaving isn't magically correct. You may lose money, relationships, status, opportunity. So go back to the earlier models: what are the payoffs? Then what? And what future are you giving up? That's why these models aren't isolated tricks. They work together.
