Internomics · Module 11 — Change the Game

There's one more form of game change that people often overlook. You can sometimes stop playing. Suppose the incentives remain destructive. Trust cannot be restored. And you lack the authority to redesign the rules. So why are you trying to become better at this game? If a negotiation can't produce an acceptable agreement, use your outside option. But exit carries costs. You may lose money or relationships or future upside. Some people never consider exit. Others treat it as the answer to every frustration. Both skip the analysis.
