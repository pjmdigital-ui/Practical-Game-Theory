Internomics · Module 11 — Change the Game

I want to be careful here. Incentives don't explain everything. People have values. People sometimes do what's right even when it costs them. If you repeatedly create a payoff for a behavior, don't be shocked when more of it appears. Good people can survive a bad game. They shouldn't have to. Here's the misunderstanding to watch for. Some hear that incentives matter and conclude values don't. Others conclude the reverse. Both are wrong. The designer's job is to stop putting values and incentives at war.
