Internomics · Module 11 — Change the Game

In 2007 Leonid Hurwicz, Eric Maskin and Roger Myerson received the Nobel Prize in Economic Sciences. They laid the foundations of mechanism design theory. This isn't a clever trick I made up. Hurwicz introduced key ideas like incentive compatibility. Maskin developed implementation theory. Myerson extended mechanism design to situations involving private information. What I want you to take from their work is the stance. They treated the rules of an interaction as something you can design. Not as weather that just happens to you. Before we design anything though, we need to stop blaming the wrong thing.
