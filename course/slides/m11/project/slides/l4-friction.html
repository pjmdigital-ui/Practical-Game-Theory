Friction is part of the game

Being able to make a choice hard doesn't mean you should.

Signing up

One click.

Cancelling

Twenty clicks. Maybe a phone call and a retention talk too.

The effect

Technically they can leave. Strategically you've made one choice costly.

Internomics · Module 11 — Change the Game

Here's a lever that deserves special care. Friction. How easy or hard each choice is made to be. Suppose you want customers to cancel a subscription honestly if they no longer want it. Then cancellation requires eight screens and a phone call. In practice some may give up and keep paying for something they don't want. Nobody lied to them. The design did the work. The fact that you can make something strategically difficult does not mean you should. Friction isn't neutral. Is the easy path also the honest path? That question leads us into ethical design.
