Internomics · Module 11 — Change the Game

This doesn't only apply to governments and companies. Imagine a couple who argue about household responsibilities every Saturday: "Who's doing what?" Then conflict. You could work endlessly on tone, listening, wording, and those things matter. But maybe the deeper solution is to stop renegotiating the same game every Saturday. You handle these, I handle those, we rotate this one, and if something unusual comes up, here's how we decide. Or a client who keeps asking for work outside the agreed scope. Every request becomes "Will Paul do it this time?" Introduce a defined scope, a change-request process, and a fee for extra work. I haven't become less generous; I've reduced the ambiguity, and that protects the relationship from resentment. And sometimes you're the designer and the player. Your phone's beside you, notifications on, while the hard work takes six steps to start. You can spend your life in a willpower contest, or you can change the environment.
