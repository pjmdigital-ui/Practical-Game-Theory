Internomics · Module 11 — Change the Game

Let's talk about the mistakes I see most often. There are eight of them. Here are the first four. Mistake one is giving another speech when the structure rewards the opposite behavior. It's the most natural move in the world. Something goes wrong and we call a meeting. But think back to the sales team at the start of this module. The workshop said cooperate. The rankings said compete. The rankings won. So before the next workshop, ask what the system actually pays for. Mistake two is blaming character before inspecting the game. Ordinary people often respond predictably to a badly built system. Ask what the game makes attractive. But don't swing to the other extreme. Structure doesn't explain everything. People still have values and still make choices. Mistake three is confusing the metric with the goal. Remember the teachers in Kenya. Scores rose and learning didn't. So ask what behavior actually moves the number. Then ask what hard-to-measure work may be losing effort. Mistake four is redesigning everything at once. Once you start seeing systems it's tempting to rebuild them from scratch. Be careful. Human systems are interconnected. Change one incentive and you may affect trust and information. You may affect status and coordination and reputation too. So start with a diagnosis. What specific mechanism seems to be producing the unwanted behavior? Then make the smallest change that can test that idea. Observe. Update. Adapt. Here's a question for you. Which of these four have you made in the last month? Be honest.
