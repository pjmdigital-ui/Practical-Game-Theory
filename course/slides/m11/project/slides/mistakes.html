Internomics · Module 11 — Change the Game

A few mistakes to watch for. First, giving another speech when the structure rewards the opposite behavior. Before the next workshop, ask what the system actually pays for. Second, blaming character before inspecting the game. Ordinary people often respond predictably to a badly built system, though structure doesn't explain everything. Third, confusing the metric with the goal: ask what behavior actually moves the number, and what hard-to-measure work may lose effort. Fourth, redesigning everything at once. Systems are interconnected, so make the smallest testable change, then observe and update. And a few more in your workbook: assuming "set rule, get outcome," designing only for saints or only for monsters, always adding rules when you should remove one, treating design as control, and either ignoring exit or treating it as automatically right.
