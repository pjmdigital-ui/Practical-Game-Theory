Internomics · Module 11 — Change the Game

That gives us one of the most important questions in this module. What exactly am I rewarding? What behavior actually improves the metric? Suppose a teacher's bonus depends on test scores. You rewarded higher scores on a particular measurement. The teacher can raise the score by teaching better or by drilling the test. Your reward can't tell the difference. Reward the number of calls and ask whether people could make useless calls. This isn't cynical. You're stress-testing the game before real people play it.
