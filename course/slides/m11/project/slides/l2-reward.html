Internomics · Module 11 — Change the Game

That gives us one of the most important questions in this module: what exactly am I rewarding? Not what do I intend to reward. What behavior actually improves the metric? Suppose you tell a teacher, "Your bonus depends on your students' test scores." What do you want? Probably better education. What did you actually reward? Higher scores on a particular measurement. Those aren't necessarily the same thing. So walk it forward. A bonus for number of calls: could people make useless calls? A reward for bugs fixed: could people generate easy bugs? A reward for customer reviews: could employees ask only happy customers? A reward for quarterly profit: could managers sacrifice long-term investment? The question works anywhere you've attached a payoff to a number.
