Internomics · Module 11 — Change the Game

What the cake rule achieves is called incentive compatibility. Hurwicz's work made it a foundational idea of mechanism design. The desired strategy lines up with each participant's own incentives. People don't have to fight their self-interest to do the right thing. The left box forces everyone to fight their incentives. The right box makes the desirable action compatible with their incentives. The cutter still wants the bigger piece. The mechanism uses that preference. This isn't bribing people to behave. The cake rule doesn't pay anyone anything. It only changes the order of moves. That brings us to the takeaway.
