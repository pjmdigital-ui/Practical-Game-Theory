Internomics · Module 11

Change the Game

Do I need a better move—or a better game?

Paul Mascetta

Welcome to Module Eleven. This is the last of the eleven mental models. For ten modules we've learned to see more clearly inside a strategic interaction. What game am I in? What does everyone actually want? How will they respond? What pattern have we created? Those questions help you make better moves. But eventually you reach a more powerful question. Do I need a better move or a better game? Sometimes you can become extraordinarily skilled at playing a game that should never have been designed that way in the first place. The incentives are wrong. The rules produce the very behavior you're trying to stop. That's what this module is about.
