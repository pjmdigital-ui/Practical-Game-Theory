Internomics · Module 11 — Change the Game

Changing an incentive can change behavior a lot. That's exactly why designing incentives is dangerous. Imagine I tell my team I want higher-quality leads. Then I pay them based entirely on the number of leads generated. What will tend to increase? Probably the number of leads. Quality may fall. Then I accuse the team of missing the point. But the scoreboard told them what the point was. Once a measurement is tied to money or status, people begin optimizing against it. Let's look closer at that gap between the metric and the goal.
