Don't incentivize one task and destroy another

Most jobs don't contain one output. A teacher might need to:

Teach knowledge

Develop reasoning

Manage behavior

Encourage curiosity

Provide feedback

Mentor students

Internomics · Module 11 — Change the Game

The second family comes from Bengt Holmström and Paul Milgrom. They called it multitask incentives. Most jobs contain many outputs. Some are much easier to measure than others. A teacher teaches knowledge and develops reasoning. They encourage curiosity and mentor students. Only some of that shows up on a test. Strongly reward only the easiest one to measure and effort can shift away from the rest. The game now pays for one box. People assume a strong incentive adds effort on top. Often it moves effort instead. That leads to the most important question in this lesson.
