Internomics · Module 11 — Change the Game

Picture a sales organization. Management keeps telling people to collaborate and share leads. But every month the company publicly ranks each salesperson against the others. Then management is shocked that salespeople guard information. So they hold a workshop on teamwork. Nothing changes. Management says cooperate. The incentive system says your coworkers are your competitors. Guess which message wins. So the question becomes what our system actually rewards. That's the beginning of game design. Where in your life is the speech saying one thing while the game says another? Let's look at the signs.
