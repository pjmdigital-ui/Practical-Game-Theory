Internomics · Module 11 — Change the Game

Let's practice. Dana manages a twelve-person software support team. A leaderboard ranks engineers by tickets closed per week, and the top three each quarter get a bonus and first choice of vacation weeks. Dana keeps saying customer satisfaction and knowledge-sharing matter most, and she's run two mentoring workshops. Yet senior engineers rarely help juniors, complex tickets sit untouched until someone is assigned them, and customers keep reopening tickets marked "resolved" within days. Reopened tickets are counted as new tickets. Escalations need Dana's personal sign-off, and she's often in meetings until late afternoon. Last month two engineers were caught splitting single customer issues into several tickets. Dana's boss suggests firing them and hiring people with better values.
