Internomics · Module 11 — Change the Game

This principle applies everywhere. The metric is not the goal. You want student learning and you measure test scores. And my favorite. You want a healthy relationship and you measure who apologized first. Each measurement is a stand-in for the goal. Once you attach a payoff to the number, people may play the metric instead of pursuing the objective. If a couple keeps score on apologies, the apology stops being about repair. It becomes a move in a contest. What number are you watching at home? Next come two families of problems that explain why good people drift.
