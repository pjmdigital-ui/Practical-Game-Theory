Internomics · Module 11 — Change the Game

This principle applies everywhere. You want student learning; you measure test scores. You want useful customer service; you measure call duration. You want good content; you measure clicks. You want productive employees; you measure hours online. You want a healthy relationship; you measure who apologized first. The measurement may be useful. I'm not telling you to stop measuring. But the measurement is not necessarily the goal. And the moment the metric affects payoffs, people may begin playing the metric instead of pursuing the underlying objective. Look at your own life for a second. What number are you watching at work, or at home, that has quietly started to stand in for the thing you actually care about?
