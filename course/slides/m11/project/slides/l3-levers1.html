Six levers that change the game

Rules

Define what players can do. A clear handoff from sales to customer success.

Payoffs

Change what pays. A new payment schedule. A refundable deposit.

Information

Change who knows what and when. Dashboards. Transparent criteria.

Timing

Change who moves when. Sequential moves. Feedback now instead of yearly.

Options

Create new moves. Trial periods. Partial agreements.

Who decides

Assign decision rights. Partner A runs marketing. Partner B runs operations.

Internomics · Module 11 — Change the Game

Let's walk the levers. Rules first. Two departments keep fighting over who owns a customer after the sale. A defined handoff removes one source of strategic ambiguity. Payoffs next. A vendor has no reason to fix a problem after receiving full payment. So change the payment schedule. Information is about who knows what and when. Timing is about the sequence.
