Internomics · Module 11 — Change the Game

Here's what you'll be able to do by the end of this module. First, tell a "better move" problem from a "better game" problem. You'll do it by asking what the structure actually rewards rather than what it claims to reward. Second, explain mechanism design and incentive compatibility in plain language. We'll use a cake to do it. Third, predict how a metric or incentive could be gamed. You'll also spot the valuable work that's harder to measure and may lose effort. Fourth, find the design levers in a recurring problem. Rules. Payoffs. Information. Timing. Options. Authority. Players. Observability. Defaults. Feedback. Friction. Exit. And fifth, design a small and ethical change to a game you're part of. Then plan how you'll watch what happens.
