Internomics · Module 11 — Change the Game

Here's what you'll be able to do by the end of this module. First, tell the difference between a "better move" problem and a "better game" problem, by asking what the structure actually rewards rather than what it claims to reward. Second, explain mechanism design and incentive compatibility in plain language, using a cake. Third, predict how a metric or incentive could be gamed, and how effort might drift away from valuable work that's harder to measure. Fourth, identify the design levers in a recurring problem: rules, payoffs, information, timing, options, authority, players, observability, defaults, feedback, friction, and exit. And fifth, design a small, ethical, testable change to a game you're part of, and plan how you'll watch what happens.
