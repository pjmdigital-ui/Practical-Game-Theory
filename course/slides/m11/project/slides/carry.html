The question to carry

Do I need a better move—or a better game?

Internomics · Next: Module 12 — Strategic Judgment

So here's the question to carry. You've tried better arguments. Better wording. More motivation. More discipline. More pressure. And the same problem keeps coming back. Ask yourself this. Do I need a better move or a better game? Then look at the rules and the payoffs. Look at the information and the timing. Sometimes the smartest player in the room isn't the one who finds the brilliant move inside the existing game. It's the one who realizes we don't have to keep playing it this way. That's the last of the eleven models. But knowing eleven concepts isn't the goal. The real skill is knowing which one matters and when. That's judgment. And that's where we finish in Module Twelve.
