Example · The client and the scope

"Will Paul do it this time?"

The situation

A client keeps asking for work outside the agreed scope. Every request turns into a debate.

What happened

A clearly defined scope. A change-request process. A fee for additional work.

The lesson

Less ambiguity isn't less generosity. It protects the relationship from resentment.

Internomics · Module 11 — Change the Game

Here's one from my own work. A client repeatedly asks for work outside the agreed scope. If I say yes sometimes and no other times, the client learns that asking always pays. So I change the game. I introduce a clearly defined scope. I add a change-request process. And I attach a fee for additional work. I haven't become less generous. I've reduced the ambiguity. Some people feel that rules and fees are cold. Often it's the lack of structure that hurts the relationship. Now for the trickiest case. You're both the designer and the player.
