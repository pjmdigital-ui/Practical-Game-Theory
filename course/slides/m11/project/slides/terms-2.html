Internomics · Module 11 — Change the Game

Four more. Multitask incentives: when a job has several valuable tasks, strongly rewarding the most measurable one can pull effort away from the others. A default: what happens when nobody chooses. It's itself a rule, and it shapes outcomes. Friction: how easy or hard each choice is made to be. Friction is part of the design, and it carries ethical weight. And exit: stopping play when the game is destructive and you can't redesign it. That's a strategic choice too, with its own payoffs and its own costs.
