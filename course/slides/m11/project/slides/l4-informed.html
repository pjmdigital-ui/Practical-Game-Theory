Internomics · Module 11 — Change the Game

Suppose a customer is choosing between three plans. Ethical design makes the price understandable and the terms clear. It shows the tradeoffs and makes the cancellation rules easy to find. Manipulative design may bury the real price and hide the exit mechanism. Only one of the two respects informed choice. Our goal is not to get the outcome by any means available. It's to create a game in which good outcomes emerge without violating the people playing it. And disclosure buried on page nine isn't the same as understanding. Sometimes the rules must also protect the weaker player.
