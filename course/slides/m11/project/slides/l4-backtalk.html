Internomics · Module 11 — Change the Game

Here's what it looks like when the system talks back. You redesign compensation and expect more collaboration. Instead people form small alliances and exclude outsiders. That's data. You introduce a productivity metric. Productivity rises. Quality collapses. That's data too. The wrong response is to ask why people aren't behaving according to your design. The right response is to ask what you misunderstood about the game. That's strategic humility. So design. Observe. Test. Update. Adapt. Your first design is a hypothesis. Don't confuse control with design. Next let's ask who exactly we're designing for.
