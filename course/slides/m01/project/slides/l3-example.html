Internomics · Module 1 — See the Game

Let's make it concrete. You're negotiating with a contractor. You want a lower price; they want you to accept theirs. You could spend all your energy trying to persuade them. But step back and ask what happens if you both walk away. Maybe you have four other contractors you could call. Maybe they have ten customers waiting. Maybe neither of you has a particularly good alternative. Maybe one of you desperately needs this deal and the other doesn't. Listen to the conversation in each version and it might sound identical: same price, same back-and-forth. But each one is a different game, and the right move in one could be the wrong move in another.
