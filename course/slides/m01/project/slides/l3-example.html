Example · The contractor negotiation

What happens if you both walk away?

The situation

You want a contractor to lower their price. They want you to accept it. You could spend all your energy on persuasion.

What happened

You ask about walking away. Maybe you have four other contractors. Maybe they have ten customers waiting. Maybe neither of you has a good option.

The lesson

The conversation sounds the same in every version. Each version is a different game. The right move changes with it.

Internomics · Module 1 — See the Game

Let's make it concrete. You're negotiating with a contractor. You want them to lower their price. But there's another question. What happens if we both walk away? Maybe you have four other contractors you could call. Maybe they have ten other customers waiting. Maybe neither of you has a good alternative. The conversation might sound identical in each version. But each version is a different game. The right move in one could be the wrong move in another. Here's the mistake I see most. People prepare their arguments and never map the alternatives.
