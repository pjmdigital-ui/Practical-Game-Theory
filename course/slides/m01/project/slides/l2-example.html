Example · Negotiating with a salesperson

You think it's you vs. the salesperson

The situation

You're negotiating a price. In your head there are two players. It's you against the person across the counter.

What happened

You look wider. The salesperson may need a manager's approval. The company may have a pricing policy. Another customer may want the product. You may have three other suppliers.

The lesson

The visible conversation is only the surface. Some of the people who matter most aren't in the room.

Internomics · Module 1 — See the Game

One of the first mistakes we make is assuming the person we're talking to is the entire game. You're negotiating with a salesperson. In your head it's you versus the salesperson. Now look at the middle card. Maybe the salesperson needs a manager's approval. Maybe the company has a pricing policy. Maybe another customer wants the same product. And maybe you have three alternative suppliers. The visible conversation is only the surface. Sometimes the people who matter most aren't even in the room. So in your next negotiation ask yourself this. Who isn't here but still has a say? Let's lock that in.
