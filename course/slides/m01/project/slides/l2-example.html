Internomics · Module 1 — See the Game

One of the first mistakes we make is assuming the person we're talking to is the entire game. Imagine you're negotiating with a salesperson. In your head, it's you versus the salesperson. But maybe the salesperson has to get approval from a manager. Maybe the company has a pricing policy. Maybe another customer is interested in the same product. Maybe you have three alternative suppliers. Suddenly the strategic situation looks very different from the surface conversation. There are other people whose decisions affect the outcome, and some of the ones who matter most aren't even in the room.
