The starting premise

Other people are decision-makers. Their decisions affect your outcomes.

Not this

"Everyone is trying to manipulate everyone else."

This

"My choice makes sense only in light of theirs."

Nash's insight: each person's behavior has to be understood in relation to everyone else's.

Internomics · Module 1 — See the Game

I want to be clear about the premise here. It's easy to hear game theory and think everyone is trying to manipulate everyone else. That's the left side of the slide and it's not what I'm proposing. The premise is simpler. Other people are decision-makers. Their decisions affect your outcomes. John Nash gave us a way to say this precisely. Each player's behavior has to be considered in relation to the behavior of the other players. Once you see that you start asking "What about this situation makes their choice reasonable?" Now let's watch this premise turn an ordinary request into a game.
