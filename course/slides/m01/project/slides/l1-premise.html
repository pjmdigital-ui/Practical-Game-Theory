The starting premise

Other people are decision-makers, and their decisions affect your outcomes.

Not "everyone is manipulating everyone." Nash's insight: each person's behavior has to be understood in relation to everyone else's.

Internomics · Module 1 — See the Game

I want to be really clear about the premise here, because it's easy to hear "game theory" and think I'm handing you a cynical philosophy where everyone is trying to manipulate everyone else. That's not what I'm proposing. I'm proposing something much simpler: other people are decision-makers, and their decisions affect your outcomes. That's it. John Nash's equilibrium concept captures the core idea: each player's behavior has to be considered in relation to the behavior of the other players. Your choice makes sense only in light of theirs, and theirs only in light of yours. Once you recognize that, communication starts to look very different.
