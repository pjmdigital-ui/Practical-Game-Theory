Example · The raise request

Before you rehearse a word

The situation

You plan to say: "I've been here three years. I've increased sales by 30%. I'd like to discuss my compensation." Then you polish every word.

What happened

You ask what comes first. What does your boss want? What if they say no? What if you turn down a counteroffer? Who decides? Who else influences it?

The lesson

The "conversation" is really a structure. It has several players and options. Its consequences run well past your first sentence.

Internomics · Module 1 — See the Game

Let's make this concrete with the raise. You plan to walk in and say something like this. "I've been here for three years. I've increased sales by 30%. I'd like to discuss increasing my compensation." But look at the middle card. When you see the game first you ask different questions. What does your boss actually want? What happens if your boss says no? Who else might influence it? It's a structure with several players and options.
