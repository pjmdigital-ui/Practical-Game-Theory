Your move changes their decision problem. Their response changes yours.

Internomics · Module 1 — See the Game

Let's go back to the raise. Say you walk in and say, "I've been here three years, I've grown sales, and I'd like to discuss my compensation." Most communication books would start grading that sentence. Was it the right thing to say? Should you have built more rapport first? Fair questions. But game theory asks something that comes before all of them: what happens after I say it? Your boss might agree, reject it, ask for more information, make a counteroffer, delay, consult someone else, or test how badly you want it. And what happens if you turn down that counteroffer? Who actually has the authority here? The "conversation" turns into a structure with several players, options, and consequences that run well past your first sentence.
