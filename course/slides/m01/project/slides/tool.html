Internomics · Module 1 — See the Game

Your tool for this module is the Game Map. The full worksheet is in your workbook. Use it before any important interaction. Here's how it flows. Start at the top left. Write the interaction in one line. Just one. If you can't say it in one line you probably haven't decided what game you're in yet. Then work down the left column. Who are the players and what does each one want? Where are you aligned and where do you differ? What can each person actually do? What limits them? And which of those constraints are only stated and which do you know are real? Then information and timing. Who knows what? Who doesn't? Who moves first? Who responds? What can each person see before they choose? Now the right column. What happens if there's no agreement, for you and for them? Who needs whom? Who has alternatives? Then the then-what question. If I make this move, what will they probably do next? And what would I do after that? Finish with the final check. Could there be a game behind the conversation? Maybe it's about credit or trust. Maybe it's about status or authority or precedent. Is this a person problem or a game problem? And the last question is my favorite. What am I failing to notice about the game? That one comes straight out of the research we just looked at. Here's the key instruction. Write short answers. Don't aim for perfection. A few words per box is plenty. The goal is to train your attention, not to complete a form. Let's practice it on a real scenario.
