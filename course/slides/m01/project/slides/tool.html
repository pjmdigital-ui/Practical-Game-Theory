Internomics · Module 1 — See the Game

Your tool for this module is the Game Map, and the full worksheet is in your workbook. Use it before any important interaction. Write the interaction in one line. Then work through the nine questions: who the players are and what they want, where you're aligned and where you differ, what each person can actually do and what limits them, including which constraints are stated and which you know are real. Who knows what, who moves first, what happens with no agreement, and who needs whom. Then what: if I make this move, what will they do, and what would I do after that? Finish with the final check. Is there a game behind the conversation? Is this a person problem or a game problem? And what am I failing to notice? Write short answers. Don't aim for perfection. The goal is to train your attention, not to complete a form.
