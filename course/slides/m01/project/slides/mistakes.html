Common mistakes

Starting with the words

Rehearsing the perfect sentence before you understand the situation. Map the situation first.

One person = the whole game

Forgetting managers and policies. Forgetting partners and other options. Ask who can affect it.

Confusing wanting with being able

They may want to say yes and still not be able to. Look at their real options.

Blaming the person first

Calling someone selfish or lazy before you check the incentives and the history.

Internomics · Module 1 — See the Game

Let me flag the mistakes I see most often. Here are the first four. The first is starting with the words before mapping the situation. The second is treating the person in front of you as the whole game. Ask who can actually affect the outcome. The third is confusing wanting with being able. When you hear a no, look at their constraints before you push on their motives. The fourth is blaming the person first. Remember the manager whose employees "weren't cooperating."
