Internomics · Module 1 — See the Game

Let me flag the mistakes I see most. First, starting with the words: rehearsing the perfect sentence before you understand the situation it's going into. Second, treating the person in front of you as the whole game, when managers, policies, partners, spouses, other customers and alternative suppliers may all affect the outcome. Third, confusing wanting with being able: someone can want to give you the refund or the raise and still not be able to. And fourth, diagnosing a person problem when it's a game problem. A few more are in your workbook: assuming "game" means competition, treating a stated constraint as automatically true or automatically a lie, ignoring what happens if there's no agreement, getting paranoid about hidden motives, and running the Game Map like a rigid script. It's there to train your attention.
