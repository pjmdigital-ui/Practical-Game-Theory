Internomics · Module 1 — See the Game

Let me flag the mistakes I see most often with this material. There are eight in all. Here are the first four. The first is starting with the words. You rehearse the perfect sentence before you understand the situation it's going into. Remember that your words enter a system of incentives and information and alternatives. Map the situation first. Then write the sentence. The second is treating the person in front of you as the whole game. It's the salesperson mistake. Managers can affect the outcome. So can policies and partners and spouses. So can other customers and alternative suppliers. Ask who can actually affect it. The third is confusing wanting with being able. Someone may genuinely want to give you the refund or the raise. They may still not be able to. When you hear a no, look at their available actions and their constraints before you push on their motives. The fourth is blaming the person first. You decide someone is selfish or lazy before you check the structure. Before you reach that verdict, look at the incentives. Look at who owns the task. Look at the history. Remember the manager whose employees "weren't cooperating." The pay system may have been doing the talking. Here's a question for you. Which of these four do you fall into most often? Be honest.
