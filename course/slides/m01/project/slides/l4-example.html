Internomics · Module 1 — See the Game

Picture two people arguing about a business decision. On the surface, they're debating which strategy the company should use. But underneath that, there may be another game going on. Who gets final authority? Who gets the credit if this works? Who gets the blame if it fails? Is one of them trying to establish a precedent for future decisions? The visible disagreement is only one layer of the interaction. Again, this doesn't mean you should become obsessed with hidden motives. It means human interactions can carry several overlapping incentives at once. And if you only argue the strategy question, you may win the argument and still lose the game that actually mattered.
