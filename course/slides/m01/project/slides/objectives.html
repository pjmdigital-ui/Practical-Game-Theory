Internomics · Module 1 — See the Game

Here's what you'll be able to do by the end of this module. You'll be able to define a game the way game theorists do, and explain why a game doesn't need a winner and a loser. You'll be able to spot every player who can affect an outcome, including the people who aren't in the room. You'll separate what someone wants from what they can actually do, and what they say constrains them from what really does. You'll map an interaction using the nine questions of the Game Map. And before you decide someone is the problem, you'll know how to check whether the structure is the problem.
