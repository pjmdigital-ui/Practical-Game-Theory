Internomics · Module 1 — See the Game

Here's what you'll be able to do by the end of this module. First you'll define a game the way game theorists do. You'll see why a game doesn't need a winner and a loser. Second you'll spot every player who can affect an outcome. That includes the people who aren't in the room. Third you'll separate what someone wants from what they can actually do. You'll also separate what people say constrains them from what really does. Fourth you'll map an interaction with the nine questions of the Game Map. And fifth you'll learn to check one thing before you decide someone is the problem. Is the structure the problem instead? Let's begin with the first shift.
