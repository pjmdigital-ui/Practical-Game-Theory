The word player can sound cold when we're talking about relationships. It shouldn't. A player is simply a decision-maker inside the interaction. Your spouse is a player. So is your business partner or your customer or your boss. And notice the dark card at the end. You are a player too. Here's the misunderstanding to head off. Calling someone a player doesn't reduce them to a selfish calculation machine. It means you take their choices seriously. That's actually a respectful way to see people.
