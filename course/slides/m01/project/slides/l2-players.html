The word "player" can sound cold when we're talking about people. It shouldn't. A player is simply a decision-maker inside the interaction. Your spouse is a player. Your customer, your employee, your boss, your business partner. You are a player. And recognizing someone as a player doesn't reduce them to a selfish calculation machine. Real people care about money, but also about fairness. They care about winning, but also about relationships. They care about status, identity, loyalty, security, principles and reputation. That's exactly why we have to learn what matters to each person instead of assuming everyone wants the same thing. We'll go much deeper on that in Module Two.
