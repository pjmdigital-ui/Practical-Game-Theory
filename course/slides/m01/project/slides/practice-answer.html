A much bigger game than Dana vs. Marcus

Players: Dana and Marcus. Outside the room: the VP and the two pickers. Also the vendor's team and local employers.

Interests: Both want a working warehouse and good people. Dana needs visible progress. Marcus needs his on-time rates.

Constraints: The bonus punishes a slowdown. The vendor can't start for three weeks. Marcus has more alternatives than Dana assumed.

Then what: He complies on paper or escalates or starts interviewing. Pickers may resign. Each makes her update worse.

Diagnosis: Mostly a game problem. Ask what would make it workable. Check the vendor. Raise the bonus conflict with the VP.
