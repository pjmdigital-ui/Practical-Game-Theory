Internomics · Module 1 — See the Game

Imagine you're negotiating and the other person says, "I would love to give you that price, but I simply can't go below ten thousand dollars." You could immediately assume they're lying. Or you could ask a better question: what actually constrains them? Maybe nothing does, and they're bluffing. Maybe the company really does have a minimum price. Maybe they have the authority to go lower but don't want to. Those three situations sound exactly the same. Strategically, they're completely different games. So don't assume a stated constraint is a lie, and don't assume it's true. Separate what people say constrains them from what actually constrains them. We'll come back to this hard in later modules on credibility.
