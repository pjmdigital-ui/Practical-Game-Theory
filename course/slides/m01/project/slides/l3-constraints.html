Internomics · Module 1 — See the Game

Imagine the other person says "I simply can't go below ten thousand dollars." We either assume they're lying or accept it as fact. What actually constrains them? Look at the three cards. Maybe they're bluffing. Maybe the company really has a minimum price. Or maybe they could go lower but don't want to. Those three sound exactly the same. But strategically they're completely different games. If it's a bluff there's room to move. If it's a real floor then pushing harder just burns goodwill. So treat a stated constraint as a claim to understand. Now let's ask what happens if you don't reach a deal at all.
