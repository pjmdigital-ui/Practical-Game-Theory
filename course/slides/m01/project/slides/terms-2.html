Internomics · Module 1 — See the Game

A few more. Dependence: who needs whom, and who has alternatives. Power often comes from that structure rather than from personality. Simultaneous versus sequential games: choosing without seeing the other person's move, versus one person moving first and the other responding. The game behind the conversation: the structure beneath the stated topic, things like trust, status, credit, or precedent. Person problem versus game problem: blaming behavior on someone's character, versus recognizing that the structure of the interaction may be producing it. And the Game Map: the nine-question perception tool. Who, what, options, constraints, information, timing, alternatives, dependencies, and then what.
