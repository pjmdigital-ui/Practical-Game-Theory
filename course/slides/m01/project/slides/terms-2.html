Internomics · Module 1 — See the Game

A few more terms. Dependence means who needs whom and who has alternatives. Power often comes from that structure rather than from personality. Simultaneous versus sequential games is about timing. In a simultaneous game you choose without seeing the other person's move. In a sequential game one person moves first and the other responds. The game behind the conversation is the structure beneath the stated topic. It might be about trust. It might be about status or credit. It might be about setting a precedent. Person problem versus game problem is the diagnosis we just practiced. One view blames the behavior on someone's character. The other view recognizes that the structure of the interaction may be producing it. And the Game Map is your nine-question perception tool. Who. What. Options. Constraints. Information. Timing. Alternatives. Dependencies. Then what. Now let's look at what the research says about seeing the game.
