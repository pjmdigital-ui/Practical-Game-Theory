Some of you may be getting nervous. Isn't game theory a lot of math? There's a lot behind it but we're not doing that here. We're looking for the mental models underneath the mathematics. The goal isn't to calculate a Nash equilibrium before you ask your spouse where she wants to eat. The goal is to notice the line on this slide. We're not actually disagreeing about the restaurant. We're both worried about choosing something the other person won't like. You don't need the formal tools first. You need the habit of looking.
