Internomics · Module 1 — See the Game

Here's the question that comes first. What happens after I say it? Your boss hears your sentence and then makes a decision. They might agree or reject the request. They might counter or delay or consult someone else. Each branch puts you in a different spot. Your move changes their decision problem. And their response changes yours. Game theorists call this a sequential game. Later decisions depend on what happened earlier. You need one habit. Plan a move and then ask "Then what?" Here's the common mistake. We prepare for the yes we want and rarely for the other responses we might get. Let's lock in the lesson.
