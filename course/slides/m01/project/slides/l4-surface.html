Internomics · Module 1 — See the Game

Sometimes the obvious subject of a conversation isn't the whole problem. Someone says "I don't care about the money." But perhaps what really matters is recognition. Someone says "I just want to know why you didn't tell me." The deeper issue may be trust. There may be a second game running underneath. The lesson is not to assume everyone is lying about their motives. That's paranoia. The lesson is narrower. Don't assume the surface tells you everything about the structure. Stay curious.
