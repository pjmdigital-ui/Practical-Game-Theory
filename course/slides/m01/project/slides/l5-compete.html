Internomics · Module 1 — See the Game

I want to make this explicit one more time. A game doesn't have to be competitive. You can think about your marriage through game theory without believing your spouse is your opponent. Understanding the structure can make cooperation easier. When someone you love frustrates you the easy story is about them. Seeing the game gives you a second story. What about their position makes this choice reasonable? You can change a structure. In the book I put it this way: you begin to understand why another person's behavior makes sense from their position. That's empathy with a map. Let's test that with a manager's complaint.
