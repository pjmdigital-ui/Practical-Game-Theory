Why this is different from traditional advice

Change the question you start with

Instead of askingStart asking
What should I say?What situation am I saying it in?
How do I get them to agree?What does agreement mean to them?
How do I win this interaction?What outcome does each of us actually want?
Why did they respond that way?What about the structure made that response reasonable for them?
What should I do next?What will my move cause them to do next?

These questions don't give you a script. They give you a way of seeing.

Internomics · Module 1 — See the Game

Most communication instruction assumes the problem is mainly linguistic. Say the right thing the right way and you'll get the right result. But your words enter a system that already holds incentives and expectations and information. So look at the table. Instead of asking only "What should I say?" ask "What situation am I saying it in?" Instead of "How do I get them to agree?" ask "What does agreement mean to them?" Not one of the right-hand questions tells you what to say. They give you a way of seeing.
