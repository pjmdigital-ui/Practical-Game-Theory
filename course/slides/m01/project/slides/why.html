Internomics · Module 1 — See the Game

Most communication advice begins with a deceptively simple question: what should I say? Before you ask for a raise, you rehearse your words and your number. That matters. But it skips what comes first. What does your boss actually want? What happens if they say no? What alternatives does each of you have? Your words don't land in empty space. They enter a system that already holds incentives, expectations, information, constraints, alternatives, history, timing, and other people making their own decisions. And here's the part I love: seeing the game isn't cynical. It often makes cooperation easier, because you start to understand why the other person's behavior makes sense from where they stand.
