A simple test

Your boss texts: "We need to talk about your performance."

What do you know?

Not much.

What don't you know?

Almost everything important.

What does your boss want?

You don't know yet.

What options does each of you have?

Several.

What if it goes badly? Or well?

Potentially quite a lot.

Who else could influence it?

Worth finding out.

Most important: why did your boss choose those particular words?

Internomics · Module 1 — See the Game

Let's put this into practice. Your boss texts you. "We need to talk about your performance." Most people immediately start preparing a defense. But try seeing the game first. What do you know? Not much. What does your boss want? You don't know yet. What options does each of you have? Several. Who else could influence the outcome? Then the question at the bottom. Why did your boss choose those words? Asking changes how you walk in. You ask before you defend. Here's the common mistake. We treat an ambiguous message as a verdict and walk in braced for a fight.
