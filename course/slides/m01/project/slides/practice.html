Practice · Dana and Marcus

For six weeks operations head Dana has pushed warehouse lead Marcus to adopt a new scheduling system. He says it "isn't ready." She plans to announce next Monday with no exceptions. What she hasn't considered: his bonus is tied to on-time shipments. The vendor's onboarding team is booked for three weeks. Two veteran pickers would rather quit. Her VP wants a month-end update. Local warehouses are hiring leads.

Pause the video and answer:

  1. Who are the players? Who isn't in Thursday's meeting?
  2. Where are Dana's and Marcus's interests aligned? Where do they differ?
  3. What constraints shape Marcus's options? What are each side's alternatives?
  4. Dana says "Next Monday. No exceptions." What does Marcus do next? Then what?
  5. Is this mainly a person problem or a game problem?
