Practice · Dana and Marcus

For six weeks, operations head Dana has pushed warehouse lead Marcus to adopt a new scheduling system. He says it "isn't ready." She plans to announce: next Monday, no exceptions. What she hasn't considered: his bonus is tied to on-time shipments, the vendor's onboarding team is booked for three weeks, two veteran pickers would rather quit, her VP wants a month-end update, and local warehouses are hiring leads.

Pause the video and answer:

  1. Who are the players, and who isn't in Thursday's meeting?

Internomics · Module 1 — See the Game

Let's practice. Dana runs operations at a regional distribution company. For six weeks she's been pushing Marcus, the warehouse lead, to adopt a new scheduling system. Marcus keeps saying it isn't ready and his team doesn't have time to learn it. Dana has started seeing him as stubborn, and at Thursday's one-on-one she plans to tell him the switch is happening next Monday, no exceptions. Here's what she hasn't thought about. Marcus's quarterly bonus is tied to on-time shipment rates. The software vendor's onboarding team is booked for the next three weeks. Two of his most experienced pickers have quietly told him they'd rather leave than learn another system. Dana's own boss, the VP, approved the system and wants a progress update at the end of the month. And Marcus knows other warehouses nearby are hiring leads.
