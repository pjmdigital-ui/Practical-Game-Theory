Internomics · Module 1 — See the Game

A game doesn't have to involve a winner and a loser. Cooperation is a game. So is negotiation or a friendship or a family making a decision together. The economist Thomas Schelling made this central to his work. Many real interactions contain both conflicting and common interests. Two business partners want the company to win. They may still disagree about how. We're not using game theory as a cynical philosophy that says everyone is out to beat you. The aim is to see the structure clearly. Often that makes cooperation easier. Now let's name the premise underneath all of this.
