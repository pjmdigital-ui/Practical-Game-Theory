Internomics · Module 1 — See the Game

Here's one of the most important questions in any strategic interaction. What happens if we don't agree? Most of us treat that as a backup plan. But your alternatives don't just matter after a negotiation fails. They change the negotiation itself. If you have a decent option elsewhere you can afford to say no. Their effect depends on the structure of the game. Here's the common mistake. People think hard about their own fallback and never ask about the other person's. So ask both sides. What happens to me if there's no agreement? What happens to them? Next let's turn that into a question about power.
