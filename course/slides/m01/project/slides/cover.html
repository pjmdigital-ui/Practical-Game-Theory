See the Game

What's really going on here?

Paul Mascetta

Welcome to Module One. This is where we build the first mental model in the course. It's called See the Game. It comes with one question I want you to ask before every conversation that matters. What's really going on here? Most of us walk into important conversations thinking about our words. This module steps back. We look at the situation those words are about to enter. Who's involved? What do they want? What can they actually do? What happens if nothing gets resolved? By the end you'll have your first real tool. It's called the Game Map. Let's start with why this matters so much.
