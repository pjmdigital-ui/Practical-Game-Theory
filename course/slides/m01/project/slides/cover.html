See the Game

What's really going on here?

Paul Mascetta

Welcome to Module One. This is where we build the first mental model in the course: See the Game. And it comes with one question I want you to start asking before every conversation that matters: what's really going on here? Most of us walk into important conversations thinking about our words. In this module, we're going to step back and look at the situation those words are about to enter. Who's involved, what they want, what they can actually do, what happens if nothing gets resolved. By the end, you'll have your first real tool, the Game Map.
