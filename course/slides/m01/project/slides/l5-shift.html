The first mental shift

From

Me → What should I say? → Them

To

Me → My move → Their response → My next move → Their next response → Outcome

Internomics · Module 1 — See the Game

Before you met this way of thinking, you probably saw an interaction like this: me, what should I say, them. A straight line. Now I want you to start seeing it like this: me, my move, their response, my next move, their next response, outcome. And underneath that chain: their incentives, my incentives, our information, our expectations, our alternatives, our constraints, our history. The structure of the interaction. That's the game. And once you see it, you stop treating every interaction as a hunt for the perfect words. Sometimes the words really are the problem. But often they're just the most visible part of a much larger system.
