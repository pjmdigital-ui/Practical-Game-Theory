Internomics · Module 1 — See the Game

Let me pull this whole module into one picture. Before this you probably saw an interaction like the left side. All the effort goes into the words. Now see it like the right side. My move. Their response. My next move. Their next response. Outcome. It's a chain of decisions. Underneath sit incentives and information and alternatives and history. That's the game. Here's the common mistake. People hear this and decide to stop preparing what to say. Keep preparing but do it second. First see the game. Then choose your words for the game you're actually in.
