Internomics · Module 1 — See the Game

Here's what mixed interests means. Imagine two business partners. They both want the company to succeed. But they might disagree about pay or decision-making authority. They might disagree about how fast to grow or who deserves the credit. So are they cooperating or competing? The honest answer is both. Their interests are neither completely aligned nor completely opposed. That's much closer to most human interactions. The common mistake is to treat a mixed game like a pure contest. First ask where your interests are aligned. Then ask where they're different. Next let's separate what people want from what they can actually do.
