Interests are usually mixed

Instead of

"Who's right?"

First ask

"Where are our interests aligned, and where are they different?"

Internomics · Module 1 — See the Game

Think about two business partners. They both want the company to succeed. So are they cooperating? Yes. But they might also disagree about how much each should be paid, who has decision-making authority, how much to reinvest, how fast to grow, and who deserves the credit. Their interests are neither completely aligned nor completely opposed. They're mixed. And that's much closer to the structure of most human interactions. So when you're trying to understand one, don't immediately ask "Who's right?" or "Who's trying to win?" First ask: where are our interests aligned, and where are they different? That's a much more useful question.
