Example · The stalled project

"My coworker is procrastinating."

The situation

You and a coworker both want a project finished. It isn't moving. You think they're procrastinating.

What happened

They think you'll take over if they start. You both want the same outcome. Your expectations of each other create the problem.

The lesson

That isn't necessarily a character flaw. It may be a game. Once you see the structure you may be able to change it.

Internomics · Module 1 — See the Game

Here's a smaller version of the same idea. You and a coworker both want a project completed. But it isn't moving. From where you sit they're procrastinating. Now look at the middle card. From where they sit they think you're going to take over if they start. And because they wait you get more convinced they're procrastinating. You both want the same outcome. Your expectations about each other are creating the problem. That's not necessarily a character flaw. It may be a game. You could say "I won't take this over." That changes the game instead of blaming the player. Now let's put the whole shift into one picture.
