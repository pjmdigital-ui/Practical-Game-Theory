Internomics · Module 1 — See the Game

So here's your first tool: the Game Map. Nine questions. Who are the players, and who can actually affect the outcome? What does each person want, and where are your interests aligned or different? What options does each player really have? What constraints limit those choices? Who knows what, and who doesn't? Who moves first, who responds, and what can each person observe? What happens if there's no agreement? Who needs whom, and who has alternatives? And then what: if I make this move, what is the other person likely to do next? Now, this is not meant to be a rigid nine-step procedure you run every time you speak to someone. The point is to train your attention. With practice, you stop running the list and start noticing these things automatically. That's what a mental model does.
