Internomics · Module 1 — See the Game

So here's your first tool. It's called the Game Map. These nine questions help you pause and look at the structure. Who are the players? What does each person want? What can each one actually do? What limits those choices? Who knows what? Who moves first? What happens if there's no agreement? Who needs whom? And then what? Now here's the most important thing. This isn't a rigid nine-step procedure. The point is to train your attention. Over time you start noticing these things automatically. Keep your answers short. Let's try the map on a quick test.
