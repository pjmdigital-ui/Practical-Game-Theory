The question to carry

What's really going on here?

Internomics · Next: Module 2 — See the Payoffs

You don't need to memorize everything in this module. If you remember one question, remember this one. What's really going on here? Who are the players? What do they want? What can they do? What do they know? What do they expect? What happens next? And what happens if nothing gets resolved? Those questions are the beginning of strategic thinking. Before you can make a better move you have to see the game you're playing. In Module Two we take the next step. Once you've identified the game, what does everyone actually want? Because if you misunderstand the payoffs, even a brilliant strategy can aim at the wrong target. I'll see you there.
