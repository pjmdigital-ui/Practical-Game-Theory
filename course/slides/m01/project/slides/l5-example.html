Internomics · Module 1 — See the Game

A manager tells me, "My employees aren't cooperating." The easy conclusion is: they're selfish. But maybe the compensation system rewards employees for outperforming one another. Maybe nobody knows who owns the task. Maybe they believe management will take credit for their ideas, or speaking up has been punished in the past. The behavior may be coming from the structure. That doesn't mean the people have no responsibility. It means you shouldn't automatically diagnose a person problem when there may be a game problem. Here's another one. You and a coworker both want a project finished. You think they're procrastinating. They think you're going to take over if they start. Same goal, but your expectations about each other are creating the problem. That's not necessarily a character flaw. It may be a game, and once you see it, you can change it.
