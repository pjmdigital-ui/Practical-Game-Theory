Example · Person problem or game problem?

"My employees aren't cooperating."

The situation

A manager's team won't work together. The easy conclusion is that the employees are selfish.

What happened

Look at the structure. Maybe pay rewards outperforming each other. Maybe nobody owns the task. Maybe speaking up was punished before.

The lesson

The behavior may come from the game. People keep their responsibility. You just look somewhere new for the fix.

Internomics · Module 1 — See the Game

This is the difference between a person problem and a game problem. A manager tells me "My employees aren't cooperating." The conclusion comes fast. They're selfish. But look at the middle card. Perhaps the pay system rewards employees for outperforming one another. Put different people in the same structure and you may get the same result. That doesn't mean the people have no responsibility. It means the fix is in a different place. A pep talk about teamwork won't beat a pay system that punishes teamwork.
