Internomics · Module 1 — See the Game

Here's your assignment this week. Pick one meaningful interaction coming up. Before it happens, spend ten minutes on a Game Map instead of a script, and mark which answers are assumptions rather than facts. Then, during and after the conversation, write down any player who turned out to matter that you hadn't listed, any constraint or alternative that was different from what you assumed, one moment where the other person's response changed your next move, and whether the stated topic was the whole issue or there was a game behind it. Next week, bring three answers. What did you notice that you'd have missed if you'd only prepared what to say? Which assumption was most wrong, and what made the other person's behavior more reasonable from their side? And was the main obstacle a person problem or a game problem?
