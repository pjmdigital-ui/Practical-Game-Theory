Internomics · Module 1 — See the Game

Here's your assignment this week. Pick one meaningful interaction coming up. Before it happens spend ten minutes on a Game Map instead of a script. Then mark which answers are assumptions rather than facts. You'll probably find more than you expected. Afterward write down four things. Any player who mattered that you hadn't listed. Any constraint or alternative you got wrong. One moment where their response changed your next move. And whether there was a game behind the conversation. Next week bring your answers. Was the main obstacle a person problem or a game problem?
