Internomics · Module 1 — See the Game

Here's a question we overlook all the time. We think about what we can do. Game theory makes us look at what everyone can do. Suppose the employee at the counter genuinely wants to give you a refund, but company policy doesn't allow it. Suppose your boss wants to give you that raise, but the budget has already been approved. Suppose your business partner wants to accept your proposal, but their other partner has veto power. In each case, the person's preference is only part of the picture. Formal game theory keeps a player's available choices separate from their preferences over outcomes. In plain language: wanting something and being able to do something are not the same thing. Push on the want when the problem is the can, and you'll just get frustrated.
