Key terms

TermPlain-language meaning
GameThe outcome depends partly on several people's decisions. It can be cooperative or competitive or mixed.
PlayerAny decision-maker who can affect the outcome. That includes people off-stage.
Mixed interestsNeither fully aligned nor fully opposed
Available actionsWhat each player can actually do. It's distinct from what they want.
ConstraintAnything that limits choices. Stated and real constraints aren't always the same.
Outside optionWhat happens to each player if there's no agreement

Internomics · Module 1 — See the Game

Here are the key terms from this module. A game is a situation where the outcome depends at least partly on the decisions of more than one person. A player is any decision-maker who can affect the outcome. That includes people who aren't in the room. Mixed interests are neither fully aligned nor fully opposed. Available actions are what each player can actually do. A constraint is anything that limits a player's choices. An outside option is what happens to each player if there's no agreement.
