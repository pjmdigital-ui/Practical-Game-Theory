Internomics · Module 1 — See the Game

Here are the key terms from this module, and you'll find all of them in your workbook. A game: a situation where the outcome depends, at least partly, on the decisions of multiple people. It can be cooperative, competitive, or mixed. A player: any decision-maker who can affect the outcome, including people who aren't in the room. Mixed interests: neither fully aligned nor fully opposed. Available actions, or strategies: what each player can actually do, as distinct from what they want to do. A constraint: anything that limits a player's choices, like a policy, a budget, an approval requirement or a veto, and remember, a stated constraint and a real one aren't always the same. And an outside option, or alternative: what happens to each player if the interaction produces no agreement.
