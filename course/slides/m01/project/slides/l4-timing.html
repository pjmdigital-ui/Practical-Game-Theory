Internomics · Module 1 — See the Game

Here's something that becomes obvious once you start seeing interactions this way: when you act matters. Suppose I make you an offer and you have to respond immediately. That's one game. Suppose I make an offer, wait for your response, and then decide whether to improve it. That's another game. Suppose you get to see my offer before deciding on yours. Different game. Suppose neither of us knows what the other is going to do. Different game again. Game theorists separate simultaneous-move games from sequential games precisely because the timing and observability of actions change what's possible. So add this to your toolkit: who moves when? Sometimes the most important part of your strategy isn't what you do. It's when you do it.
