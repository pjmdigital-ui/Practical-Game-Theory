Ask: Who moves when? Sometimes the key part of your strategy isn't what you do. It's when you do it.

Internomics · Module 1 — See the Game

When you act matters. I make you an offer and you must respond on the spot. That's one game. I make an offer and wait and then decide whether to improve it. That's another. Same two people. Different games. Timing changes what's possible. So does what each person can observe. If someone pushes you for an answer right now, you can often say "Let me come back to you tomorrow." Here's the common mistake. We obsess over what to say and almost never think about when to say it. Next let's see how the game itself can change.
