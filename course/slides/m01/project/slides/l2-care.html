Internomics · Module 1 — See the Game

Real human beings have complicated motivations. People care about money. But they also care about fairness and relationships. They care about status and identity and reputation. If you assume everyone wants the same thing you'll misread almost every interaction. You might assume your boss only cares about the budget. But maybe they care about fairness across the team. Each of those is part of their payoff. Miss them and your perfect argument lands in the wrong place. So think of someone you're in a tough conversation with. Which of these cards matters most to them? Now let's look at how interests line up between people.
