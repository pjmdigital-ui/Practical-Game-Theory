Internomics · Module 1 — See the Game

Here's something else that's easy to miss. A game isn't always static. Your own actions change the situation. You ask someone for a favor and they say yes: now you've established something about your relationship. You break a promise: now they may behave differently next time. You reveal information: now they know something they didn't. You publicly commit to a position: now backing away gets more expensive. Every move can change what the other person knows, what they believe, what they expect, and which choices are still on the table. That's why game theory isn't about picking the best move from a fixed menu. It's about understanding decisions that depend on each other, over time.
