The game can change while you're playing it

They say yes to a favor

Now you've established something about the relationship.

You break a promise

Now they may behave differently next time.

You reveal a fact

Now they know something they didn't before.

You commit publicly

Now backing away may cost more.

Each move can change what they know and expect. Game theory is about interdependent decisions over time.

Internomics · Module 1 — See the Game

A game isn't always static. Your own actions can change the situation. You break a promise and they may behave differently next time. You reveal information and now they know something new. You publicly commit to a position and now backing away costs more. In each case the board itself shifted. Say you announce your position in a team meeting. After it changing course means walking it back in front of everyone. Here's the misunderstanding to watch for. We treat each conversation as a fresh start. It rarely is. So ask yourself what your last move taught them to expect. Now let's look under the surface of the conversation.
