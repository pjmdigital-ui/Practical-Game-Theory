Internomics · Module 2 — See the Payoffs

Here's the one shift I want you to walk away with. Before this module you might look at someone's behavior and think, "Why would they do that?" That question usually comes with a little judgment attached. It assumes the behavior doesn't make sense. Now I want you to ask a different question. What payoff would make that behavior make sense? That's a very different question. It sends you looking for the reason instead of the verdict. Let me be clear about what this shift is not. It doesn't mean you'll agree with them. It doesn't mean you'll excuse them. It doesn't even mean you'll always figure them out. It simply means you stop assuming that your preferences are the universal standard for rational behavior. You're trying to see the game from their side. And one more thing. This is understanding. It is not manipulation. The goal is never to find someone's hot button and push it. The goal is to understand the incentive structure before you choose your move. We'll come back to that boundary at the end of the module. Here's a question to hold onto. Who in your life have you labeled as difficult? Keep that person in mind. We'll use them later. Now let's look at what you'll be able to do by the end of this module.
