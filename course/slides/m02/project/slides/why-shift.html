Internomics · Module 2 — See the Payoffs

Here's the one shift I want you to walk away with. Before this module you might look at someone's behavior and think, "Why would they do that?" Now ask a different question. What payoff would make that behavior make sense? It sends you looking for the reason instead of the verdict. This doesn't mean you'll agree with them or excuse them. It means you stop assuming that your preferences are the universal standard for rational behavior. And this is understanding. It is not manipulation. Now let's look at what you'll be able to do.
