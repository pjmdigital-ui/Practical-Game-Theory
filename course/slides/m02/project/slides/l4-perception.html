Perception is part of the game

Same deal. Two different payoffs.

One person sees

"I'm getting $10,000."

The other sees

"I'm losing $5,000 compared with what I expected."

Decisions are based on perceived outcomes. Your perception of the game shapes how you play it. Theirs shapes how they play it.

Internomics · Module 2 — See the Payoffs

There's the objective situation. And there's how each player perceives it. Suppose two people are offered the exact same deal. One thinks "I'm getting ten thousand dollars." The other thinks "I'm losing five thousand dollars compared with what I expected." Same objective outcome. Different perceived payoff. The first says yes. The second might feel cheated and walk away. If you're making the offer, you might think it's generous. But they're using their reference point. So before you make an offer, ask what they're expecting.
