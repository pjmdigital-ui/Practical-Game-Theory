Internomics · Module 2 — See the Payoffs

Money can be a payoff. But so can time, security, status, freedom, convenience, fairness, reputation, control, relationships, avoiding embarrassment, reducing risk, keeping a promise, and protecting your identity. And sometimes what someone wants isn't even the outcome itself. It's what the outcome represents. A person may fight over five hundred dollars because of what conceding those five hundred dollars would mean to them. They may be protecting respect, or principle, or status, or the feeling that they weren't taken advantage of. If you only look at the dollar amount, you can completely misunderstand the game. Think of two business partners arguing about reinvesting profits. It looks like a fight about money. But if one is optimizing for long-term growth and the other for financial security, neither is irrational. They're optimizing for different things.
