Internomics · Module 2 — See the Payoffs

Money can be a payoff. Of course it can. But it's only one currency. Look at the cards on screen. Time is a payoff. So is freedom. So is plain convenience. Security is a payoff. Reducing risk is a payoff. Status and reputation are payoffs. So is avoiding embarrassment. Fairness matters to people. So does keeping a promise. Control over their own choices matters. Relationships matter. And so does protecting their identity. Here's the part people miss. Sometimes what someone wants isn't even the outcome itself. It's what the outcome represents. A person may fight over five hundred dollars because of what conceding those five hundred dollars would mean to them. They may be protecting respect. They may be protecting a principle. They may be protecting their status. Or they may be protecting the feeling that they weren't taken advantage of. If you only look at the dollar amount, you can completely misunderstand the game. You'll think the argument is about five hundred dollars. You'll offer them two hundred and fifty to split the difference. And you'll be confused when they get angrier. You were negotiating in the wrong currency. So here's a question for you. Think of the last argument you had about money. Was it really about the money? Let's look at an example where it wasn't.
