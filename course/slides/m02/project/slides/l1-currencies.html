Internomics · Module 2 — See the Payoffs

Money can be a payoff. But it's only one currency. Time and security are payoffs. So are status and fairness and control. Here's the part people miss. Sometimes what someone wants is what the outcome represents. A person may fight over five hundred dollars because of what conceding it would mean to them. They may be protecting respect or a principle. You'll offer them two hundred and fifty to split the difference. And you'll be confused when they get angrier.
