Internomics · Module 2 — See the Payoffs

Imagine two business partners arguing over how much money to reinvest in their company. Partner A wants to reinvest eighty percent. Partner B wants to distribute most of the profits. On the surface this looks like a fight about money. But look underneath. Partner A is motivated mainly by long-term growth. Partner B is motivated mainly by financial security. Neither of them is necessarily irrational. They're optimizing for different things. Now you can ask a better question. How do we grow without putting B's security at risk?
