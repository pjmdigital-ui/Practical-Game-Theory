The Ultimatum Game

Why would anyone turn down free money?

The setup

One person proposes how to split a sum of money. The other accepts or rejects. If they reject it both get nothing.

The simple prediction

Accept almost any positive amount. $1 out of $100 still beats $0.

What people do

They often reject offers they see as unfair. Even when that leaves them with nothing.

If people sacrifice money to punish unfairness, fairness is part of the game.
