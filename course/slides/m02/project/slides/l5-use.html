Internomics · Module 2 — See the Payoffs

This is where payoffs connect to communication. If I know what you value, I can understand what you're likely to respond to, what you're likely to resist, what you'll see as a threat, and what you'll see as an opportunity. Sometimes that understanding will help you persuade. Sometimes it will help you negotiate, or cooperate. Sometimes it will tell you that agreement isn't possible. And sometimes it will tell you that you're solving the wrong problem entirely. But notice what we're not saying. We're not saying, "Find their hot button and push it." That's tactics, and it fails the ethical standard we set in Module Zero. We're doing something more fundamental: understanding the incentive structure before choosing a move that respects the other person's autonomy and informed choice. That's why this is a mental model, not a technique.
