Internomics · Module 2 — See the Payoffs

This is where payoffs connect to communication. And it's where this gets really powerful. If I know what you value, I can understand a lot about how you'll react. I can see what you're likely to respond to. I can see what you're likely to resist. I can see what you'll perceive as a threat. And I can see what you'll see as an opportunity. So what does that give you? Look at the four cards. Sometimes that understanding will help you persuade. Sometimes it will help you negotiate. Sometimes it will help you cooperate. Sometimes it will tell you that agreement isn't possible. And sometimes it will tell you that you're solving the wrong problem entirely. That last one happens more than you'd think. But notice what we're not saying. We're not saying, "Find their hot button and push it." That's tactics. It treats the other person's values as a lever for you to pull. And it fails the ethical standard we set in Module Zero. We're doing something more fundamental. We're understanding the incentive structure before choosing our move. And the move we choose should respect the other person's autonomy and informed choice. That's why this is a mental model rather than a technique. A technique is something you do to someone. A mental model is a way of seeing the situation clearly. Here's the question to ask yourself. If the other person could see exactly what I'm doing with what I know, would they be okay with it?
