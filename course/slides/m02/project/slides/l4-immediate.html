Internomics · Module 2 — See the Payoffs

One of the easiest mistakes is looking only at what someone gains right now. Three pictures from the book. Someone refuses a small financial benefit because it would damage their reputation. Someone takes a short-term loss to preserve a long-term relationship. Someone agrees to an uncomfortable conversation because they believe it will improve the relationship later. In every case the immediate payoff isn't the whole payoff. If you only score the current moment, you'll misread all three. Whose choice have you been scoring too narrowly?
