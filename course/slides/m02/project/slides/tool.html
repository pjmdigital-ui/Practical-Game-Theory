Internomics · Module 2 — See the Payoffs

Your tool for this module is the Payoff Map. It sits on top of the Game Map from Module One. List the players and work the prompts. What do I want? What do they want? Where do we align and conflict? What are we each trying to avoid? What might I be missing? Then circle the structure. Opposed, aligned or mixed? Answer the key question. Am I assuming their payoff is the same as mine? Finally write a next move that respects their autonomy. The common mistake is rushing their column. Slow down there. Let's practice.
