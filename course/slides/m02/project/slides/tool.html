Internomics · Module 2 — See the Payoffs

Your tool for this module is the Payoff Map, and it sits right on top of the Game Map you built in Module One. Start with the interaction and the players. Then: what do I want, listing more than one currency? What do they want? Not what they should want, not what I'd want in their shoes, not only what they said. Where do our interests align? Where do they conflict? What are we each trying to avoid, me and them? What might I be missing: status, identity, fairness, autonomy, relationships? Then circle the structure: mostly opposed, mostly aligned, or mixed. Answer the most important question, am I assuming their payoff is the same as mine? And finally, write your next move, one that respects their autonomy and informed choice.
