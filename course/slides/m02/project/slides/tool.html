Internomics · Module 2 — See the Payoffs

Your tool for this module is the Payoff Map. It's in your workbook. It sits right on top of the Game Map you built in Module One. Use it before any important interaction. Start by writing down the interaction and the players. You can pull the players straight from your Game Map. Then work through the prompts. One. What do I want? List more than one currency. Two. What do they want? Not what they should want. Not what I'd want in their shoes. And not only what they said. Three. Where do our interests align? What can we both gain? Four. Where do they conflict? What does one of us gain at the other's expense? Five. What are we each trying to avoid? Write down their losses and risks. Then write down yours. Six. What might I be missing? Could status matter here? Could identity or fairness or autonomy or a relationship? Seven. Circle the structure. Is it mostly opposed? Mostly aligned? Or mixed? Then answer the most important question on the page. Am I assuming their payoff is the same as mine? Be honest with that one. And finally write down your next move. Make it a move that respects their autonomy and informed choice. Here's the common mistake. People fill in their own column carefully and rush the other person's column. Slow down on theirs. That's where the insight is. Let's practice it on a real scenario.
