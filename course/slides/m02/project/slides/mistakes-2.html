Internomics · Module 2 — See the Payoffs

Here are three more mistakes. The first is treating money as the only currency. Status and fairness can matter as much. The second is turning hidden payoffs into conspiracy theories. Most people aren't hiding anything. They just care about more than what's on the table. The third is taking stated preferences as the whole story. Watch choices as well as words. But don't assume people are lying. In each mistake we score people by a payoff we picked for them. The fix is to ask what actually matters to them. Now your assignment.
