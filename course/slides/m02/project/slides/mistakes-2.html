Internomics · Module 2 — See the Payoffs

Here are three more mistakes worth naming. The first is treating money as the only currency. It's easy to do because money is visible and countable. Status isn't. Fairness isn't. But status and fairness can matter as much as money. Sometimes more. So can autonomy. So can identity. So can relationships. Think back to the five hundred dollar fight. It was never about the five hundred dollars. The second mistake is turning hidden payoffs into conspiracy theories. Once you learn that people have hidden payoffs, it's tempting to see secret motives everywhere. Don't. That's not the lesson. The lesson is only that the visible payoff may not be the complete payoff. Most people aren't hiding anything. They just care about more than what's on the table. The third mistake is taking stated preferences as the whole story. Remember the person who said price was their only concern. Their choice told you more than their words did. So watch choices as well as words. But don't jump to the conclusion that people are lying. Usually they're discovering what they want at the same moment you are. Now notice what all three mistakes have in common. In each one we score other people by a payoff we picked for them. We picked money. Or we picked a sinister motive. Or we picked whatever they said first. The fix is the same every time. Go back and ask what actually appears to matter to them. Now here's your assignment for the week.
