Internomics · Module 2 — See the Payoffs

Daniel Kahneman and Amos Tversky developed what's called prospect theory. It showed that choices often depend on how gains and losses are framed relative to a reference point. A reference point is the baseline we judge from. If I expected a big bonus, a smaller one can feel like a loss. And losses can carry more psychological weight than equivalent gains in many contexts.
