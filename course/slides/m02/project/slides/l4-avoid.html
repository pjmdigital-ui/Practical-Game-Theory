Internomics · Module 2 — See the Payoffs

Here's another question that reveals hidden payoffs. What are they trying to avoid? People aren't motivated only by what they can gain. They can be just as strongly motivated by what they could lose. Look at the list on screen. They may want to avoid embarrassment. They may want to avoid uncertainty. They may want to avoid conflict. They may be afraid of losing status. They may want to avoid financial risk. They may be terrified of looking incompetent. Or they may just hate being controlled. Here's why this matters so much. Two people can value exactly the same positive outcome. And they can still give very different weight to the risks of getting there. You pitch an idea. One person hears it and says, "Let's try it." Another person hears the same idea and says, "What if it fails?" Same potential upside. Different perceived downside. Different behavior. And if you only think about the upside, you'll keep selling the second person on how great it could be. They already know. That's not what's stopping them. What's stopping them is the downside. So you'd be far better off talking about what happens if it fails. The common mistake is mapping only what someone wants to gain. If you do that, you miss half of what's driving them. So here's your question. Think of someone who's been resisting you. What might they be trying to avoid? Next let's look at why losses can carry extra weight.
