Internomics · Module 2 — See the Payoffs

Here's another question that reveals hidden payoffs. What are they trying to avoid? People can be just as strongly motivated by what they could lose. They may want to avoid embarrassment or uncertainty or conflict. They may be terrified of looking incompetent. You pitch an idea. One person says "Let's try it." Another says "What if it fails?" Same upside. Different perceived downside. If you keep selling the second person on the upside, you miss what's stopping them. Talk about what happens if it fails.
