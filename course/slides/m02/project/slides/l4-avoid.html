Internomics · Module 2 — See the Payoffs

Here's another question that reveals hidden payoffs: what are they trying to avoid? People aren't motivated only by what they can gain. They can be just as strongly motivated by what they could lose. They may want to avoid embarrassment, uncertainty, conflict, losing status, financial risk, looking incompetent, or being controlled. This matters because two people can value exactly the same positive outcome and still give very different weight to the risks of getting there. One person hears the idea and says, "Let's try it." Another says, "What if it fails?" Same potential upside. Different perceived downside. Different behavior. If you only map what someone wants to gain, you'll miss half of what's driving them.
