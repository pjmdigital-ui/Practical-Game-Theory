Internomics · Module 2 — See the Payoffs

Now, I don't want you turning this into a "there's always a win-win" philosophy. Sometimes there isn't. Two people can genuinely want opposite things. A company may want to pay less while an employee wants to earn more. A buyer wants the lowest price, a seller the highest. Two competitors may both want the same customer. Those are real conflicts. The point of understanding payoffs isn't to pretend they don't exist. It's to identify them accurately. Because if you misdiagnose a conflict as cooperation, you'll make bad decisions. And if you misdiagnose an opportunity for cooperation as pure conflict, you'll leave value on the table. Both mistakes are expensive.
