Internomics · Module 2 — See the Payoffs

The payoff structure isn't necessarily fixed. Imagine two employees competing for a promotion. The company rewards individual performance. So of course they're guarded with each other. Now management changes the compensation system. Both employees receive a bonus if the entire team hits its target. The people didn't change. But the game changed. Now helping each other pays. That means you have more than one lever. Changing the person is hard. You can also look at what the environment rewards. What behavior are the rules quietly rewarding in your home?
