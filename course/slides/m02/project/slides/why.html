Internomics · Module 2 — See the Payoffs

Identifying the players isn't enough. You also have to understand what each player is trying to accomplish. And here's a mistake we make all the time: we assume other people want what we want. Then, when they don't behave the way we expect, we conclude they're irrational, difficult, selfish, stubborn, or just wrong. Sometimes they are. But often they're simply responding to a different set of incentives. They're playing the same game, but they're playing for a different payoff. If you only look at the dollar amount, or at what you would want in their shoes, you can completely misunderstand the game. This module is about seeing it from their side.
