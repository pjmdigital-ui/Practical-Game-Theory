Internomics · Module 2 — See the Payoffs

Here's why this module matters. In Module One you learned to identify the players. But that isn't enough. You also have to understand what each player is trying to accomplish. And here's a mistake we all make. We assume other people want what we want. Then they don't behave the way we expect. So we reach for a verdict. They're irrational. They're difficult. They're selfish. They're stubborn. Or they're just wrong. Sometimes they are. I'm not going to pretend otherwise. But often something else is going on. They're simply responding to a different set of incentives. They're playing the same game as you. They're just playing for a different payoff. Think about the last time someone frustrated you at work or at home. Did you stop to ask what they were trying to get out of it? Or did you jump straight to the verdict? Most of us jump. I've done it more times than I can count. A payoff is simply what makes an outcome better or worse for a player. Money is one payoff. So are time and security. So are status and fairness and identity. If you only look at the dollar amount, you can completely misunderstand the game. So this module builds one mental shift. Let me show you what it is.
