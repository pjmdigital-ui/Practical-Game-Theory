Internomics · Module 2 — See the Payoffs

Here's why this module matters. In Module One you learned to identify the players. You also have to understand what each player is trying to accomplish. And here's a mistake we all make. We assume other people want what we want. So we reach for a verdict. They're irrational. Sometimes they are. But often they're simply responding to a different set of incentives. Think about the last time someone frustrated you. Did you ask what they were trying to get out of it? So this module builds one mental shift.
