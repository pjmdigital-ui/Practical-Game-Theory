Internomics · Module 2 — See the Payoffs

Let's practice. Here's the scenario. Dana runs a small marketing agency. Her best designer is Marcus. He's been there four years. Dana offers him a promotion to creative director with a fifteen percent raise. She expects him to be thrilled. Instead he hesitates. He asks for a week to think about it. In a hallway conversation he mentions that the new role "means a lot more meetings." He says he'd "stop doing the actual design work." A few days later Dana learns something from a colleague. Marcus was hurt that the promotion was announced to the leadership team before it was offered to him. Dana is frustrated. She says, "I'm offering him more money and a better title. Why is he being so difficult?" Notice that question. You've heard it before in this module. She's now considering adding another five percent to close the deal. And there's one more detail.
