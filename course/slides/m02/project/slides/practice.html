Practice · Exercise A

The promotion Marcus didn't jump at

Dana offers her best designer Marcus creative director and a 15% raise. He hesitates. The role means more meetings and less design work. He was hurt that leadership heard before he did. He says he "doesn't care about titles." Last year he turned down a higher-paying offer. Dana is thinking of adding 5%.

Pause the video and answer:

  1. What payoff is Dana assuming? What suggests his is different?
  2. Name three currencies in play. Include one he's trying to avoid.
  3. Is the structure opposed, aligned or mixed? Where exactly?
  4. What does the turned-down offer reveal next to what he's said?
  5. Why might another 5% fail? What's a better next move?
