Internomics · Module 2 — See the Payoffs

Let's practice. Dana runs a small marketing agency. Her best designer, Marcus, has been there four years. She offers him a promotion to creative director with a fifteen percent raise, and she expects him to be thrilled. Instead he hesitates and asks for a week to think. In a hallway conversation he mentions the new role "means a lot more meetings" and he'd "stop doing the actual design work." A few days later Dana learns he was hurt that the promotion was announced to the leadership team before it was offered to him. Dana is frustrated: "I'm offering him more money and a better title. Why is he being so difficult?" She's considering adding another five percent. And Marcus has said several times he doesn't care about titles, but last year he turned down a higher-paying offer from a larger firm.
