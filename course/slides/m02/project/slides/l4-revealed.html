Here's a subtler lesson. People can tell you what they want, but their behavior gives you additional information. Someone says, "Price is my only concern." Then they reject a cheaper option because it takes longer to deliver. Now you've learned something. Perhaps price wasn't actually their only concern. This doesn't mean they were lying to you. People often don't fully understand their own preferences until they face an actual choice. But strategically, behavior tells us what matters. That's why game theory often looks at observed choices rather than simply accepting statements at face value. So listen to the words, and watch the choices.
