Internomics · Module 2 — See the Payoffs

Behavior tells you more than words. Someone tells you, "Price is my only concern." So you bring them the cheapest option. Then they reject it because it takes longer to deliver. Speed mattered too. A stated preference is what someone says they want. A revealed preference is what their choices show they value. This doesn't mean people are lying to you. People often don't fully understand their own preferences until they face an actual choice. So listen to the words and watch the choices. When they don't match, get curious.
