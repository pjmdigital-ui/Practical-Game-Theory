Internomics · Module 2 — See the Payoffs

Let's go back to the raise from Module Zero. You walk into your boss's office and ask for more money. What does your boss want? Most of us answer immediately: to save money. Maybe. But perhaps your boss also wants to retain good employees, stay within budget, avoid setting an expensive precedent, keep the rest of the team happy, demonstrate fairness, hit this year's profit target, and avoid explaining the raise to their own superior. And here's the one we almost never consider: your boss may actually want to give you the raise, but doesn't believe they can afford to. You came in thinking, "I want more money, and my boss doesn't want to give it to me." The actual game might be, "We both want me to stay, but we have different constraints and preferences about how compensation is structured." That's a completely different problem, and it calls for a completely different way of thinking.
