This is where empathy and game theory meet. People often think empathy means imagining how I would feel if I were them. Strategic thinking asks for something slightly different: try to understand what the situation looks like from their position. Those aren't the same thing. If you put yourself in their shoes but keep your own preferences, your interpretation might be completely wrong. So instead, reconstruct their game. What do they value? What are they afraid of losing? What alternatives do they have? What constraints are they facing? What outcome would make them feel they won, and what would make them feel they lost? You aren't necessarily agreeing with them. You're trying to understand the incentive structure they're operating within.
