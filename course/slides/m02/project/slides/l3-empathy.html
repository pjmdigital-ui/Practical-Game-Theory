Where empathy and game theory meet

From

"How would I feel if I were them?"

To

"What does this look like from their position?"

If you were them but kept your preferences, your reading could be completely wrong.

Internomics · Module 2 — See the Payoffs

This is where empathy and game theory meet. People often think empathy means imagining how I would feel if I were them. Strategic thinking asks you to understand what the situation looks like from their position. On the left you bring your own preferences and fears with you. On the right you ask what they value. Think back to the person who wouldn't apologize. You might apologize in a heartbeat. But for them an apology may cost dignity in a way it wouldn't cost you.
