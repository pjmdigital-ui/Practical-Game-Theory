Internomics · Module 2 — See the Payoffs

Here's your assignment for this week. Catch yourself in one "Why would they do that?" moment. Before you respond or judge, sketch a quick Payoff Map. Then test it with one honest open question. "What would a good outcome look like for you here?" Record the behavior and your first interpretation and what you learned. Next week bring three answers. Where did you assume their payoff was the same as yours? Which payoff did you miss? Did understanding their payoffs change your next move? Or did it confirm that your interests really were opposed? Either answer is a good one.
