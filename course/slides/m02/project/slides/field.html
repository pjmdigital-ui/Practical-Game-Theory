Internomics · Module 2 — See the Payoffs

Here's your assignment for this week. Catch yourself in one "Why would they do that?" moment. It can be at work or at home. You'll know it when it happens. You'll feel that little flash of judgment. Before you respond or judge, sketch a quick Payoff Map. It can be a few lines on your phone. Then test it if it's appropriate. Ask one honest open question. Something like, "What would a good outcome look like for you here?" Or, "What are you most worried about?" Then record four things. The behavior. Your first interpretation. The payoffs you guessed on both sides, what they're trying to gain and what they're trying to avoid. And what you learned. Next week bring three answers with you. First, where did your first interpretation assume their payoff was the same as yours? Second, which payoff did you miss? Name the currency. Third, did understanding their payoffs change your next move? Or did it confirm that your interests really were opposed? Either answer is a good one.
