The question to carry

What payoff would make their behavior make sense?

Internomics · Next: Module 3 — Think in Responses

So here's the question to carry this week. When you run into resistance or disagreement or behavior you didn't expect, pause before judging. Then ask what payoff would make their behavior make sense. Go one step further. What are they trying to gain? What are they trying to avoid? Where are our interests aligned? Where are they genuinely opposed? What am I assuming they value that they may not? Once you understand the payoffs, you can begin to understand the choices. And once you understand the choices, you can begin to anticipate the response. That's Module Three. Think in Responses. Because in a game your move is only half the move. I'll see you there.
