Internomics · Module 2 — See the Payoffs

Here are the key terms from this module, and you'll find the full list in your workbook. A payoff: what makes an outcome better or worse for a particular player. A payoff function: the full set of things a person values and how they weigh them. Mixed interests: shared goals on some dimensions, conflict on others, alongside the opposed and aligned structures we covered in lesson two. A hidden payoff: a value like status or dignity that drives a decision without being the visible stake. The Ultimatum Game: one person proposes a split, the other accepts or rejects, and rejection leaves both with nothing. A reference point: the baseline, often an expectation, against which an outcome feels like a gain or a loss. And stated versus revealed preference: what someone says they want versus what their choices show they value.
