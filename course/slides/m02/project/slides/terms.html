Internomics · Module 2 — See the Payoffs

Here are the key terms from this module. You'll find the full list in your workbook. A payoff is what makes an outcome better or worse for a particular player. A payoff function is the full set of things a person values and how they weigh them. Mixed interests means shared goals on some dimensions and conflict on others. That sits alongside the opposed and aligned structures from lesson two. A hidden payoff is a value like status or dignity. It drives a decision without being the visible stake. The Ultimatum Game is the experiment where one person proposes a split. The other accepts or rejects. Rejection leaves both with nothing. A reference point is the baseline we judge an outcome against. It's often an expectation. It's what makes an outcome feel like a gain or a loss. And stated versus revealed preference is the gap between what someone says they want and what their choices show they value. Now let's look at the research behind two of these ideas.
