Mixed-motive games hold room for conflict and cooperation at the same time.

Internomics · Module 2 — See the Payoffs

Much of human life happens in mixed interests. We both want the company to grow. But we disagree about what to do with the profits. We both want the deal completed. But we disagree about price. There's a shared goal on top and a real disagreement underneath. So the strategic thinker isn't looking for a winner. They're looking for the structure of the interests. In a disagreement the shared goal goes quiet. But usually the other person isn't against you entirely. They're against one part of your plan. Now let's see what this opens up.
