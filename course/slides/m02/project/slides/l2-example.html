Internomics · Module 2 — See the Payoffs

Here's how this looks in practice. You want to buy a house for five hundred thousand dollars. The seller wants five hundred and fifty. There's an obvious conflict: you want a lower price, they want a higher one. But there's also a powerful area of alignment: you both want the transaction to happen. So this isn't a purely competitive game. You have conflicting interests over price and aligned interests over completing the sale. Now take a business deal. You disagree over price, but you discover the seller cares enormously about getting paid quickly, while you care more about paying over time. Restructure the deal, and the seller gets faster payment while you get better terms. The total value can go up even though you still disagree about the final allocation.
