Internomics · Module 2 — See the Payoffs

Here are the mistakes I see most often with this model. The first is the one the whole chapter is built around: assuming they want what you want. Before you interpret anyone's behavior, ask whether you're assuming their payoff is the same as yours. Second, calling someone irrational too fast. Usually that just means they aren't maximizing what you assumed they cared about, and money isn't the only currency. Third, getting the structure wrong in either direction: treating every interaction as zero-sum, or insisting there's always a win-win. Most interactions are mixed, so identify conflict accurately rather than inventing it or wishing it away. And fourth, confusing understanding with excusing, or with exploiting. Seeing why a behavior makes sense doesn't make it good. And knowing what someone values is not a hot button for you to push.
