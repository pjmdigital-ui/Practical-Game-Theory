Internomics · Module 2 — See the Payoffs

Here's how perception plays out at work. A manager gives two employees additional responsibility. Employee A thinks, "They trust me." Employee B thinks, "They're dumping more work on me." Same action. Different interpretation. Different perceived payoff. And so, a different likely response: one leans in, the other may push back or quietly resent it. If you're the manager and you only look at what you did, you'll be confused by B's reaction. That's why strategic thinking requires you to distinguish what happened from what each person thinks happened. We'll go much deeper on that when we get to information, beliefs, and framing later in the course.
