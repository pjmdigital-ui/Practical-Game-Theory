Example · More responsibility

One action, two payoffs

The situation

A manager gives two employees additional responsibility.

What happened

Employee A thinks "They trust me." Employee B thinks "They're dumping more work on me."

The lesson

Same action. Different perceived payoff. Different likely response.

Separate what happened from what each person thinks happened.

Internomics · Module 2 — See the Payoffs

Here's how perception plays out at work. A manager gives two employees additional responsibility. Employee A thinks "They trust me." Employee B thinks "They're dumping more work on me." Same action. Different perceived payoff. Employee A probably leans in. Employee B may push back or quietly resent it. As the manager you didn't give them the same thing. You gave them the same action. They each received a different payoff. So separate what happened from what each person thinks happened. We assume our intent travels with our action. It often doesn't. Here's the big idea.
