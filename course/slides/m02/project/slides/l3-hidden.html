The hidden payoff

The visible payoff may not be the complete payoff.

They refuse a reasonable offer

Accepting would make them look weak in front of colleagues. The money isn't the whole payoff. Status is in it too.

They refuse to apologize

They think apologizing means admitting they were entirely at fault. The payoff is relationship + dignity + self-image.

This isn't "everyone has a secret motive." It's a fuller picture.

Internomics · Module 2 — See the Payoffs

Sometimes the payoff you can see isn't the payoff that drives the decision. Imagine someone refuses a perfectly reasonable offer. But suppose accepting would make them look weak in front of their colleagues. Now there's also status. The refusal makes sense. Or imagine someone refuses to apologize. They believe apologizing means admitting they were entirely at fault. Their payoff is relationship plus dignity plus self-image. The lesson is not that everyone has a secret hidden motive. It's that the visible payoff may not be the complete payoff. That's where empathy comes in.
