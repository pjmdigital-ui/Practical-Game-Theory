The hidden payoff

The visible payoff may not be the complete payoff.

Someone who won't apologize may care about the relationship, and also about dignity and self-image. That's not a secret motive. It's a fuller picture.

Internomics · Module 2 — See the Payoffs

Sometimes the payoff you can see isn't the payoff that actually drives the decision. Someone refuses a perfectly reasonable offer, and you think, "They're being irrational." But suppose accepting would make them look weak in front of their colleagues. Now the money isn't the whole payoff. There's also status. Or someone refuses to apologize, and you think, "They don't care about the relationship." Maybe they do. But they believe apologizing means admitting they were entirely at fault. Their payoff isn't relationship versus no relationship. It's relationship plus dignity plus self-image. Now, the lesson is not "everyone has a secret hidden motive." That would be an unhealthy way to use this model. The lesson is simply that the visible payoff may not be the complete payoff.
