Internomics · Module 2 — See the Payoffs

Instead of borrowing their shoes with your own preferences, reconstruct their game. Here are six questions. What do they value? What are they afraid of losing? What alternatives do they have? What constraints are they facing? What outcome would make them feel they won? And what would make them feel they lost? Say a colleague keeps blocking a change you've proposed. Maybe they're afraid of losing a process they built. Suddenly the blocking makes sense. You aren't agreeing with them. You're understanding their incentives. Which of these six do you usually skip?
