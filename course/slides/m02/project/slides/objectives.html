Internomics · Module 2 — See the Payoffs

Here's what you'll be able to do by the end of this module. First, you'll define a payoff in plain language. You'll also name the currencies besides money that drive real decisions. Second, you'll tell whether interests are opposed or aligned or mixed. You'll find exactly where a disagreement conflicts and where it lines up. Third, you'll separate what people say they want from what their choices reveal. Fourth, you'll separate what happened from what each person thinks happened. Those two are not the same thing. Fifth, you'll ask what someone is trying to avoid. Not just what they're trying to gain. And sixth, you'll complete a Payoff Map. You'll use it to choose a move that respects the other person's autonomy and dignity. That last part matters to me. Everything here is about understanding people better. It is never about working them. Let's start with the most basic question. What is a payoff?
