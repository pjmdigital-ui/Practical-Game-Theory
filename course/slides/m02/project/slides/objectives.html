Internomics · Module 2 — See the Payoffs

Here's what you'll be able to do by the end of this module. You'll define a payoff and name the currencies besides money. You'll tell whether interests are opposed or aligned or mixed. You'll separate what people say from what their choices reveal. You'll separate what happened from what each person thinks happened. You'll ask what someone is trying to avoid. And you'll complete a Payoff Map that respects their autonomy and dignity. So what is a payoff?
