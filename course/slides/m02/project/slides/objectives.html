Internomics · Module 2 — See the Payoffs

Here's what you'll be able to do by the end of this module. You'll be able to define a payoff in plain language and name the currencies besides money that drive real decisions. You'll be able to tell whether interests are opposed, aligned, or mixed, and find exactly where a disagreement conflicts and where it lines up. You'll separate what people say they want from what their choices reveal, and what happened from what each person thinks happened. You'll ask what someone is trying to avoid, not just what they're trying to gain. And you'll complete a Payoff Map and use it to choose a move that respects the other person's autonomy and dignity.
