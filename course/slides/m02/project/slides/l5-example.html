Example · The curfew

"Be home by midnight." "That's ridiculous."

The situation

A parent sets a curfew. The teenager pushes back hard.

What happened

The parent may be optimizing for safety. The teen may be optimizing for independence.

The lesson

Framed as parent versus teen it's a standoff. Seen as safety versus independence both positions make sense.

Before "How do I convince them?" ask "What payoff are they protecting?"

Internomics · Module 2 — See the Payoffs

Here's an example almost every family has lived. A parent says "You need to be home by midnight." The teenager says "That's ridiculous." That's a standoff. So ask the payoff question. What is the parent optimizing for? Maybe safety. What is the teenager optimizing for? Maybe independence. Now you can see why both positions exist. They're each protecting something real. That opens up a different conversation. Not "midnight or one o'clock." Instead it's how do we protect safety and still give you more independence. Next time someone disagrees, ask what payoff they're protecting. Here's the big idea.
