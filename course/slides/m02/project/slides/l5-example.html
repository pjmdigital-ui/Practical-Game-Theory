Internomics · Module 2 — See the Payoffs

Here's an example almost every family has lived. A parent says, "You need to be home by midnight." The teenager says, "That's ridiculous." What is the parent optimizing for? Maybe safety. What is the teenager optimizing for? Maybe independence. If you frame the disagreement as parent versus teenager, it's a standoff, and you miss the underlying structure. If you recognize the competing payoffs, you can begin to see why both positions exist. So the next time someone disagrees with you, don't immediately ask, "How do I convince them?" First ask, "What payoff are they protecting?" Once you see it, the disagreement may turn out to be smaller than it looked. Or it may not. Either way, you've learned something important.
