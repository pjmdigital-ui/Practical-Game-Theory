Internomics · Module 2 — See the Payoffs

Here's a practical exercise you can use today. Take a difficult interaction you've had recently. Maybe it's the person you thought of at the start of this module. Now catch the question you've been asking. It's probably something like, "Why are they being so difficult?" Set that question down. It doesn't lead anywhere useful. Ask this instead. What outcome would make this person feel that they got what they wanted? Then ask the flip side. What outcome would make them feel that they lost? Be specific. Picture the conversation ending. What would they need to walk away with? What would make them walk away feeling beaten? Now look at the bottom of the screen. Here's what you might discover. Maybe they don't actually need the thing you thought they needed. Maybe they care about something you never even considered. Maybe the conflict isn't over the stated issue at all. You've been arguing about the schedule. They've been worried about respect. Or maybe you discover that your interests really are fundamentally opposed. I want to be clear that this last one counts as a win too. It's useful. Because now you know what kind of game you're in. You can stop hoping for a breakthrough that isn't coming. And you can plan for the game you're actually playing. So try it this week. Pick one person. Answer both questions in writing. Next let's look at how all this changes the way you communicate.
