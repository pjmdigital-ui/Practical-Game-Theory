Internomics · Module 2 — See the Payoffs

Here's a practical exercise you can use today. Take a difficult interaction you've had recently. Catch the question you've been asking. It's probably "Why are they being so difficult?" Ask this instead. What outcome would make this person feel that they got what they wanted? Then ask what would make them feel that they lost. Maybe they care about something you never considered. You've been arguing about the schedule. They've been worried about respect. Or maybe your interests really are fundamentally opposed. That counts as a win too. Next let's look at communication.
