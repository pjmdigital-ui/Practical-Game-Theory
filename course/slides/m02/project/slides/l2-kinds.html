Three structures of interest

Opposed

What benefits me hurts you. Zero-sum games like chess are the strong form. They're rarer than we imagine.

Aligned

We both benefit from the same outcome. The problem is often coordination. Not conflict.

Mixed

We both want it to succeed. We disagree about how. This is where most of life happens.

Replace "How do I beat them?" with "Where are our interests aligned? Where are they opposed?"

Interests have different structures. First, opposed interests. What benefits me hurts you. The strong form is a zero-sum game like chess. But pure zero-sum situations are far less common in everyday relationships than we imagine. Second, aligned interests. Picture two employees trying to get a difficult project over the line. Their main problem may be coordination. Third, mixed interests. We both want the thing to succeed. We just disagree about how. So stop asking how you beat them. Start asking where your interests are aligned and where they're opposed. More on mixed interests.
