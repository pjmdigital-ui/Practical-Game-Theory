Internomics · Module 2 — See the Payoffs

Now you can add a second layer to the Game Map from Module One. When you're heading into an important interaction, ask six questions. What do I want? What outcomes matter to me, and list more than one currency. What do they want? What outcomes appear to matter to them? Where do our interests align: what can we both gain? Where do they conflict: what does one of us gain at the other's expense? What are we each trying to avoid? And what might I be missing? Could status, identity, fairness, autonomy, or relationships matter here? Then the most important question on the whole map: am I assuming their payoff is the same as mine? If the answer is yes, go back and look again.
