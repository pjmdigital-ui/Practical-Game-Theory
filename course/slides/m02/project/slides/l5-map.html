Internomics · Module 2 — See the Payoffs

Now you can add a second layer to the Game Map from Module One. The Payoff Map tells you what they're playing for. Before an important interaction, ask six questions. What do I want? What do they want? Where do our interests align? Where do they conflict? What are we each trying to avoid? What might I be missing? Then ask the most important question. Am I assuming their payoff is the same as mine? If the answer is yes, look again. The map isn't a script. It's a way of seeing. So what does it give you?
