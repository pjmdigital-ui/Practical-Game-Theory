Internomics · Module 2

See the Payoffs

What payoff would make their behavior make sense?

Paul Mascetta

Welcome to Module Two. This one is called See the Payoffs. Last module you learned to see the game before you choose your move. You learned to ask who the players are and what's at stake. This module adds the next layer. Once you know who's playing, you need to know what each of them is actually playing for. That's what a payoff is. And there's one question that runs through everything we'll do together this week. Here it is: what payoff would make their behavior make sense? Keep it in your head as we go. By the end of this module I think it will change how you read the people around you.
