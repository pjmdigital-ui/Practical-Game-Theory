Paul Mascetta

Welcome to Module Two: See the Payoffs. Last module, you learned to see the game before choosing your move: who the players are, what's at stake, how it's structured. This module adds the next layer. Once you know who's playing, you have to understand what each of them is actually playing for. And the question that runs through everything we'll do together this week is this one: what payoff would make their behavior make sense?
