Whenever you're dealing with another person, ask this. What are they actually trying to get out of this? It's not asking what you think they should want. It's not asking what you would want if you were them. And it's not even necessarily asking what they told you. Instead ask what outcome appears to matter to them. Each wrong version puts you at the center. The right version starts from theirs. When you last misread someone, which of those three mistakes did you make?
