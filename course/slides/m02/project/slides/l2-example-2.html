Example · A business deal

Grow the pie, then divide it

The situation

You and a seller disagree over price.

What happened

The seller cares most about getting paid quickly. You care more about paying over time.

The lesson

Restructure the deal. The seller gets faster payment. You get better terms.

Total value can rise even while you still disagree about the split.

Internomics · Module 2 — See the Payoffs

Imagine you're negotiating a business deal. You disagree over price. But as you talk, you discover something. The seller cares enormously about getting paid quickly. You care more about paying over time. Those preferences don't collide. They fit together. You may be able to restructure the deal. The seller gets faster payment. You get better terms. The total value goes up. You still disagree about the final allocation. But now you're dividing something bigger. And you found it by learning what the seller actually valued. That leads to the big idea.
