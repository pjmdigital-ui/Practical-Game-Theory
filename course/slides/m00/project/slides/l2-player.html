Internomics · Module 0 — Orientation

Throughout this course I'll use the word "player." I know it can sound cold. A player is simply a decision-maker within the interaction. Your spouse is a player. So is your boss. And so are you. Real people care about money. They also care about fairness and status and loyalty. That's why we need to learn what matters to each person. So here's the warning I want you to hear clearly. If you leave this course treating people as incentives and leverage points, you've learned the wrong lesson. The person across from you has fears. They have dignity. People are always more than players. Next let's talk about how to hold all of these ideas.
