Internomics · Module 0 — Orientation

Let me start with a confession. In 2011 I started a publishing company focused on influence and persuasion. Since then I've published close to a hundred trainings. For most of that time I focused on strategies and tactics. But there was something underneath them that I had never really named. I wasn't only thinking about what to say. I was trying to understand the situation surrounding what was being said. That's the part most training skips. Think of the communication advice you've picked up. How much of it was about reading the situation before the words? Let's look at what sits underneath them.
