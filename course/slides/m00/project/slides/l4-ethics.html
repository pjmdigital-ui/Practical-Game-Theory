Internomics · Module 0 — Orientation

Here's the standard. Every strategy you build in this course has to pass four tests. Truth: no deception, no creating false impressions. Autonomy: the other person still makes their own choice. Dignity: people are never just pieces on a board. And informed choice: they decide with the information they need. Game theory itself doesn't supply these values. We bring them, because strategic intelligence without humanity becomes manipulation, and manipulation is not what we're building here. The goal isn't to control people. It's to understand interactions well enough to make wiser choices inside them.
