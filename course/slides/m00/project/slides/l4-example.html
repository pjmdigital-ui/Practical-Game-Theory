Internomics · Module 0 — Orientation

Here's a simple example. A colleague keeps resisting your project. Your model: it's about status. So you act: you offer them public credit. You observe: they're still resistant. Instead of deciding they're irrational, you update. Maybe the real cost to them isn't status at all. Maybe it's workload, or risk to their own goals. So you adapt: you stop offering credit and start asking about capacity. The surprise didn't mean you failed. It improved your model of the game.
