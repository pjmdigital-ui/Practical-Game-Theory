Example · The loop in action

The resistant colleague

The situation

A colleague keeps resisting your project. You decide it must be about status.

What happened

You offer them public credit for the project. They're still resistant.

The lesson

Don't label them irrational. The surprise means your model of the game was incomplete.

Internomics · Module 0 — Orientation

Picture a colleague who keeps resisting a project you're leading. So you build a model. You decide their resistance is about status. Maybe they feel sidelined. So you act on it. You offer them public credit. And then you observe. They're still resistant. Now this is the moment that matters. Most of us decide they're just difficult and write them off. The other option is to treat the surprise as information. Your model predicted that credit would solve it. It didn't. The problem wasn't your wording. It was the read underneath. Here's the misunderstanding to watch for. If the model is wrong, a stronger version of the same move just fails harder. Let's walk through how the loop plays out.
