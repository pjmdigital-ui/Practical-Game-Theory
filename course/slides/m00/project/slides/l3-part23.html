Internomics · Module 0 — Orientation

Part Two is about seeing what isn't obvious: uncertainty. Model six, Map Information and Beliefs: what do I actually know, and what am I assuming? Model seven, Separate Words From Credibility: what makes this message believable? Model eight, Commitment Changes the Game: what actually binds a promise, a threat, or a boundary? Part Three is about thinking beyond the moment. Model nine, the Shadow of the Future: what changes because there will be a next time? Model ten, Diagnose the Coordination Problem: are we really in conflict, or just failing to get on the same page? And model eleven, Change the Game: do I need a better move, or a better game?
