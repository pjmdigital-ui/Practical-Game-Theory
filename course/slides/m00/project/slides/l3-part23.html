Part Two is about seeing what isn't obvious. In one word it's about uncertainty. Model six is Map Information and Beliefs. What do I actually know and what am I assuming? Most of us treat our assumptions as facts without noticing. Model seven is Separate Words From Credibility. What makes this message believable? Model eight is Commitment Changes the Game. What actually binds a promise or a threat or a boundary? A promise can be persuasive in one situation and meaningless in another. Here's the common misunderstanding. People hear "credibility" and think it means being suspicious of everyone. It doesn't. Suspicion assumes hostile intent. Strategic thinking just tests possibilities. Now let's look at Part Three.
