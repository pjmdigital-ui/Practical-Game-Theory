Definition

A game is any situation in which the outcome depends, at least partly, on the choices of more than one person.

No winner or loser required. No math required.

Internomics · Module 0 — Orientation

When most people hear "game theory," they imagine chess, poker, or two countries threatening each other. Those are games. But game theory is much broader than that. A game, in the game-theoretic sense, is any situation where the outcome depends, at least partly, on the decisions of more than one person. That's it. It doesn't need a winner and a loser. And you won't need any math in this course.
