Practice · Your own game

What did you assume?

1. Pick one recurring interaction that frustrates you. Work, home or a customer.

2. Write down the last thing you said in it.

3. List everything you assumed before you said it. What they wanted. What they knew. How they'd respond.

4. Circle any assumption you have never actually tested.

Internomics · Module 0 — Orientation

Now let's bring this home. Step one. Think of one recurring interaction that frustrates you. Step two. Write down the last thing you said in it. Step three is the important one. List everything you assumed before you said those words. What did you assume they wanted? What did you assume they knew? How did you assume they'd respond? Step four. Circle any assumption you have never actually tested. Those circled assumptions are your model of the game. The common mistake is to treat this as a confession of everything you did wrong.
