Internomics · Module 0 — Orientation

Here's what that shift looks like in practice. Instead of "How do I convince them?" we ask "What do they actually value?" Instead of "Why does this problem keep happening?" we ask "What is making this pattern stable?" Every new question points away from you and toward the situation. That's the whole idea of this course in one table. Different questions lead to different choices. Which column do you live in most of the time? Be honest. Most of us live in the left one.
