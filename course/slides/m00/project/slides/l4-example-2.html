Internomics · Module 0 — Orientation

Let's walk through it one step at a time. Model. Your first read was status. Act. You offered public credit. Observe. They're still resistant. Now comes the step most people skip. Update. You ask what you misunderstood about the game. Maybe the real cost to them is workload. Or maybe it's risk. If your project fails, maybe they'll pay for it. Credit doesn't reduce anyone's workload or risk. It might even raise their risk. Then adapt. You stop offering credit. You start asking about capacity. You go around the loop again. The surprise didn't mean you failed. It improved your model. Is there someone whose response keeps surprising you? Here's the takeaway for this lesson.
