Internomics · Module 0 — Orientation

I don't want you walking into a conversation thinking step one is identify the game. The eleven models are not a procedure. They're a way of seeing. Think about an experienced driver. They just notice things. At first you'll consciously ask the questions. Eventually you'll notice the structure almost automatically. Not recitation. Recognition. That's what I mean by strategic judgment. Knowing which model matters. Knowing when it matters. Knowing how much weight to give it. And knowing when new evidence should change your mind. Here's the common mistake. Some people learn these models and then overanalyze everything. Use as much analysis as the stakes and the uncertainty justify.
