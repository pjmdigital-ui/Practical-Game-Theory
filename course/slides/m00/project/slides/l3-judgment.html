The real goal

You won't carry eleven checklists into a conversation.

Strategic judgment is knowing which model matters, when it matters, how much weight to give it, and when to change your mind.

Internomics · Module 0 — Orientation

Now, I don't want you walking into a conversation thinking, "Chapter seven, credibility, let me run the checklist." That's useful while you're learning. But the goal is for these models to fade into the background, so you simply notice things. "He has every incentive to tell me that." "We keep blaming each other, but neither of us can fix this alone." That's strategic judgment: knowing which model matters, when it matters, how much weight to give it, and when new evidence should change your mind.
