Internomics · Module 0 — Orientation

The second room was a lot harder. After high school I took a job on Wall Street. About two years later the FBI showed up at my door. They wanted me to cooperate in their investigation of the owners. I refused. At twenty years old I found myself in federal prison. I was one of the two youngest inmates on the unit. Some rules were written. Many were not. Who could I trust? When should I keep my mouth shut? Not one of those questions is about choosing the right words. Every one is about reading the game. Then I came home and faced a different problem.
