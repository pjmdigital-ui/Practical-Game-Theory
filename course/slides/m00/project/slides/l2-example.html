Example · Asking for a raise

Before you say a word

The situation

You walk into your boss's office to ask for a raise.

What we usually do

Think about the words. How much to ask for. How to show our value. Which objections to expect.

The lesson

All of that matters. But the outcome depends on two people's decisions. Not just yours.

Internomics · Module 0 — Orientation

Here's the example the book opens with. Imagine you walk into your boss's office to ask for a raise. Most people think about the words. But your request doesn't exist in isolation. Your boss might agree or reject it. They might make a counteroffer. They might even test how badly you want the raise. Your move changes their decision problem. And their response changes yours. That's the essence of strategic interaction. Calling this a game doesn't mean you're trying to beat your boss. The best outcomes usually work for both of you. So before you rehearse your opening line, there's a set of questions to ask first.
