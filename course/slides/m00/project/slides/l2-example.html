Internomics · Module 0 — Orientation

Here's the example the book opens with. Imagine you walk into your boss's office to ask for a raise. Most of us start thinking about the words. But look at the questions underneath. What does your boss actually want? What happens if they say no? What alternatives do you have, and what alternatives do they have? What does your boss know that you don't? Who else influences the decision? Suddenly this isn't simply a conversation. It's a game, not in the sense that you're trying to beat your boss, but in the sense that the outcome depends on both of your decisions.
