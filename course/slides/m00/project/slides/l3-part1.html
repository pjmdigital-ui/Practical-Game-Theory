Internomics · Module 0 — Orientation

The course follows the book's three parts. Part One is about seeing the structure beneath the conversation. There are five models here. Model one is See the Game. Its question is what game am I actually in? This is where we map the players and the options. Model two is See the Payoffs. Its question is what does each player actually value? That's rarely just money. It might be status or security or fairness. Model three is Think in Responses. Its question is if I make this move, then what? Your move changes their decision. Their response changes yours. Model four is Manage Predictability. Its question is what am I teaching other people to predict about me? Every time you respond to something, you teach people how you'll respond next time. Model five is See the Equilibrium. Its question is what pattern are our responses creating together? This is where we ask why a problem keeps happening even though nobody wants it. Now notice the progression at the bottom of the slide. Situation. Motivation. Response. Anticipation. Pattern. Each model builds on the one before it. First you see the situation. Then what drives each person. Then how they respond to each other. Then how they anticipate each other. Then the pattern all of that settles into. Here's a question for you as we go through these. Which one of these questions do you almost never ask? Put a little mark next to it in your workbook. That's probably where you'll get the most out of this course. Now on to Part Two.
