Internomics · Module 0 — Orientation

The course follows the book's three parts. Part One is about seeing the structure beneath the conversation. Model one, See the Game: what game am I actually in? Model two, See the Payoffs: what does each player actually value? Model three, Think in Responses: if I make this move, then what? Model four, Manage Predictability: what am I teaching other people to predict about me? And model five, See the Equilibrium: what pattern are our responses creating together? Notice the progression: situation, motivation, response, anticipation, pattern.
