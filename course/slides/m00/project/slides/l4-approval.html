Internomics · Module 0 — Orientation

One distinction before we go on. Game theory may help you understand why someone behaved badly. Understanding that doesn't mean excusing it. Someone can have an understandable incentive and still make a bad decision. Game theory doesn't remove moral responsibility. What it adds is another level of analysis. What conditions made this behavior attractive? You can condemn an action and still understand its logic. Understanding the logic often helps you prevent it from happening again. So when someone frustrates you, try a different question. Ask "What payoff would make that behavior make sense?" You're seeing the game from their side.
