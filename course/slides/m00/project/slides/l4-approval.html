An important distinction

Understanding why someone behaves a certain way is not the same as approving of it.

The better question: what conditions made this behavior attractive, sustainable, or hard to change?

Internomics · Module 0 — Orientation

One distinction before we go on. Game theory may help you understand why someone behaved badly. Maybe the incentives made it attractive. Maybe the information made mistrust rational. Understanding that does not mean excusing it. People can lie, betray, and exploit, and game theory doesn't remove moral responsibility. What it adds is another level of analysis: what conditions made this behavior attractive, sustainable, or difficult to change? You can condemn an action and still understand its logic. In fact, understanding the logic often helps you prevent it from happening again.
