Internomics · Module 0 — Orientation

Most communication training starts with words: what to say, how to phrase it, which questions to ask, how to handle the objection. Those things matter. But by the time you're choosing words, you've already made a series of quieter decisions. What's happening here? What does this person want? What do they know? How are they likely to respond? If those judgments are wrong, great communication just helps you execute the wrong strategy more effectively. This course teaches that layer underneath the words.
