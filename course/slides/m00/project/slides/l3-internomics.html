Internomics · Module 0 — Orientation

So what is Internomics? It's the study of what happens between people. It applies the principles of game theory to communication and human interaction. Its purpose is not to teach you what to say. It's to teach you to understand the game you're already playing. And you are already playing. There are no scripts in this course and no math. Instead you'll get eleven mental models. Each one gives you a better question to ask before you speak. A script only works in the situation it was written for. A good question works in situations nobody anticipated. And seeing the game doesn't mean winning it at someone else's expense. Let's look at the eleven models.
