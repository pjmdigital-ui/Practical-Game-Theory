Internomics · Module 0 — Orientation

So what is Internomics? It's the study of what happens between people. It applies the principles of game theory to communication and human interaction. Not to teach you what to say, but to teach you how to understand the game you're already playing. Because you are already playing. Every day, at work and at home, you're making moves inside games you may never have consciously identified. This course makes them visible.
