The classroom: learning to read a room

The situation

An only child on a busy block. Everyone else went home to brothers and sisters. I went home alone.

What happened

I wanted friends. So I tried to be funny. Sometimes it worked. Sometimes I took it much too far.

The lesson

The same joke could make one group laugh and annoy another. I had to feel the room before I spoke.

Internomics · Module 0 — Orientation

This came from three rooms in my life. The first room was childhood. I grew up as an only child on a dead-end block in the 1980s. But at the end of the day everybody else went home to brothers and sisters. I went home alone. I wanted friends. So I tried to be funny. But underneath all of that I was learning to read people. The exact same thing could make one group laugh and annoy another. I learned to feel what was happening in a room before deciding what to say. Where did you first learn to read a room? Then life gave me a much more serious test.
