Internomics · Module 0 — Orientation

Let me tell you where this came from for me. As an only child, I learned to read a room by trying to be funny. I learned the same joke could make one group laugh and annoy another. At twenty, I was in federal prison, surrounded by people whose lives were completely different from mine, and the stakes of reading people went way up. Who could I trust? When should I talk? When should I keep my mouth shut? Then in sales and copywriting, my income depended on understanding people. In every one of those rooms, what mattered most wasn't the words. It was the situation surrounding the words. I didn't have a name for it until I found game theory.
