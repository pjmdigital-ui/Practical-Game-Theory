Internomics · Module 0 — Orientation

A game doesn't have to be competitive. Families, friendships, teams and business partnerships are all games in this sense. Some games are cooperative: everyone gains by working together. Some are competitive. Most real-world games are mixed: we share some interests and not others. Thomas Schelling, who won the Nobel Prize for this work, emphasized exactly that point. And some are coordination games, where everyone wants to end up in the same place but can't be sure where the others are going.
