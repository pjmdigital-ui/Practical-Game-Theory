One more thing about how to hold these ideas. Human beings are far messier than the perfectly rational players in many mathematical models. So we'll use game theory as a lens. A lens doesn't tell you what will happen. It tells you where to look. Whenever real behavior departs from the clean model, I'll say so. The common mistake is to learn a model and then insist the other person must fit it. When they don't, people decide the other person is irrational. A better response is to say my model was incomplete. We'll come back to that in lesson four. Now let's put all of this to work on a very ordinary situation.
