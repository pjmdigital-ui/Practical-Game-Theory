Internomics · Module 0 — Orientation

One more thing about how to hold these ideas. Game theory was never created as a theory of better communication. It's the formal study of strategic interaction, and human beings are much messier than the perfectly rational players in many mathematical models. So we'll use it as a lens, a way of noticing things, not as a guarantee that anyone will behave a certain way. Throughout the course, whenever real behavior departs from the clean model, I'll say so.
