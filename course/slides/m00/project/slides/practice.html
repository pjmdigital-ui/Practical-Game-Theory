Internomics · Module 0 — Orientation

Let's practice. Dana manages a small marketing team. For three weeks she's asked a senior designer named Marcus to submit drafts two days before client deadlines. She has explained why. She has rephrased the request. She sent a firm email. Marcus keeps submitting the night before. A colleague tells Dana to work on her delivery. But look underneath. Marcus is paid a bonus based on client satisfaction scores. He isn't paid on internal deadlines. And the client contact loves Marcus. That client often requests last-minute changes directly from him.
