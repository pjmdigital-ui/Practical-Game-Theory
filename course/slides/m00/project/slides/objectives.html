Internomics · Module 0 — Orientation

Here's what you'll be able to do by the end of this module. First, you'll be able to explain in one sentence what we mean by a game. You'll also be able to explain why it has nothing to do with manipulation or competition. Second, you'll know the eleven mental models and the question each one asks. Third, you'll understand the learning loop that runs through the whole course. Model. Act. Observe. Update. Adapt. Fourth, you'll know the ethical standard every strategy in this course has to pass. There are four parts to it. Truth. Autonomy. Dignity. Informed choice. And fifth, you'll start your Interaction Journal. You'll add to it every single week. Let's begin with the first lesson.
