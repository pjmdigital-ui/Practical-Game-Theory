Internomics · Module 0 — Orientation

Here are the key terms from this module. You'll find all of them in your workbook. Internomics: the study of what happens between people. A game: any situation where outcomes depend on more than one person's choices. A player: anyone whose choices affect the outcome, including people who aren't in the room. Strategic interaction: when your best choice depends on what others do. A mental model: a lens that tells you what to look at, not a law that predicts exactly what people will do. And strategic judgment: knowing which model matters, when it matters, and when to change your mind.
