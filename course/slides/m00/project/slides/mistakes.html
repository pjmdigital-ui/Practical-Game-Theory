Common mistakes

Treating "game" as cynical

Games include cooperation, friendship and family life. Seeing the structure is about understanding.

Expecting scripts

You get better questions instead. They transfer to situations no script anticipates.

Overanalyzing everything

Use as much analysis as the stakes and uncertainty justify. No more.

Mistaking your model for truth

Every read of another person is a hypothesis to test. It's not a fact.

Internomics · Module 0 — Orientation

Here are the four mistakes I see most. The first is treating "game" as a cynical word. If you catch yourself using these ideas to get one over on people, go back to the four ethical tests. The second is expecting scripts. A script only works in the situation it was written for. Better questions transfer. The third is overanalyzing everything. A quick chat about lunch needs no analysis. A salary negotiation needs a lot. The fourth is mistaking your model for the truth. Remember the resistant colleague. The first read was status. The real issue was something else.
