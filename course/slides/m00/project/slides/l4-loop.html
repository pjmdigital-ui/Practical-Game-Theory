Internomics · Module 0 — Orientation

And here's the loop. Every read you make of another person is a hypothesis. So you model your best understanding of the game. Then you act. Then you observe what actually happens. Then you update. Where did reality differ from your prediction? Then you adapt your strategy or your model. And you go around again. Strategic thinking isn't perfect prediction. It's learning faster from what happens. Here's an example from the book. You predict that if you refuse a request, the other person will get angry. You refuse. They say "No problem." Good. You were wrong. Here's the common mistake. When people surprise us, we say "They're irrational." That label stops all learning. Let's walk through the loop with a real example.
