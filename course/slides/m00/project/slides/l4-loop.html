Internomics · Module 0 — Orientation

And here's the loop. Every read you make of another person is a hypothesis, not a fact. So you model: you build your best explanation of the game. You act: eventually you have to move. You observe what actually happens. You update your read. And you adapt your next move. That loop may matter more than any single model in this course. And it means surprise is a gift. When someone doesn't respond the way you predicted, they've just shown you that your model of the game was incomplete.
