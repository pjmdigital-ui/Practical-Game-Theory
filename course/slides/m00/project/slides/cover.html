Orientation

The game behind the conversation

Paul Mascetta

Welcome to Internomics. I'm Paul Mascetta, and over the next thirteen weeks I'm going to teach you a different way of looking at communication. Not a new set of scripts. Not seven phrases to use when someone disagrees with you. Something underneath all of that: the game behind the conversation. This first module is the orientation. By the end of it you'll know what we mean by a game, the eleven mental models we'll build together, the ethical standard we hold ourselves to, and the one loop that runs through everything in this course.
