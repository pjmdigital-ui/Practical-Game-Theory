Internomics · Module 0 — Orientation

Almost all communication training focuses on strategies and tactics: words, questions, objection handling, rapport. But underneath every tactic is a set of judgments about the situation. Who is involved, including people who aren't in the room? What does each person actually want? What choices do they really have? What do they know, and what do they believe? What are they expecting you to do? And if you make this move, how are they likely to respond? Get those wrong, and the best tactic in the world won't save you.
