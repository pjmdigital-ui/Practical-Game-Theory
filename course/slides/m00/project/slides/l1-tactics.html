Internomics · Module 0 — Orientation

Underneath every tactic is a set of judgments about the situation. Who is involved? What does each person actually value? What can they really do? What do they know? And if you make this move, how will they respond? A tactic is a tool for carrying out a plan. The plan comes from your read of the situation. If you think your boss is worried about the budget, you'll pick one approach. If your boss is really worried about losing you, that approach misses completely. When a conversation fails, most of us blame the delivery. Very often the delivery was fine and the read was off. Which brings us to a puzzle you've probably lived.
