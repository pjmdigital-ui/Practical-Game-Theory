Internomics · Module 0 — Orientation

Part Three is about thinking beyond the moment. Model nine is the Shadow of the Future. What changes because there will be a next time? Short-term wins can become long-term losses. Model ten is Diagnose the Coordination Problem. Are we in conflict or are we failing to coordinate? Two people can look like they're fighting when they just don't know what the other will do. Model eleven is Change the Game. Do I need a better move or a better game? Sometimes the incentives reward the opposite of what everyone wants. Think of a problem that keeps coming back at work or at home. Is it time to change the game? Now there's a big risk with eleven models.
