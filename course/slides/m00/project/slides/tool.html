Internomics · Module 0 — Orientation

Your tool for this module is the Interaction Journal. It's in your workbook, and you'll use it every week. One page per interaction. Write down the date and setting, who was involved, including people who weren't in the room, what you wanted, and what you think they wanted. Then what you said or did, and how they responded. Did their response surprise you, and what might that tell you? And finally: if you could replay it, what question would you ask yourself before speaking? Each module will add new questions to this journal.
