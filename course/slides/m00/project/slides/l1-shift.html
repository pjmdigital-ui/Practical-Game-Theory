Internomics · Module 0 — Orientation

So here's the shift this whole course is built on. Stop starting with "What should I say?" Start with "What game am I actually in?" Think about walking into your boss's office to ask for a raise. What does your boss actually want? What alternatives does each of you have? I'm not saying the words don't matter. They just come second. Once you see the game you know what your words need to do. Here's the misunderstanding to avoid. This isn't about overthinking every small exchange. It's about the conversations that matter. The book gives us a few more questions that work the same way.
