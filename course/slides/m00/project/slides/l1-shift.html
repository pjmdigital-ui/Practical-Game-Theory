The shift

From

"What should I say?"

To

"What game am I actually in?"

Internomics · Module 0 — Orientation

So the shift this whole course is built on is simple to say and hard to practice. Stop starting with "What should I say?" Start with "What game am I actually in?" The words still matter. They just come second. Once you can see the game, choosing the words gets much easier, because you know what they need to accomplish.
