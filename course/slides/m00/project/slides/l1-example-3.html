Internomics · Module 0 — Orientation

The third room was sales. I was twenty with a federal conviction and no college degree. The jobs I could get were mostly sales jobs. I had to recognize resistance. I had to know when to push and when to stop. What I said was only part of the equation. What really mattered was how the other person responded to it. Then in 2004 a friend needed a copywriter. I simply wrote the way I thought people needed to be spoken to. Over roughly two years I wrote more than a hundred sales letters. In all three rooms what mattered most wasn't the words. I just didn't have a name for it yet.
