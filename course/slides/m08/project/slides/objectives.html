Internomics · Module 8 — Commitment Changes the Game

By the end of this module you'll be able to do five things. Tell an intention from a promise and a strategic commitment. Test any threat or promise or deadline. Ask what actually binds someone who says "my hands are tied." Predict how a commitment changes the other player's best response. And tell commitment apart from signaling and stubbornness and sunk cost. Let's start with Lesson One.
