Internomics · Module 8 — Commitment Changes the Game

Here's what you'll be able to do by the end of this module. First you'll be able to tell an intention from a promise and from a strategic commitment. You'll also see why only the third one changes the future game. Second you'll be able to test any threat or promise or deadline. The test is simple. Will carrying it out still make sense when the time comes? Third you'll know what to ask when someone says "my hands are tied." You'll separate real constraints from words. Fourth you'll be able to predict how a commitment changes the other player's best response. That includes the risk of an impasse when both sides commit. And fifth you'll be able to tell strategic commitment apart from signaling. You'll also separate it from stubbornness and from the sunk-cost fallacy. All of it stays inside our standard of truth and autonomy and dignity and informed choice. Let's start with Lesson One.
