Internomics · Module 8 — Commitment Changes the Game

Here's what you'll be able to do by the end of this module. You'll be able to distinguish an intention, a promise, and a strategic commitment, and explain why only the third one changes the future game. You'll be able to evaluate a threat, a promise, a boundary, a deadline, or a "final" position by asking whether carrying it out will still make sense when the time comes. You'll know how to ask what actually binds a person who says "my hands are tied." You'll be able to predict how a commitment changes the other player's best response, including the risk of an impasse when both sides commit. And you'll be able to tell strategic commitment apart from signaling, from stubbornness, and from the sunk-cost fallacy, while holding to our standard of truth, autonomy, dignity, and informed choice.
