Internomics · Module 8 — Commitment Changes the Game

People know "I can't" is more powerful than "I don't want to." So they sometimes pretend to be constrained. "I'd love to help you, but corporate won't let me." Maybe that's true. Or maybe corporate is ten feet away and would approve it instantly. So ask what actually binds them. A contract? A rule? An irreversible investment? Or merely words? If something real binds them pushing harder accomplishes nothing. The common mistake is to argue or give up. Ask respectfully what the constraint is. Real constraints usually have answers. Fake ones get vague. Next let's see a constraint that helps both sides.
