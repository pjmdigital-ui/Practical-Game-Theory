Internomics · Module 8 — Commitment Changes the Game

Now the ethics. Imagine someone deliberately creates a crisis and says "I've made it impossible for either of us to turn back, so now you have to do what I want." That can become coercive. It strips away your meaningful ability to choose. Our standard stays the same. Truth. Autonomy. Dignity. Informed choice. A contract or a boundary or a transparent deadline can help people coordinate and trust one another. Strategy asks whether a commitment will work. Ethics asks whether it respects the other person. A tactic can be effective and still wrong. Next remember that your commitment is itself a move.
