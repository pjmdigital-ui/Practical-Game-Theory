Commitment Changes the Game

What makes this commitment real?

Paul Mascetta

Welcome to Module Eight. This one is called Commitment Changes the Game. In the last module we asked what makes a message credible. Sometimes the answer was evidence. Sometimes it was reputation or a signal that's hard to fake. This module adds another answer. You can change the game so that doing something different later becomes costly or even impossible. That leads to one of the most counterintuitive ideas in all of game theory. Sometimes having fewer options can make you stronger. By the end of this module you'll be able to look at any threat or promise or "final" offer and ask the one question that matters. What makes this commitment real?
