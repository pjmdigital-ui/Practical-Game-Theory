The question to carry

What makes this commitment real?

Internomics · Next: Module 9 — The Shadow of the Future

Before you make a threat, a promise, a deadline, a boundary, or a "final" offer this week, ask: what makes this commitment real? Then ask: what option am I giving up? How does giving it up change their decision? What flexibility will I wish I still had if circumstances change? And what happens if they refuse to move? Sometimes strength comes from having more options, and sometimes it comes from credibly removing one. But there's another force that changes today's behavior without removing any options at all: simply knowing there will be a next time. That's Module Nine, the Shadow of the Future. I'll see you there.
