The question to carry

What makes this commitment real?

Internomics · Next: Module 9 — The Shadow of the Future

Before you make a threat or a promise or a "final" offer this week, ask this. What makes this commitment real? Then ask what option you're giving up. Ask how giving it up changes their decision. Ask what flexibility you'll wish you still had if things change. And ask what happens if they refuse to move. Sometimes strength comes from having more options. Sometimes it comes from credibly removing one. But there's another force that changes today's behavior without removing any options at all. It's simply knowing there will be a next time.
