Internomics · Module 8 — Commitment Changes the Game

Remember Module Three? Think in responses. A commitment is itself a move. And the other player gets to respond. Their response is the entire game. Maybe they concede. Maybe they walk away. Maybe they make their own commitment and you're in the hundred-versus-ninety standoff. Maybe they find an alternative or retaliate or stop negotiating. Only one of those six is what you wanted.
