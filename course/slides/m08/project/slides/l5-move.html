Internomics · Module 8 — Commitment Changes the Game

Remember Module Three: think in responses. A commitment is itself a move, and the other player gets to respond to it. Maybe they concede, which is what you were hoping for. Maybe they walk away. Maybe they make their own commitment, and now you're in the no-deal situation we just saw. Maybe they search for an alternative. Maybe they retaliate. Maybe they simply stop negotiating. The whole point of a commitment is that it changes their decision problem. So if you don't think about their response, you're missing half the game. Before you lock anything in, ask: how will they respond to the fact that I'm limiting my options? And what happens if they refuse to move?
