Three ways to get up at five

The situation

What happened

Internomics · Module 8 — Commitment Changes the Game

Here's the example from the book. "I'm definitely waking up at five" is an intention. "I promise I'll meet you at the gym at five-thirty" is a promise. Now the third version. My training partner picks me up at five-fifteen. And I've prepaid a session I lose if I don't show up. At five in the morning getting out of bed has a different payoff. I didn't just become more motivated. I changed the game my future self will face. Most of us treat this as a willpower problem. But changing the payoffs is what moves behavior. That brings us to the takeaway.
