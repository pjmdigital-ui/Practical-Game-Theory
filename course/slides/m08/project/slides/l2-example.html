Internomics · Module 8 — Commitment Changes the Game

Here's the example from the book. "I'm definitely waking up at five tomorrow." That's intention. "I promise I'll meet you at the gym at five-thirty." That's a promise. But if I arrange for my training partner to pick me up at five-fifteen, and I've already paid for a session I lose if I don't show, I've altered the future decision. Getting out of bed now has a different payoff. I didn't just become more motivated. I changed the game my future self will face. Because sometimes the other player is you. Tonight you say, "I'm getting up early." Tomorrow morning you say, "Five more minutes." So tonight you change tomorrow's game: the alarm across the room, the prepaid session, the tempting food removed from the house. Those are commitment devices.
