Internomics · Module 8 — Commitment Changes the Game

Let's practice. Dana runs a small design studio and is renewing a contract with her largest client. The procurement lead, Marcus, asks for a fifteen percent cut. Dana says, "Our rates are final, we can't go lower," then agrees to five percent off when he pushes. Now he wants another five, and Dana says this time she means it. Marcus mentions that his authority caps the budget at a set number, and anything above it goes to his CFO. At home, Dana has told her teenage son three times this month that if he misses curfew again, he loses the car for the rest of the year, and each time he kept the keys. And she's drafting a public post promising her studio will never charge clients for revisions, though her biggest project next quarter has a scope that's hard to predict.
