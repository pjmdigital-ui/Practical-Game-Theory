Internomics · Module 8 — Commitment Changes the Game

Let's practice. Meet Dana. She runs a small design studio. Her largest client's procurement lead Marcus asks for a fifteen percent cut. Dana says "Our rates are final." Then she agrees to five percent off. Marcus says his authority caps the budget. At home Dana has told her son three times that missing curfew costs him the car "for the rest of the year." Each time he kept the keys. And she's drafting a public post promising never to charge clients for revisions. For each one ask what makes this commitment real. Then go to the questions.
