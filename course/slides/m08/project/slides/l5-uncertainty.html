Internomics · Module 8 — Commitment Changes the Game

Here's another cost that's easy to miss. The less you know about the future the more valuable flexibility can become. Payoffs may change. New information may emerge. Even your own preferences may change. Committing strongly today could mean locking in an answer before you know the question. So ask how much uncertainty you're locking yourself into. If the future is reasonably predictable commitment may be valuable. If it's extremely uncertain flexibility may be worth more. People often commit hardest when they feel most anxious. But that's often exactly when flexibility is worth the most. Next let's look at going public.
