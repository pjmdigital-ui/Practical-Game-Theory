Internomics · Module 8 — Commitment Changes the Game

The classic image is an army burning the bridge behind it. With the bridge standing the army can retreat if battle gets costly. The other side knows that and plans around it. Burning the bridge seems insane. But retreat is no longer part of the future game. The army's determination becomes more credible precisely because it reduced its own freedom. Now please hear me on this. The lesson is not "burn every bridge." That would be terrible advice. In most of life you want your bridges. Flexibility has value. Flexibility can also undermine credibility. Let's see this play out in our negotiation.
