Example · The commitment trap

"We will never raise our prices."

The situation

A business owner makes this public announcement. It sounds admirable. Customers love it.

What happened

Years later costs double. Now the company has a problem.

The lesson

The commitment created credibility. It also destroyed flexibility.

A good commitment asks: what happens if the world changes?

Internomics · Module 8 — Commitment Changes the Game

Here's the first version of the commitment trap. A business owner publicly announces "We will never raise our prices." Customers love it. It's a clear and credible promise. Then years later costs double. Every option left is painful. Keep the promise and absorb costs that may sink the business. Or break a public promise and pay the reputational price. The commitment created credibility. It also destroyed flexibility. And nobody asked the key question at the start. What happens if the world changes? The problem wasn't making a promise.
