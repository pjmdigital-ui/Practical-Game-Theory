Internomics · Module 8 — Commitment Changes the Game

Here are two versions of the commitment trap. A business owner publicly announces, "We will never raise our prices." It sounds admirable. Customers love it. Then, years later, costs double. The original commitment created credibility, but it also destroyed flexibility. Or a manager declares, "Anyone who makes this mistake is automatically fired." Then the best employee in the company makes an honest mistake under unusual circumstances. Now the manager has to choose between breaking the commitment and carrying out a punishment that no longer makes sense. Either way, it costs something. That's why a good commitment always asks one more question before it's made: what happens if the world changes?
