Internomics · Module 8 — Commitment Changes the Game

Sometimes the other player is your own future self. Tonight you say "I'm getting up early." Tomorrow morning you say "Five more minutes." So tonight smart people change tomorrow's game. They put the alarm across the room. They prepay the session. They remove the tempting food from the house. Those are commitment devices. The book mentions a field experiment with university students. A nonbinding "soft commitment" to stay on track increased exam registration and participation and passing. Where do your evening self and your morning self keep disagreeing?
