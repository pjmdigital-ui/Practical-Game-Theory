Internomics · Module 8 — Commitment Changes the Game

Here's the big idea of this module. Sometimes having fewer options can make you stronger. That sounds wrong at first. But when another person decides based on what they expect you to do later, your freedom to change your mind can become a weakness. If I say a hundred thousand is my absolute minimum and you know I could still take ninety, you'll just wait me out. But a credible commitment can also lock you into a terrible outcome. So the goal is not "commit harder." Here's what you'll be able to do.
