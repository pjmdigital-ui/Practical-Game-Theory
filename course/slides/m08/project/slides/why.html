Internomics · Module 8 — Commitment Changes the Game

That sounds wrong at first. More options should be better. More flexibility should give you more power. And usually, it does. But when another person is deciding based partly on what they expect you to do later, your freedom to change your mind can become a weakness. If I tell you a hundred thousand is my absolute minimum, and you know I could still take ninety, my words don't mean much. Deliberately and visibly limiting that freedom can change what you rationally choose to do now. That's commitment: changing the game so that doing something different later becomes difficult, costly, or impossible. But I want to be honest with you from the start. A credible commitment can also lock you into a terrible outcome. So the goal of this module is not "commit harder." It's to understand what a commitment does to the game.
