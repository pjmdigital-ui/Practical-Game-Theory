Internomics · Module 8 — Commitment Changes the Game

Here's the big idea of this module. Sometimes having fewer options can make you stronger. I know that sounds wrong at first. More options should be better. More flexibility should give you more power. And usually it does. But something changes when another person is deciding based partly on what they expect you to do later. In that situation your freedom to change your mind can become a weakness. Think about a negotiation. If I tell you a hundred thousand is my absolute minimum and you know I could still take ninety, my words don't mean much. You'll just wait me out. Now imagine I deliberately and visibly limit my own freedom. That can change what you rationally choose to do right now. That's what commitment is. You change the game so that doing something different later becomes difficult or costly or impossible. But I want to be honest with you from the start. A credible commitment can also lock you into a terrible outcome. So the goal of this module is not "commit harder." The goal is to understand what a commitment does to the game. And when someone tells you "I have no choice" you'll have a sharper question to ask. What actually binds them? Before we go on, think of the last time someone told you a number was final. Did you believe them? Let's look at what you'll be able to do by the end of this module.
