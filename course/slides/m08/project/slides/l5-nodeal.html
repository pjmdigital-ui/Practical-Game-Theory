Internomics · Module 8 — Commitment Changes the Game

The strongest commitment isn't always the best one. I commit "I will accept nothing under a hundred dollars." You commit "I will pay nothing above ninety." We've created no deal. Neither of us is bluffing. The problem is that the commitments are incompatible. And because we locked ourselves in we can't go looking for a number that works. So never evaluate a commitment solely by how strongly it binds you. Evaluate it by the game it creates. People assume whoever commits first and hardest wins. But if the other side commits too you both lose the deal. Next comes another hidden cost. Uncertainty.
