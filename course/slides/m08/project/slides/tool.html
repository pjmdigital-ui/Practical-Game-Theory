Internomics · Module 8 — Commitment Changes the Game

Your tool this week is the Commitment Test. It's in your workbook. Use it before you make a threat or promise or deadline. Write the commitment down and classify it. Then work through the parts. What actually binds it and can they see it? What option are you giving up? Will carrying it out still make sense? What will you do when a boundary is crossed? How will they respond? What flexibility will you wish you still had? Does it respect their informed choice? Then decide. Commit as planned. Commit to something smaller. Or stay flexible. The value is in the questions.
