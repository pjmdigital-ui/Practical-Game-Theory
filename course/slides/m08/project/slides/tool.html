Internomics · Module 8 — Commitment Changes the Game

Your tool this week is the Commitment Test. Use it before you make a threat, promise, deadline, boundary, or "final" position, and to evaluate one someone makes to you. Start by writing the commitment down and classifying it: intention, promise, or strategic commitment. Part one: what actually binds it, and can the other player observe or verify it? Part two: what future option am I giving up, precisely, and what strategic benefit does that create? Part three, for threats and promises: when the moment arrives, will carrying it out still make sense, and is there a smaller consequence I'd enforce every time? Part four, boundaries: what will I actually do, calmly and consistently, when it's crossed? Part five: how will their best response change, and could this produce an impasse? Part six: what happens if new information appears, and is this strategy, or stubbornness and sunk cost? Part seven: does it preserve truth, autonomy, dignity, and informed choice? Then decide: commit as planned, commit to something smaller or more enforceable, or stay flexible, and write down why.
