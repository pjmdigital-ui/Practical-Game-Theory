Internomics · Module 8 — Commitment Changes the Game

A threat and a promise feel like opposites. But they share the same strategic problem. Will the other player believe you'll follow through later? Here's the example from the book. A company tells an employee "If you hit this target, we'll promote you." The employee hits it. Then management finds the promotion inconvenient. If management has a history of changing its mind, future employees stop responding to those promises. The fix is a commitment mechanism. Write the bonus into a contract.
