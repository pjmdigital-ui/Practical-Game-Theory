Example · Another 10 percent off

Same request, three answers

The situation

You negotiate for your company. The other side asks you to approve another 10 percent discount.

What happened

"I'd rather not" invites a push. A true "Beyond this number it needs the board" changes their decision.

The lesson

The board isn't magical. Your options really are different. "Corporate won't let me" may not be.

Same words. Very different games. Know which one you're in.

Internomics · Module 8 — Commitment Changes the Game

Here's one negotiation. The other side asks you for another ten percent discount. Answer one is "I'd rather not." That invites a push. Answer two has to be true to work. "My authority stops at this number. Anything beyond it requires approval from the board." That changes their decision problem. Your available actions really are different. Answer three is "I'd love to help you, but corporate won't let me." Same kind of words. Very different games. On your side only claim a constraint when it's real. On their side ask what actually binds them. That leads to the takeaway.
