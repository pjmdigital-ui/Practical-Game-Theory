Internomics · Module 8 — Commitment Changes the Game

Suppose you're negotiating on behalf of a company, and the other side asks for another ten percent discount. Answer one: "I'd rather not." That invites a push, because they know your preference can change. Answer two, and it has to be true: "My authority stops at this number. Anything beyond it requires approval from the board." That changes their decision problem. Not because the board is magical, but because your available actions really are different. Now answer three, the one you've probably heard yourself: "I'd love to help you, but corporate won't let me." Maybe that's completely true. Or maybe corporate is sitting ten feet away and would approve it instantly. Same words, very different games. Your job is to know which one you're in.
