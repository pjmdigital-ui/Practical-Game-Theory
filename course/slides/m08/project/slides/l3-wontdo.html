Internomics · Module 8 — Commitment Changes the Game

This is one of the simplest lessons in the module. Don't threaten what you won't do. People make threats in moments of emotion. "I'm quitting." Then they don't follow through. Each time the other person updates. The next threat carries less information. You're teaching them that your stated consequence doesn't predict your behavior. So they'll discount everything you say. Promises work the same way. A manager who keeps promising rewards and doesn't deliver may blame motivation. The real problem may be credibility destroyed by failed commitments. Who has learned to tune out your threats or promises? Let's see this at home.
