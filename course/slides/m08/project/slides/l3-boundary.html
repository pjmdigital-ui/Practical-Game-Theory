Example · Boundaries are commitments

"I'm ending the conversation"

The situation

Someone keeps speaking disrespectfully to you. You say: "If this continues, I'm ending the conversation."

What happened

It happens again. You stay. It happens again. You stay. They learn it's cheap talk.

The lesson

Calmly say "We'll continue this when we can talk respectfully." Then leave. Every time.

A boundary is "If this occurs, here is what I will do."

Internomics · Module 8 — Commitment Changes the Game

Boundaries are commitments. Someone repeatedly speaks disrespectfully to you. You say "If this continues, I'm ending the conversation." It happens again. You stay. They've learned your statement doesn't constrain your behavior. It's cheap talk. You trained them. Now imagine you calmly say "We'll continue this when we can talk respectfully." And you leave. Do that consistently and their behavior has a predictable cost. People think a boundary is a demand. But you can't control what another person does. A boundary is about your action. And it matters only when your behavior makes it credible. Let's sum up this lesson.
