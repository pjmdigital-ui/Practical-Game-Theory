Internomics · Module 8 — Commitment Changes the Game

Your assignment this week: audit your commitments. Each day, notice one threat, promise, deadline, boundary, or "final" position, yours or someone else's. Write down the exact words, whether it was an intention, a promise, or a strategic commitment, what actually bound it, or "words only," and whether it was followed through. Then pick one boundary or consequence you've stated but haven't enforced. Replace it with a smaller, clear consequence you will actually carry out. State it once, calmly, and follow through the next time the condition happens. Record what happens. Next week, come ready to answer: how many of the "final" positions you heard were backed by something real? When you enforced something smaller, did their expectations change? And where did a commitment turn into a trap because circumstances changed?
