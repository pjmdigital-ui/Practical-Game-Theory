Internomics · Module 8 — Commitment Changes the Game

Your assignment this week is to audit your commitments. Each day notice one threat or promise or deadline or "final" position. It can be yours or someone else's. Write down the exact words. Write whether it was an intention or a promise or a strategic commitment. Write what actually bound it. If nothing did write "words only." And note whether it was followed through. Then do one more thing. Pick one boundary or consequence you've stated but haven't enforced. Replace it with a smaller and clear consequence you'll actually carry out. State it once. Say it calmly. Then follow through the next time the condition happens. Record what happens. Next week come ready to answer three questions. How many of the "final" positions you heard were backed by something real? How could you tell? When you enforced something smaller did their expectations change? And where did a commitment turn into a trap because circumstances changed?
