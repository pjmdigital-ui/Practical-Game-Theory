Internomics · Module 8 — Commitment Changes the Game

Your assignment this week is to audit your commitments. Each day notice one threat or promise or "final" position. It can be yours or someone else's. Write down the exact words. Note whether it was an intention or a promise or a commitment. Write what bound it or "words only." Note whether it was followed through. Then pick one consequence you've stated but haven't enforced. Replace it with a smaller one you'll actually carry out. State it once and calmly. Then follow through.
