Internomics · Module 8 — Commitment Changes the Game

A real commitment isn't always a weapon. Sometimes it helps both sides by making the future clearer. Suppose someone keeps saying "Maybe I'll accept. Maybe I won't." You don't know if waiting helps. So you hedge and stall and test. Now suppose they credibly commit. "This offer expires Friday at noon, and after that the asset goes to another buyer." If that's true uncertainty drops. But a fake deadline does the opposite. If Friday passes and the offer is still there, the next deadline means less. When you set a deadline will you honor it?
