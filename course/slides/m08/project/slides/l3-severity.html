A threat is only as good as the future decision

Severity is not credibility.

The question isn't "How severe is the threat?" It's "Will carrying it out still make sense when the time comes?"

Internomics · Module 8 — Commitment Changes the Game

Here's where most of us get threats wrong. We think the bigger the consequence, the more seriously the other person will take it. But think about it from their side. Suppose someone says, "If you reject my offer, I'll walk." And when the time comes, walking away would be extremely costly for them. The other player can reasonably think, "You say you'll leave, but when you actually have to make that decision, you probably won't." The flexibility undermines the threat. So the important question is never how severe the threat is. It's whether carrying it out will still make sense when the moment arrives. If it won't, a big threat is just a big statement.
