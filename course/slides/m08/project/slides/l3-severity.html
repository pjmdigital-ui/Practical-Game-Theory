A threat is only as good as the future decision

Severity is not credibility.

The wrong question

"How severe is the threat?"

The right question

"Will carrying it out still make sense when the time comes?"

If following through will be too costly for you, the other side can predict that you'll back down.

Internomics · Module 8 — Commitment Changes the Game

Here's where most of us get threats wrong. We think the bigger the consequence the more seriously they'll take it. But severity is not credibility. The other person is asking what you'll actually do when the moment arrives. A huge threat can be less believable. The bigger the consequence the more it often costs you to carry it out. Will carrying it out still make sense when the time comes? Here's a quick test. Picture the moment you'd have to follow through. If you flinch they'll probably see that coming.
