Internomics · Module 8 — Commitment Changes the Game

Here's the short answer. A commitment changes what the other player expects you to do later. And what they expect tomorrow shapes what they do today. Player A tells Player B "If you reject my offer, I'll walk." But walking away would be extremely costly for A. So B thinks "When the moment comes you probably won't." A's flexibility undermines A's own threat. Now suppose A truly can't keep negotiating after a deadline. B's decision changes. The future game is different.
