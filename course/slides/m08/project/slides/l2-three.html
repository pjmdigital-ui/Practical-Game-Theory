Internomics · Module 8 — Commitment Changes the Game

We say things like "I'm committed to getting in shape," or "I'm committed to making this relationship work." Those statements can be completely sincere. But that's not the game-theoretic meaning we're after. So let's separate three things. An intention: I plan to do something. A promise: I tell you that I plan to do something. That's communication, and it may or may not be backed by anything. And a strategic commitment: I take an action that changes the incentives or options surrounding what I can do later, so that doing something else becomes difficult, costly, or impossible. The first two can be heartfelt. Only the third actually changes the future game, and that's why it can change how other people behave before the future even arrives.
