Three things we call "commitment"

Intention

"I plan to do something."

"I'm going to do it." Sincere. Changes nothing about the game.

Promise

"I tell you that I plan to do something."

"I promise I'll do it." Communication. May or may not be backed by anything.

Strategic commitment

I take an action that changes the incentives or options around what I can do later.

"I've arranged things so that doing anything else would be extremely costly."

Only the third changes the future game.

Internomics · Module 8 — Commitment Changes the Game

Only one of these three things actually changes the game. An intention is "I'm going to do it." It may be heartfelt but nothing about the game has changed. A promise is "I promise I'll do it." That's communication. It may or may not be backed by anything. A strategic commitment is an action that makes doing anything else difficult or costly or impossible. That can affect behavior before the future arrives. The common mistake is thinking a louder promise becomes a commitment. Volume doesn't change the payoffs. Only an action does.
