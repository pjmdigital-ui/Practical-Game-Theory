Internomics · Module 8 — Commitment Changes the Game

Four more. Delegation means handing authority to someone whose choices are more restricted. It turns "I don't want to" into a truthful "I can't." A boundary is "If this occurs, here is what I will do." It's a commitment. And it matters only when your behavior makes it credible. A commitment device is an arrangement that limits your future self's options. Think of the prepaid session or the alarm across the room. And the sunk-cost fallacy means letting yesterday's irreversible cost distort today's decision when that cost should no longer matter to the choice. Keep that last one close. It's the thing people most often mistake for commitment.
