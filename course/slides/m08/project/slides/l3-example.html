Example · At home

Six months grounded

The situation

A parent says: "If you don't clean your room, you're grounded for six months."

What happened

The child knows it's never happening. Six months of punishment would be miserable for the parent too.

The lesson

"If the room isn't cleaned by six, there's no gaming tonight." Enforced every time.

Less dramatic. More strategic effect.

Internomics · Module 8 — Commitment Changes the Game

Let's bring this home. Literally. A parent tells a child "If you don't clean your room, you're grounded for six months." The child knows it's never happening. Six months of punishment would be miserable for the parent too. So the child expects the parent to back down. Now compare "If the room isn't cleaned by six, there's no gaming tonight." And the parent enforces it consistently. It's much less dramatic. But the child believes it will happen. The common misunderstanding is that small consequences feel weak. So parents escalate. But escalation without follow-through teaches the child to wait it out. Now the same logic in a boundary.
