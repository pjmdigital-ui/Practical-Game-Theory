Internomics · Module 8 — Commitment Changes the Game

Here are the key terms from this module. You'll find them all in your workbook. An intention is "I plan to do something." It's a private plan that changes nothing about the game. A promise is "I tell you that I plan to do something." That's communication. It may or may not be backed by anything. A strategic commitment is an action that changes the incentives or options around what you can do later. Doing something else becomes difficult or costly or impossible. A credible threat or promise sounds like "If you do X, I will do Y." Or "I will reward you with Y." What makes it credible is that following through will still make sense when the moment arrives. Or it will be hard to avoid. And an observable commitment is one the other player knows about or can credibly infer. Only those can change their present decision. Four more terms on the next slide.
