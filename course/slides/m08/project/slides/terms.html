Internomics · Module 8 — Commitment Changes the Game

Here are the key terms from this module, and you'll find them all in your workbook. An intention: "I plan to do something," a private plan that changes nothing about the game. A promise: "I tell you that I plan to do something," which may or may not be backed by anything. A strategic commitment: an action that changes the incentives or options around what you can do later, so that doing something else becomes difficult, costly, or impossible. A credible threat or promise: "If you do X, I will do Y," or "reward you with Y," where following through will still make sense, or be hard to avoid, when the moment arrives. And an observable commitment: one the other player knows about or can credibly infer. Only those can change their present decision.
