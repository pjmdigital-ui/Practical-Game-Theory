Why it matters: delegation can create commitment. Handing authority to someone with fewer choices changes what's possible. It has to be true.

Internomics · Module 8 — Commitment Changes the Game

There's a major strategic difference between "I don't want to" and "I can't." "I'd rather not" invites pressure. The other side knows your preference can change. But suppose you can truthfully say "My authority stops at this number." It's not your mood that changed. It's your available actions. There's no point pushing on a door that's locked. That's why delegation can create commitment. The owner could say yes to anything. A negotiator with a fixed range can't. The key word is truthfully. If you invent a constraint you're lying and building a credibility problem. Next let's tell a real constraint from a fake one.
